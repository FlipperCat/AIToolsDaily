QuillBot has become a favorite among students and writers who need to paraphrase content while maintaining meaning. Its multiple rewriting modes make it versatile for different needs.

## Paraphrasing Modes

QuillBot offers seven distinct modes for different purposes:
- **Standard**: Balanced rewriting
- **Fluency**: Natural flow focus
- **Formal**: Professional tone
- **Simple**: Plain language
- **Creative**: More variations
- **Expand**: Add detail
- **Shorten**: Condense text

## Additional Tools

- **Summarizer**: Condense long text
- **Grammar Checker**: Fix errors
- **Citation Generator**: Format references
- **Plagiarism Checker**: Verify originality

QuillBot is particularly popular in academic settings where students need to paraphrase sources while maintaining originality. The citation generator adds extra value for research papers.
