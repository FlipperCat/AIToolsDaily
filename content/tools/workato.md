Workato is a leader in enterprise integration platforms (iPaaS). With over 1,000 connectors and AI-powered recipe building, it handles the most complex enterprise automation needs.

## Enterprise Integration Leader

Workato excels at connecting enterprise systems:
- **CRM**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics
- **ERP**: SAP, NetSuite, Oracle
- **HRIS**: Workday, BambooHR
- **Finance**: Coupa, Bill.com

## Recipe IQ AI

Workato's AI capabilities:
- Suggest recipes based on connected apps
- Auto-map fields between systems
- Recommend error handling
- Optimize performance

## Workbot Integration

Unique conversational automation:
- Commands from Slack or Teams
- Approval workflows in chat
- Notifications and alerts
- Self-service for employees

## Pricing Model

Workato uses task-based pricing for enterprises. Typical deployments cost $25,000-100,000+ annually depending on:
- Number of recipes
- Task volume
- Connector requirements
- Support level

This is a serious enterprise tool with pricing to match—best suited for organizations with dedicated integration needs.
