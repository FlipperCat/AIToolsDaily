Mem takes a radically different approach to notes: let AI handle the organization. Just capture your thoughts, and Mem's AI surfaces them when they're relevant.

## Self-Organizing Intelligence

Traditional note apps require folders and tags. Mem replaces that with:
- Automatic categorization
- AI-detected relationships
- Context-aware surfacing
- Smart search that understands meaning

## Key Features

- **Mem Chat**: Ask questions about your notes
- **Related Mems**: See connected information
- **Smart Write**: AI writing with your context
- **Collections**: AI-generated groupings
- **Timeline**: Chronological capture

## Who Mem Is For

Mem is perfect for people who:
- Hate organizing notes
- Want to capture quickly
- Trust AI to find things later
- Work solo or small teams

If you prefer structured databases and complex hierarchies, Notion is better. For capture-first, think-later workflows, Mem excels.
