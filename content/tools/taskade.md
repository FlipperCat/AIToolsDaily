Taskade combines project management, note-taking, and AI assistance in one affordable package. Its AI agents can automate repetitive tasks and help generate content.

## AI Agents

Taskade's unique feature is customizable AI agents:
- Create task lists from descriptions
- Generate project plans
- Summarize notes
- Research topics

## Multiple Workspace Views

View your work in any format:
- **List**: Traditional task lists
- **Board**: Kanban workflow
- **Mind Map**: Visual brainstorming
- **Org Chart**: Hierarchy view
- **Calendar**: Time-based view

## Pricing Structure

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | 3 projects, basic AI |
| Pro | $8/mo | Unlimited, full AI |
| Business | $16/mo | Advanced permissions |
| Ultimate | $19/mo | Priority support |

## Who It's Best For

Taskade is ideal for teams wanting an all-in-one workspace without the complexity of Notion or the cost of dedicated project management tools. Its AI features add significant value at every tier.
