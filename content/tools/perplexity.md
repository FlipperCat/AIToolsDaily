Perplexity reimagines search for the AI era. Ask questions in natural language and get comprehensive answers with sources—not just a list of blue links.

## Answer Engine, Not Search Engine

Perplexity synthesizes information from multiple sources:
- Reads and summarizes web pages
- Provides cited sources
- Follows up with related questions
- Updates in real-time

## Pro Search

Deep research mode that:
- Asks clarifying questions
- Searches multiple times
- Synthesizes comprehensive answers
- Provides detailed citations

## Focus Modes

- **Web**: General internet search
- **Academic**: Scholarly sources
- **Writing**: No search, just AI
- **Wolfram**: Math and computation
- **Reddit**: Community discussions
- **YouTube**: Video content

Perplexity is the go-to for research tasks where accuracy and sources matter more than creativity.
