Synthesia is the leading AI video generation platform, specializing in creating realistic avatar-based videos from text. It's revolutionized how companies produce training videos, marketing content, and localized videos.

## Realistic AI Avatars

Synthesia features over 100 photorealistic AI avatars that deliver your script naturally. The avatars have realistic facial expressions, hand gestures, and body language—far superior to early AI video tools that looked robotic.

## Multilingual Powerhouse

One of Synthesia's greatest strengths is language support. Create your video once and automatically generate versions in 130+ languages. Each language gets a native-sounding voice that syncs perfectly with the avatar's lip movements.

This makes international training and marketing campaigns dramatically more efficient. Instead of hiring and managing multilingual voice talent, you get instant localized videos.

## Workflow

1. Write your script (or paste existing content)
2. Choose an avatar and voice
3. Customize background and branding
4. Generate the video
5. Download in minutes

The entire process takes 10-30 minutes depending on video length, drastically faster than traditional video production.

## Custom Avatars

You can create videos featuring you or your team members. Synthesia records your likeness and allows videos with your avatar—powerful for brand recognition and personal connection.

## Technical Quality

Recent updates significantly improved lip-sync accuracy and natural delivery. Audio quality is professional-grade with no distortion or unnatural patterns.
