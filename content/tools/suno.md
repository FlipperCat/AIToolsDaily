Suno creates complete songs—lyrics, melody, vocals, and instrumentation—from simple text prompts.

## What is Suno?

Suno is an AI music generation platform that produces full songs with vocals. Unlike tools that create backing tracks, Suno generates complete, radio-ready songs from text descriptions.

## Key Features

### Text-to-Song

Describe what you want:
- "An upbeat pop song about summer road trips"
- "A melancholic folk ballad about lost love"
- "An energetic EDM track for workouts"

Suno creates the complete song.

### AI Vocals

Choose from:
- Male or female voices
- Various singing styles
- Multiple languages
- Emotional tones

### Genre Diversity

Create in any style:
- Pop, Rock, Hip-hop
- Country, Folk, Jazz
- Electronic, Classical
- And experimental genres

### Custom Mode

Advanced control:
- Write your own lyrics
- Select specific genres
- Control song structure
- Influence mood and tempo
