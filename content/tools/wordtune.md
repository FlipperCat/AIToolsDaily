Wordtune takes a different approach from other AI writing tools. Instead of generating content from scratch, it focuses on helping you improve and refine your existing writing.

## Rewriting, Not Generating

Wordtune excels at taking your rough ideas and polishing them into clear, professional prose. It offers multiple rewrite suggestions for any selected text.

## Key Features

- **Rewrite**: Multiple alternative phrasings
- **Tone**: Adjust formal/casual tone
- **Length**: Expand or shorten text
- **Read**: Summarize long documents
- **Spices**: Add examples, statistics

## Pricing Plans

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | 10 rewrites/day |

## Best Use Cases

Wordtune shines when you have a draft that needs polishing. It's particularly valuable for non-native English speakers, business communication, and academic writing.
