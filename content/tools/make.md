Make (formerly Integromat) is the power user's automation tool. Its visual builder and advanced data manipulation capabilities make it ideal for complex workflows that would be cumbersome elsewhere.

## Visual Workflow Building

Make's drag-and-drop interface shows your entire workflow visually. You can see exactly how data flows between apps and where transformations occur.

## Advanced Features

- **Routers**: Split workflows into branches
- **Iterators**: Loop through arrays
- **Aggregators**: Combine multiple items
- **Error Handlers**: Graceful failure management
- **Custom Functions**: JavaScript for transformations
- **Data Stores**: Persistent storage

## Make vs Zapier

Make often costs 50-80% less for equivalent workflows. It handles data transformation better but requires more technical understanding. Choose Make for complex workflows and value; choose Zapier for simplicity.
