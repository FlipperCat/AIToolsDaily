tl;dv focuses on making meeting content shareable and actionable. Record meetings, let AI identify key moments, and share clips with teammates who couldn't attend.

## Clip-Centric Workflow

tl;dv's strength is shareability:
- Create clips of key moments
- Share via link
- Embed in documents
- Build clip collections

## Team Collaboration

- **Library**: Central meeting repository
- **Reactions**: React to moments with emoji
- **Comments**: Discuss specific timestamps
- **Collections**: Organize clips by topic
