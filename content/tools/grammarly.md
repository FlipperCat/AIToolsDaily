Grammarly is the most widely-used AI writing assistant, helping millions of people write more clearly and correctly every day. It goes beyond basic spell-check to improve your overall writing quality.

## More Than Grammar

While Grammarly started as a grammar checker, it now offers comprehensive writing assistance including clarity improvements, tone detection, and AI-powered text generation with GrammarlyGO.

## Where Grammarly Works

- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- **Desktop**: Windows, macOS apps
- **Mobile**: iOS, Android keyboards
- **Integrations**: Gmail, Google Docs, Word, Slack

## Pricing Options

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Basic grammar, spelling, punctuation |
| Premium | $12/mo | Advanced suggestions, tone, GrammarlyGO |
| Business | $15/user/mo | Team features, style guides, analytics |

## Why Grammarly Remains Essential

Even with ChatGPT and other AI tools, Grammarly serves a different purpose: it catches errors in real-time as you type anywhere, making it an essential layer of quality control.
