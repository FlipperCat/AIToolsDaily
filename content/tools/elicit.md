Elicit is the AI research assistant built specifically for academic work. It searches papers, extracts key findings, and helps you understand the research landscape.

## Research Workflows

Elicit helps with:
- **Literature Reviews**: Find relevant papers
- **Extraction**: Pull key data from papers
- **Synthesis**: Understand consensus
- **Organization**: Manage citations

## Key Features

- **Paper Search**: Semantic search of papers
- **Abstract Analysis**: Quick paper summaries
- **Data Extraction**: Pull findings, methods, results
- **Comparison**: Compare across papers
- **Export**: To Zotero, Mendeley, etc.

Elicit is essential for anyone doing serious academic research. It doesn't replace reading papers but dramatically speeds up finding, filtering, and extracting insights from them.
