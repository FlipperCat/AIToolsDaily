Coda AI enhances an already powerful doc platform. Unlike pure text AI, Coda AI understands your tables, databases, and formulas, making it uniquely suited for structured work.

## AI + Structured Data

Coda AI can:
- Analyze table data
- Generate formulas
- Create visualizations
- Answer questions about your data
- Automate workflows

## Key Capabilities

- **Column AI**: Auto-fill columns with AI
- **Formula Helper**: Generate complex formulas
- **Content Generation**: Write in context
- **Data Insights**: Analyze trends
- **Workflow AI**: Suggest automations

## Coda vs Notion

While both are doc platforms with AI, Coda's strength is computation—formulas, tables, and automations. Choose Coda if you need to work with structured data; choose Notion for general documentation.
