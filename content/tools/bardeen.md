Bardeen runs directly in your browser, automating tasks on any website you visit. It's particularly powerful for scraping data, filling forms, and connecting web-based tools.

## Browser-Native Automation

Unlike server-based tools, Bardeen runs in your Chrome browser:
- Scrape data from any website
- Automate repetitive clicks
- Fill forms automatically
- Transfer data between tabs

## AI Magic Box

Bardeen's AI can:
- Extract structured data from pages
- Identify patterns automatically
- Suggest automation actions
- Generate scraping selectors
