Descript transforms video editing. Instead of a complex timeline, you edit a text transcript—and the video follows. It's the most innovative approach to video editing in years.

## Edit Video Like a Doc

Descript's core innovation:
1. Import video/audio
2. AI transcribes automatically
3. Edit the transcript text
4. Video edits match automatically

Delete a word, the video cuts. Copy a sentence, the video duplicates.

## AI Features

- **Overdub**: Clone your voice
- **Studio Sound**: Fix bad audio
- **Eye Contact**: Correct gaze
- **Filler Words**: Auto-remove "um"s
- **Green Screen**: AI background removal
