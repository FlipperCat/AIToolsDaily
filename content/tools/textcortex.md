TextCortex brings AI writing assistance directly into your browser. Instead of switching between your email, website, and a separate AI tool, TextCortex works everywhere you type—making AI-powered writing as natural as your regular typing.

## Why TextCortex Stands Out

The fundamental challenge with AI writing tools is context-switching. You're writing an email, need help, copy text to a separate tool, generate content, then paste it back. TextCortex eliminates this friction entirely. Activate it in your email, LinkedIn, blog platform, or anywhere else and get AI assistance instantly.

## How It Works

Install the TextCortex browser extension. A sidebar appears wherever you have a text field. Options include:

**Generate**: Create new content from scratch. Specify your topic, tone, and length. TextCortex generates original writing.

**Rewrite**: Improve existing text. Select your paragraph and choose from multiple rewriting styles—formal, casual, professional, creative.

**Expand**: Make short text longer. Perfect for developing bullet points into full paragraphs.

**Simplify**: Make complex text clearer and easier to read.

**Tone Adjustment**: Change emotional tone without changing meaning. Convert formal to casual or vice versa.

**Grammar Check**: Identify grammar, punctuation, and style issues.

## Key Templates

**Email**: Professional emails, follow-ups, negotiations, apologies, congratulations. Templates ensure appropriate tone.
