Otter.ai automates the tedious part of meetings: taking notes. It joins your calls, transcribes everything, identifies who said what, and generates summaries automatically.

## Meeting Intelligence

Otter doesn't just transcribe—it understands:
- Who spoke and when
- Key discussion points
- Action items and owners
- Questions that were asked
- Decisions that were made

## OtterPilot

Let Otter join meetings for you:
- Auto-joins scheduled meetings
- Records and transcribes
- Sends summary to all participants
- Works even if you can't attend
