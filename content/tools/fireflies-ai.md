Fireflies.ai goes beyond transcription to provide conversation intelligence. Track topics, analyze talk time, and gain insights from patterns across all your meetings.

## Conversation Intelligence

Fireflies analyzes meetings for:
- Talk-to-listen ratios
- Topic frequency
- Sentiment analysis
- Question patterns
- Keyword tracking

## Sales Team Features

- **CRM Sync**: Auto-log calls to Salesforce
- **Deal Tracking**: Monitor conversation health
- **Coaching**: Review team calls
- **Scorecards**: Rate call quality
