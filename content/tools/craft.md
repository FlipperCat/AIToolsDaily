Craft is what happens when Apple design philosophy meets note-taking. It's the most beautiful document app available, now enhanced with AI assistance.

## Apple-Native Excellence

Craft is built specifically for Apple platforms:
- Native SwiftUI performance
- iCloud sync
- Widgets support
- Shortcuts integration
- Apple Pencil support

## AI Features

- **Write**: Generate content from prompts
- **Summarize**: Condense documents
- **Extend**: Expand on ideas
- **Improve**: Polish writing
- **Translate**: Convert languages

| Plan | Price | Features |

## Why Choose Craft?

If you value aesthetics, use Apple devices, and want a fast, reliable note-taking app with AI, Craft is unmatched. It's not as flexible as Notion but far more polished.
