Gamma reimagines presentations by using AI to generate beautiful, engaging content instantly.

## What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered presentation tool that creates visually stunning decks from simple text prompts. It's designed to make anyone look like a professional designer.

## Key Features

### AI Generation

Create presentations instantly:
- Enter a topic or outline
- AI generates full deck
- Professional design applied
- Content structured automatically

### Beautiful Design

Automatic aesthetics:
- Modern templates
- Consistent styling
- Smart layouts
- Visual hierarchy

### Interactive Content

More than static slides:
- Embed videos
- Interactive charts
- Live websites
- Nested cards

### Collaboration

Work together:
- Real-time editing
- Comments and feedback
- Share links
- Team workspaces

## How It Works

1. **Enter your topic** - Write what you want to present
2. **Choose a template** - Select visual style
3. **AI generates** - Full presentation created
4. **Refine** - Edit text and images
5. **Share** - Present or export

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Provide clear outlines** - Structure helps AI
2. **Be specific about audience** - Tone adjusts accordingly
3. **Edit generated content** - Add your expertise
4. **Use the AI to iterate** - Refine with follow-up prompts
