Ideogram emerged as the answer to AI art's biggest weakness: text. While other tools struggle with legible text in images, Ideogram consistently produces clean, readable typography.

## Text That Actually Works

Ideogram's breakthrough is reliable text rendering:
- Logos with clear typography
- Posters with readable headlines
- Signs and banners
- Stylized text effects
- Multi-line text compositions

## Magic Prompt Feature

Magic Prompt automatically enhances your prompts for better results. It adds artistic details and technical specifications that improve output quality without requiring prompt engineering expertise.
