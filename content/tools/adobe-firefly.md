Adobe Firefly stands out as the commercially-safest AI image generator. Trained exclusively on licensed and public domain content, it's designed for professional use without legal concerns.

## Commercial Safety First

Firefly's training data includes:
- Adobe Stock licensed images
- Openly licensed content
- Public domain works

This means your generations are safe for commercial use, with Adobe offering IP indemnification for enterprise customers.

## Creative Cloud Integration

Firefly powers AI features across Adobe products:
- **Photoshop**: Generative Fill, Expand
- **Illustrator**: Vector recoloring, generation
- **Express**: Quick content creation
- **Premiere**: Coming soon

## Why Choose Firefly?

If you're a professional designer concerned about copyright and commercial use, Firefly is the safest choice. Its Adobe integration also makes it incredibly convenient for existing Creative Cloud users.
