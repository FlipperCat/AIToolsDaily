Pika is one of the leading AI video generation platforms, creating impressive short videos from text descriptions or images.

## What is Pika?

Pika generates video content using AI. Describe what you want or upload an image, and Pika creates a video clip with realistic motion and effects.

## Key Features

### Text-to-Video

Generate from descriptions:
- Describe scenes in natural language
- Specify camera movements
- Control style and mood
- Multiple generation options

### Image-to-Video

Animate still images:
- Upload any image
- AI adds natural motion
- Bring photos to life
- Product animations

### Video Editing

Modify existing videos:
- Expand canvas
- Change elements
- Add or remove objects
- Modify motion

### Lip Sync

Add speech to characters:
- Upload audio
- Characters lip sync
- Natural mouth movements
- Multiple languages

## Quality Tips

1. **Be specific** - Detailed prompts get better results
2. **Describe motion** - Specify how things should move
3. **Use references** - Image-to-video is often more predictable
4. **Iterate** - Generate multiple versions

## Current Limitations

- Maximum ~4 second clips
- Complex scenes can be inconsistent
- Human faces can be challenging
- Real-time generation not available
