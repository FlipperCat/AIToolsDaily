Runway is at the forefront of AI video generation. Its Gen-2 model produces some of the most impressive text-to-video results available, opening new possibilities for creators.

## Text-to-Video Pioneer

Runway's Gen-2 can create video from:
- Text descriptions alone
- Images (animate stills)
- Existing video (transform style)
- Combination of inputs

## Video Capabilities

- **Generation**: 4 seconds of video from text
- **Motion Brush**: Control movement in specific areas
- **Camera Controls**: Pan, zoom, tilt
- **Expansion**: Extend video beyond edges
- **Interpolation**: Smooth frame transitions
