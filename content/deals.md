## Annual vs Monthly: When to Commit

**Get annual if:**
- You've used the monthly plan for 2+ months
- The tool is core to your workflow
- Annual discount is 20%+ (usually 30-50%)

**Stay monthly if:**
- You're still testing
- AI tools are evolving fast (new options coming)
- Your needs might change
