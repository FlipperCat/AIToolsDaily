Real estate, one of the oldest industries, is being transformed by AI. From property valuations to tenant management, here's how AI is reshaping how we buy, sell, and manage property.

## Property Valuation

### Automated Valuation Models (AVMs)

AI-powered property valuations are now standard:

| Provider | Accuracy | Coverage | Use Case |
|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Zillow Zestimate | ±7% median error | 100M+ homes | Consumer guidance |
| HouseCanary | ±5% median error | National | Institutional |
| CoreLogic | ±4% median error | National | Lender decisions |

**How they work:**
- Property characteristics (beds, baths, sqft)
- Recent comparable sales
- Neighborhood trends
- Economic indicators
- Satellite imagery analysis

**Impact:** Instant valuations that once took days. Not replacing appraisers for transactions, but transforming initial estimates.

### Computer Vision Applications

AI analyzes property images:
- Condition assessment from photos
- Renovation opportunity identification
- Quality of finishes scoring
- Curb appeal measurement

**Impact:** More objective property assessment, less reliance on agent descriptions.

## Lead Generation and Qualification

### Predictive Lead Scoring

AI identifies likely sellers before they list:

**Signals analyzed:**
- Property tenure length
- Life events (job changes, divorces, deaths)
- Financial indicators
- Search behavior
- Social signals

**Tools:** SmartZip, Offrs, Revaluate

**Impact:** Agents contact likely sellers months before listing.

### Automated Qualification

AI chatbots qualify leads 24/7:
- Budget assessment
- Timeline understanding
- Preferences gathering
- Appointment scheduling

**Tools:** Structurely, Ylopo, Chime

**Impact:** Agents focus on qualified prospects only.

## Property Search

### Intelligent Matching

AI understands what buyers really want:

**Beyond filters:**
- "Walkable neighborhood" → proximity + walkability scores
- "Good schools" → school ratings + catchment areas
- "Quiet street" → traffic data + noise analysis
- "Natural light" → window orientation + tree cover

**Tools:** Redfin, Compass, HouseCanary

**Impact:** Better matches, fewer showings needed.

### Visual Search

Upload a photo of a home you like → Find similar properties

**How it works:**
- Style classification (modern, craftsman, colonial)
- Feature detection (open floor plan, large windows)
- Aesthetic matching

## Challenges and Limitations

### Data Quality

AVMs are only as good as their data:
- Unique properties challenge models
- Recent renovations not captured
- Neighborhood micro-variations

### Bias Concerns

Historical data embeds historical biases:
- Redlining legacy in training data
- Appraisal bias perpetuation
- Fair housing implications

**Mitigation:** Algorithmic audits, bias testing, diverse training data.

### Human Judgment

AI can't replace:
- Relationship building
- Negotiation skills
- Local market nuance
- Emotional intelligence in transactions
