Agriculture is experiencing a technological revolution with AI enabling precision farming, yield optimization, and sustainable practices at unprecedented scales.

## Precision Agriculture

### Crop Monitoring

AI analyzes field conditions:

| Method | Technology | Detection |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Satellite imagery | Multispectral analysis | Large-scale patterns |
| Drone surveys | High-resolution images | Field-level detail |
| Ground sensors | IoT devices | Real-time conditions |
| Weather stations | Predictive models | Climate patterns |

**Impact**: 10-20% yield improvements typical.

### Variable Rate Application

AI optimizes input application:
- Fertilizer rates by zone
- Pesticide application targeting
- Irrigation scheduling
- Seed density optimization

**Savings**: 15-25% reduction in input costs.

### Soil Analysis

AI assesses soil health:
- Nutrient mapping
- pH variations
- Moisture levels
- Organic matter content

**Tools**: Climate Corporation, Taranis, Ceres Imaging

## Yield Prediction

### Forecasting Models

AI predicts harvest outcomes:
- Weather pattern analysis
- Historical yield data
- Satellite imagery assessment
- Growth stage modeling

**Accuracy**: Within 5-10% of actual yields.

### Risk Assessment

AI identifies threats:
- Disease outbreak prediction
- Pest pressure forecasting
- Weather damage risk
- Market price prediction

## Crop Protection

### Pest Detection

AI identifies threats early:
- Image recognition of pests
- Drone-based scouting
- Trap monitoring
- Predictive spread models

**Response Time**: Days earlier than manual detection.

### Disease Identification

AI diagnoses plant disease:
- Leaf image analysis
- Pattern recognition
- Treatment recommendations
- Severity assessment

**Accuracy**: 95%+ for common diseases.

### Weed Management

AI enables targeted control:
- Weed identification
- Precision spraying
- Mechanical removal
- Herbicide optimization

**Tools**: Blue River Technology (John Deere), Plantix
