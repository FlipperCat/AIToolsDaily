# About AI Tools Daily

AI Tools Daily helps you work smarter with AI. We review tools, create tutorials, and share strategies for automating your work.

## What We Cover

- **AI Productivity Tools** — ChatGPT, Claude, Notion AI, and others
- **Automation Platforms** — Zapier, Make.com, n8n, and alternatives
- **AI Writing Tools** — Content creation, copywriting, editing
- **Workflow Automation** — Connect your tools, eliminate manual work
- **Honest Reviews** — Real testing, actual opinions, no fluff

## Our Approach

We test everything we write about. No sponsored reviews, no pay-for-placement. If a tool is good, we say so. If it has problems, we mention those too.

We use affiliate links where available — this supports the site at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we'd actually use ourselves.

## Stay Updated

New articles posted weekly. Best way to stay updated is to bookmark the site and check back regularly.
