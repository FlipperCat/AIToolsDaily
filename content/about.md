# About AI Tools Daily

**Since 2023**, AI Tools Daily has helped thousands of professionals, entrepreneurs, and creators work smarter with AI. We're the no-BS guide to finding and using the right AI tools for your workflow.

The AI landscape changes weekly. New tools launch constantly. Most reviews are paid promotions or surface-level overviews.

We're different.

## Our Approach

### 1. We Test Everything

Every tool review comes from actual usage. We don't write about tools we haven't tried. Most reviews include screenshots and real examples from our testing.

### 2. No Pay-for-Play

Companies don't pay for reviews or placement. Our rankings are based on actual performance, not advertising spend.

### 3. We Use Affiliate Links — Transparently

Yes, we use affiliate links where available. This keeps the site free and independent. But we never recommend tools just because they pay well. If a tool isn't good, we say so.

### 4. We Update Our Content

AI moves fast. We regularly update reviews when tools change. Check the "Updated" dates on articles to see when we last verified the information.
