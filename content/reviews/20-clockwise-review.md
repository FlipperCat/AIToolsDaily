**Smart Scheduling**: AI organizes meetings and focus time for optimal productivity patterns.

**Focus Time Protection**: Automatically reserves and protects deep work blocks from interruptions.

**Meeting Consolidation**: Groups meetings into focused blocks to minimize context switching.
