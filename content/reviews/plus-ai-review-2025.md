Most AI presentation tools ask you to move into their world: new editor, new format, new export headaches. Plus AI takes the opposite bet — it lives inside Google Slides and PowerPoint as an add-on, generating and editing native slides in the tools your company already uses. After several weeks of building real work decks with it, that bet looks smart, with some clear limits. Here's our take as of March 2025.

## What is Plus AI?

Plus AI is an AI presentation assistant that installs as a Google Workspace add-on (and a PowerPoint add-in). Give it a prompt, an outline, or a pasted document, and it generates a complete deck — titles, body copy, layouts, images — directly in a normal Slides or PowerPoint file. From there you edit like you always do, with the AI available per-slide for rewrites, layout remixes, and insertions.

The distinction from tools like Gamma or Tome is structural, not cosmetic: there is no separate platform. The output *is* the deliverable.

## Key features

- **Prompt-to-deck generation.** Describe the presentation, pick a slide count and theme, and get a full first draft. You can review and edit the generated outline before it commits to slides, which is worth doing every time.
- **Document-to-deck conversion.** Paste meeting notes, a report, or a blog post and Plus AI restructures it into slides. This is the feature we used most — turning existing prose into a deck skeleton is the genuinely tedious part of presentation work.
- **Per-slide editing.** Select any slide — including in old decks — and ask for a rewrite, a tone change, a layout remix, or a new slide inserted to match the theme.
- **Custom themes and branding.** Paid tiers let you set brand colors, fonts, and layouts so generated decks start on-brand instead of on-template.
- **Native output.** Everything is ordinary Slides/PowerPoint objects: your colleagues can edit the deck without knowing AI touched it, and nothing breaks on export because there is no export.

## Pros

- **No platform lock-in.** This is the headline advantage. Decks live in your Google Drive or OneDrive, follow your sharing permissions, and outlive your subscription.
- **The editing loop is where it shines.** Competitors are strong at generating deck one; Plus AI is unusually good at deck *fifty* — the weekly update where you need three new slides matching an existing theme.
- **First drafts are structurally sound.** Sensible narrative order, one idea per slide, reasonable text density. You'll rewrite the words, but rarely the skeleton.
- **Fits corporate reality.** IT-approved tools, existing templates, colleagues who edit your files — Plus AI works with all of it rather than around it.

## Cons and limitations

- **Design ceiling is Google Slides' ceiling.** Gamma's card-style output simply looks more modern than anything constrained to standard Slides layouts. If visual wow is the goal, this isn't the tool — see our [Gamma review](/reviews/14-gamma-review/) for that end of the spectrum.
- **Generated visuals are the weak spot.** Stock and AI-generated images are serviceable but generic, and diagrams/charts often need manual rework. We replaced most images in every deck we shipped.
- **Copy runs corporate-bland.** The writing is competent and clichéd. Expect to rewrite headlines in your own voice.
- **Slower than native platforms.** Generation runs through an add-on sidebar, which feels clunkier than Gamma's web app and can hiccup on very long decks.
- **No free tier to speak of.** There's a trial, but ongoing use requires a subscription, whereas [Tome](/reviews/15-tome-review/) and Gamma both offer usable free plans.

## Pricing

As of March 2025, approximately: individual plans run about $10–15 per user/month depending on tier and billing cycle, with a Pro tier around $20–25 adding custom branding, higher generation limits, and priority features, and team pricing above that. A short free trial covers a handful of decks. As always with AI tools, treat these numbers as a snapshot — check the current pricing page.

## Who is it for?

- **People who present inside Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 for a living** — consultants, account managers, sales engineers, internal comms. The weekly-deck crowd.
- **Teams with template discipline,** where "make it match the brand deck" is a requirement, not a preference.
- **Anyone converting documents to decks regularly.**

Who should look elsewhere: startup founders and creators optimizing for visual impact (Gamma), narrative/scrolling formats (Tome), and data-heavy dashboard-style decks, where a purpose-built layout tool like Beautiful.ai still has an edge — our [Gamma vs Tome comparison](/compare/gamma-vs-tome-2024/) maps that landscape.

## Verdict

Plus AI wins by refusing to be a destination. It's not the prettiest AI deck generator — Gamma keeps that crown — but it's the one whose output slots directly into how organizations actually make, share, and edit presentations. The drafts are solid, the per-slide editing on existing decks is quietly the best feature in the category, and the lack of lock-in means adopting it is a low-stakes decision. If your presentations must end their life as Google Slides or PowerPoint files — and for most working professionals, they must — Plus AI is the most practical AI presentation tool we've tested.
