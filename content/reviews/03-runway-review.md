## Runway ML Review 2026: Professional AI Video Generation and Editing

Runway ML has revolutionized video production by making advanced AI tools accessible to creators of all skill levels. With features for generating videos from text, editing existing footage with AI, and applying professional effects, Runway ML is transforming how videos are created in 2026.

### What is Runway ML?

Runway ML is a cutting-edge creative platform that brings AI-powered video generation and editing capabilities to filmmakers, content creators, and marketing professionals. Founded by machine learning researchers, Runway combines text-to-video generation, intelligent video editing, and advanced effects tools in one intuitive platform.

### Key Features

**Text-to-Video Generation**: Create videos directly from text descriptions. Describe your vision and watch it come to life with AI-generated footage that includes motion, lighting, and effects.

**Image-to-Video Conversion**: Transform static images into dynamic video sequences with realistic motion and depth.

**Green Screen Removal**: Automatically remove or replace backgrounds without complex masking or rotoscoping.

**Sky & Object Replacement**: Intelligent tools to replace skies, objects, and elements within video footage automatically.

**Motion Tracking**: Advanced AI tracks objects across footage for seamless effects application and compositing.

**Inpainting & Outpainting**: Extend video frames, remove unwanted elements, or fill areas intelligently using context-aware AI.

**Multi-Motion Brush**: Control motion direction and intensity across specific areas of your video frame.

**Video Frame Interpolation**: Increase frame rates and smooth motion for 60fps or 120fps output.

### Cons

- **Credit System Learning**: New users must understand credit allocation and plan usage accordingly
- **Generation Speed**: Video generation takes minutes rather than seconds, unsuitable for real-time workflows
- **Variable Quality**: Output quality depends heavily on prompt clarity and instruction detail
- **Cost for Volume Creators**: Extensive video production can become expensive with monthly credit limits
- **Limited Free Tier**: Free plan offers minimal credits for serious exploration
- **Export Watermarks**: Some lower tiers add watermarks to exported videos
- **Computing Limitations**: Complex scenes with multiple effects may have processing constraints
