Perplexity AI combines search engines with AI to answer questions with sources. Instead of giving you links to click, it reads the sources and gives you a direct answer.

## What is Perplexity?

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine. Ask a question, and it:
1. Searches the web in real-time
2. Reads relevant sources
3. Synthesizes an answer
4. Shows citations for verification

It's like having a research assistant that reads articles for you and summarizes the findings.

### Answer Engine

Ask any question and get a direct answer with sources. No clicking through 10 links to find what you need.

**Perplexity response:** Direct answer with current rates, factors to consider, sourced from financial sites — all in one response.

Upload documents and ask questions about them. Perplexity reads the file and answers based on its contents plus web knowledge.

## What Perplexity Does Well

### 1. Speed to Answer

Google gives you links. You click, read, click back, try another. Perplexity gives you the answer immediately.

For factual questions, this saves significant time.

### 2. Source Transparency

Every claim is cited. Click the source number to verify. This matters for:
- Research
- Fact-checking
- Building trust in the answer

### 3. Current Information

Unlike ChatGPT (knowledge cutoff) or Google (SEO-gamed results), Perplexity searches live and cites recent sources.

For current events, recent product releases, or up-to-date information, it's superior.

### 4. Research Depth

Pro Search genuinely helps with complex topics. It asks follow-up questions to understand what you need, then delivers comprehensive answers.

### 5. No Ads

Google search results are increasingly cluttered with ads and SEO content. Perplexity is clean and focused on answering your question.

## What Perplexity Does Poorly

### 1. Not Conversational

It's built for search, not conversation. For brainstorming, creative work, or back-and-forth dialogue, ChatGPT or Claude are better.

### 2. Can Still Be Wrong

Despite citing sources, Perplexity can misinterpret them or cite unreliable sources. Always verify important information.

### 3. Limited for Non-Search Tasks

It doesn't write long-form content well. It doesn't code. It doesn't create images. It does one thing — search — and does it well.

### 4. Source Quality Varies

It pulls from whatever ranks on the web. Sometimes that includes low-quality or biased sources. Check the citations.

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific

Bad: "Tell me about AI"
Good: "What are the main differences between transformer and diffusion models in AI?"

### 2. Use Focus Modes

Academic mode for research papers. Reddit mode for community opinions. Match the mode to your need.

### 3. Use Pro Search for Complex Topics

Basic search for quick facts. Pro Search when you need depth and nuance.

### 4. Save to Collections

Organize research by project. Reference later without re-searching.

### 5. Verify Sources

Always click through on important claims. Perplexity summarizes well but isn't infallible.
