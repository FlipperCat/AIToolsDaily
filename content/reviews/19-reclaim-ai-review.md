### What is Reclaim AI?

Reclaim AI is an intelligent calendar assistant that uses machine learning to optimize schedules and protect productive work time. By automatically finding meeting slots, preventing back-to-back meetings, and consolidating focus time, Reclaim AI transforms chaotic calendars into optimized schedules that boost productivity and reduce burnout.

### Key Features

**Intelligent Scheduling**: AI finds optimal meeting times by analyzing availability and preferences.

**Focus Time Protection**: Automatically blocks focus time for deep work and prevents interruptions.

**Meeting Consolidation**: Groups meetings together to minimize context switching and calendar fragmentation.

**Buffer Time**: Creates breaks between meetings for transition time and recovery.

**Smart Decline**: AI intelligently declines or suggests alternatives for conflicting invites.

**Async Work Blocks**: Reserves time for asynchronous tasks without unnecessary meetings.

**Team Insights**: Shows team productivity data and meeting load across organization.

**Integration Ecosystem**: Connects with Google Calendar, Outlook, and productivity tools.

**Calendar Analytics**: Provides insights into time usage and productivity patterns.

**Customizable Rules**: Set personal preferences and rules that govern AI scheduling decisions.
