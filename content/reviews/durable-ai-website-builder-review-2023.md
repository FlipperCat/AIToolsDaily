The pitch for Durable is almost comically simple: tell it what your business is and where it is, and it builds you a complete website in about 30 seconds. No templates to browse, no drag-and-drop learning curve, no blank page. For the plumber or dog groomer who has put off getting a website for three years, that pitch is aimed squarely at you.

We spent a week building sites with it to see whether the promise holds up. Short answer: the 30 seconds is real, the website is real, and the ceiling is low — which is sometimes exactly what you want.

## What Durable is

Durable is an AI website builder aimed at solo service businesses. Instead of starting from a template gallery, you answer two questions — business type and location — and its AI assembles a one-page site: hero section, services list, testimonials placeholder, about blurb, contact form, and stock images that roughly match your trade. It writes all the copy itself.

Around the website, Durable has been quietly bolting on a small-business toolkit: a simple CRM for tracking leads from your contact form, basic invoicing, and an AI copywriting assistant for regenerating any section of text. The company's angle is clearly "the software stack for a one-person business," with the website as the wedge.

## Key features

- **Instant generation.** Business type + location + a name → a full site in under a minute. Regenerate any section you don't like with one click.
- **AI copywriting throughout.** Every headline and paragraph can be rewritten by the AI. Quality is serviceable — think competent brochure copy, not brand voice. (If you've used tools like [ChatGPT](/reviews/chatgpt-review/) for marketing copy, the output will feel familiar.)
- **Section-based editor.** You edit within a structured layout: reorder sections, swap images, change colors and fonts. It's closer to filling out a form than designing a page.
- **Built-in CRM and invoicing.** Contact form submissions land in a lightweight lead tracker, and you can send simple invoices. Basic, but genuinely useful for a solo operator.
- **Custom domains and basic SEO fields.** Connect your own domain on paid plans and edit page titles and descriptions.

## What it does well

**Speed to "good enough."** This is the whole product. We generated a site for a fictional Austin pressure-washing business and the first draft was — honestly — better than half the real small-business sites we see. Clear headline, plausible service list, working contact form, mobile-friendly out of the box.

**Zero decision fatigue.** Traditional builders bury you in choices. Durable makes nearly all of them for you, and for its target user that's a feature, not a bug. There is no way to spend six hours choosing fonts, because there are barely any fonts to choose.

**The regenerate button.** Don't like the copy or the layout of a section? Click, get a new one, repeat. It turns editing into a slot machine you usually win.

## Cons and limitations

- **One page, mostly.** Durable is fundamentally a one-page-site tool. Multi-page structures are limited, and anything resembling a content site or blog strategy is out of scope.
- **Design ceiling is low.** Every Durable site looks like a Durable site: clean, modern, and interchangeable. If brand distinctiveness matters, you'll outgrow it fast.
- **No real e-commerce.** There's no product catalog or checkout. Service businesses only.
- **AI copy needs a pass.** The generated text is generic by design — "We provide quality service you can trust" territory. Plan to rewrite the sections customers actually read. A tool like [Rytr](/reviews/rytr-review-2023/) or ChatGPT can help punch it up, or just write it yourself.
- **Stock imagery gives it away.** The auto-selected photos are fine but visibly stock. Swapping in real photos of your work is the single highest-impact edit you can make.
- **You're locked in.** There's no site export. If you leave Durable, you rebuild elsewhere.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | What you get |

| Free | $0 | Generate and preview sites, no publishing |
| Starter | ~$12-15/month | Publish your site, custom domain, AI editing |

That undercuts most mainstream builders slightly, and the free preview means you can see exactly what you'd get before paying anything. Prices and plan boundaries have been shifting as the product evolves, so treat these as ballpark figures.

## Who it's for

- **Solo service businesses** — trades, cleaners, tutors, consultants, salons — that need a credible web presence this afternoon.
- **Side hustles** testing whether an idea deserves a real website.
- **Agencies and freelancers** spinning up quick placeholder sites for clients (though see the lock-in caveat).

Who should skip it: online stores, content publishers, anyone with a designer's eye, and businesses that expect their website to be a differentiator rather than a checkbox. If your marketing runs on content and automation, you'll want a more conventional stack — something like a proper builder plus [Zapier](/reviews/zapier-review/) for the workflow glue — rather than Durable's closed garden.

## Verdict

Durable does exactly what it says, and the honest question isn't "is it powerful?" — it isn't — but "is a good-enough website today worth more than a great website never?" For a huge slice of small businesses, yes. It's the fastest path we've tested from nothing to a live, professional-looking site, and the built-in CRM makes it more than a gimmick.

Just go in knowing what it is: a starting point with a low ceiling, generic copy you should rewrite, and no exit door. At around $12-15 a month, that trade is easy to justify for a solo operator — and easy to outgrow the moment your ambitions do.

**Rating: 4/5** for its target user; 2.5/5 for everyone else.
