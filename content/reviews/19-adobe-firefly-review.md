## Adobe Firefly Review 2026: Generative AI for Creative Professionals

Adobe Firefly is a generative AI family of models integrated directly into Creative Cloud applications. It represents Adobe's answer to standalone AI tools like DALL-E and Midjourney, with the advantage of being built into tools professional designers already use daily.

## What Is Adobe Firefly?

Adobe Firefly is a collection of generative AI features that enable users to create, edit, and enhance digital content within Adobe's ecosystem. Available in Photoshop, Illustrator, Express, and other Creative Cloud apps, Firefly allows professionals to generate images, expand backgrounds, remove objects, and modify content using natural language prompts.

Firefly is trained on Adobe's library of images and licensed content, making it suitable for commercial use without copyright concerns—a significant advantage over other generative AI tools.

## Key Features

**Generative Fill in Photoshop**
This game-changing feature allows users to select areas of images and use text prompts to fill, remove, or modify content. It's revolutionary for photographers and designers who previously spent hours on manual editing.

**Generative Expand**
Extend the boundaries of images by describing what you want to add. Perfect for creating different aspect ratios or expanding compositions without reshooting.

**Text to Image**
Generate original images from text descriptions, usable as starting points for design projects or standalone assets.

**Generative Remove and Recolor**
Intelligently remove unwanted elements from photos while preserving natural backgrounds, or recolor objects to match design specifications.

**Style Transfer**
Apply artistic styles from reference images or descriptions to your photos and designs.

**Content-Aware Scaling**
Resize images intelligently while preserving important content, enhanced by generative AI to fill new space naturally.

**Integration with Creative Cloud**
Firefly integrates seamlessly into Photoshop, Illustrator, Adobe Express, and InDesign, making it accessible to designers' existing workflows without context switching.

**Commercial Use License**
Images generated with Firefly can be used commercially without copyright restrictions, a major advantage for professional applications.
