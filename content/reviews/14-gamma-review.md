## Gamma Review 2026: AI Presentation and Document Creation Platform

Gamma has emerged as one of the most versatile AI design platforms, extending beyond presentations to include documents, webpages, and interactive content. With AI that understands design principles and can interpret intent from minimal input, Gamma enables creators to produce professional visual content across multiple formats.

### What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered platform for creating presentations, documents, interactive webpages, and visual content. Using natural language understanding, Gamma interprets your ideas and generates complete, professionally designed assets that would typically require hours of design work or hiring professionals.

### Key Features

**AI Content Generation**: Describe your idea and Gamma generates complete presentations, documents, or pages.

**Multiple Format Support**: Create presentations, documents, webpages, and interactive content from single platform.

**Text-to-Design**: Write content naturally and let AI handle all layout, design, and formatting.

**Real-time Collaboration**: Multiple users edit simultaneously on projects.

**Design Themes**: Extensive theme library ensures consistent, professional appearance.

**Media Integration**: Easily add images, videos, and embeds to enhance content.

**Interactive Elements**: Add buttons, forms, and interactive components to engage audiences.

**Mobile Optimization**: All content automatically optimized for mobile viewing.

**Export & Publish**: Share live links or export in multiple formats.

**Analytics**: Track engagement and performance of published content.
