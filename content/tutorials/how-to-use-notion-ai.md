Notion AI brings artificial intelligence directly into your workspace. Instead of switching between apps, you can write, summarize, and organize—all without leaving Notion.

This guide covers everything you need to know.

## What is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an add-on ($10/month) that adds AI capabilities to Notion:

- **Write** — Draft content, expand ideas, continue writing
- **Edit** — Fix grammar, change tone, simplify language
- **Summarize** — Condense long pages into key points
- **Extract** — Pull action items, key info from notes
- **Brainstorm** — Generate ideas, outlines, lists
- **Translate** — Convert content to other languages
- **Q&A** — Ask questions about your workspace

## Getting Started

### Enabling Notion AI

1. Open Notion
2. Go to Settings & Members
3. Select Plans under Workspace
4. Add Notion AI ($10/member/month)

Or start a free trial when prompted.

### Accessing Notion AI

Three ways to access:

1. **Space key** — Press `Space` at the start of a new line
2. **Slash command** — Type `/ai` anywhere
3. **Highlight text** — Select text, click "Ask AI"

## Core Features

### 1. Writing with AI

**Start a new draft:**
1. Open a new page or block
2. Press `Space`
3. Type your request: "Write a project proposal for..."
4. AI generates content
5. Accept, edit, or try again

**Continue writing:**
1. Write your opening
2. Press `Space` at the end
3. Select "Continue writing"
4. AI extends your text naturally

**Best prompts for writing:**
- "Write a summary of..."
- "Draft an email about..."
- "Create an outline for..."
- "Write meeting notes for..."

### 2. Editing Existing Content

Select any text and choose:

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| Improve writing | General enhancement |
| Fix spelling & grammar | Correct errors |
| Make shorter | Condense content |
| Make longer | Expand with detail |
| Change tone | Professional, casual, etc. |
| Simplify language | Make easier to read |

**Example workflow:**
1. Write a rough draft
2. Select all text
3. "Improve writing"
4. "Make shorter"
5. "Change tone to professional"

### 3. Summarizing Content

**Summarize a page:**
1. Open any page
2. Type `/summarize` or press `Space` → "Summarize"
3. AI creates a concise summary

**Summarize selected text:**
1. Highlight text
2. Click "Ask AI"
3. Select "Summarize"

**Best uses:**
- Meeting notes → Key decisions
- Research → Main findings
- Articles → Core points
- Long documents → Executive summary

### 4. Extracting Information

**Extract action items:**
1. Open meeting notes or document
2. `/ai` → "Extract action items"
3. AI pulls out tasks with owners/dates

**Extract key information:**
- "Find all dates mentioned"
- "List all people referenced"
- "Extract decisions made"
- "Find all questions raised"

### 5. Brainstorming

**Generate ideas:**
1. Press `Space`
2. "Brainstorm 10 ideas for..."
3. Review and expand on best ones

**Brainstorm prompts:**
- "Brainstorm marketing strategies for..."
- "Generate blog post topics about..."
- "List potential solutions to..."
- "Create name ideas for..."

### 6. Q&A (Ask Questions)

**Ask about current page:**
- "What are the main points?"
- "When is the deadline mentioned?"
- "What did we decide about X?"

**Ask about workspace:**
- "Find my notes from last week's meeting"
- "What projects mention the API?"
- "Summarize recent updates in Project X"

## Practical Workflows

### Meeting Notes Workflow

**Before meeting:**
1. Create meeting page
2. `/ai` → "Create meeting agenda template for [topic]"

**During meeting:**
1. Take rough notes (bullets are fine)

**After meeting:**
1. Select notes
2. "Improve writing" → Clean up
3. "Extract action items" → Create task list
4. "Summarize" → Add executive summary at top

### Writing Workflow

1. **Outline:** `/ai` → "Create outline for article about X"
2. **Draft:** For each section, "Write a paragraph about..."
3. **Expand:** Select thin sections → "Make longer"
4. **Polish:** Select all → "Improve writing"
5. **Tone:** "Change tone to [casual/professional]"

### Research Workflow

1. **Collect:** Paste research into Notion page
2. **Summarize:** "Summarize this research"
3. **Extract:** "List key findings"
4. **Organize:** "Create categories for this information"
5. **Apply:** "How does this apply to [your project]?"

### Email Workflow

1. `/ai` → "Write a professional email about [topic]"
2. Review draft
3. "Make shorter" (emails should be concise)
4. "Change tone to [appropriate tone]"
5. Copy to email client

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific
❌ "Write about marketing"
✅ "Write 3 strategies for marketing a B2B SaaS product to small businesses"

### 2. Provide Context
❌ "Summarize this"
✅ "Summarize this meeting notes page, focusing on decisions made and action items"

### 3. Iterate
First output not right? Try:
- "Make this more specific"
- "Add examples"
- "Focus more on X"
- "Remove the part about Y"

### 4. Use for Structure First
Let AI create structure (outlines, templates), then fill in your expertise.

### 5. Always Review
AI makes mistakes. Verify:
- Facts and figures
- Names and dates
- Tone appropriateness
- Logical accuracy

## Limitations

- **Not connected to internet** — Can't research current info
- **Context window** — Very long pages may be truncated
- **Accuracy** — Can make factual errors
- **Creativity** — Output can be generic without good prompts
- **Integration depth** — Can't modify databases programmatically
