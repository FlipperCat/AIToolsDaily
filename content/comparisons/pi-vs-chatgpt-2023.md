Pi and ChatGPT both put a text box in front of a large language model, but they're built for different jobs. ChatGPT is a general-purpose work assistant that can write, code, analyze, and summarize. Pi, from Inflection AI, is designed to be a "personal AI": warm, curious, and conversational, closer to a thoughtful friend than a productivity tool.

We used both side by side for several weeks on everyday tasks: planning, venting after a long day, learning new topics, drafting emails, and debugging code. The results were more lopsided in some areas than we expected, and closer in others.

If you want a deeper look at either tool on its own, see our [Pi review](/reviews/pi-inflection-ai-review-2023/) and [ChatGPT review](/reviews/chatgpt-review/).

## Conversation style

This is the biggest difference, and it's on purpose.

**Pi** keeps its replies short and nearly always ends with a question. Tell it you're stressed about a job interview and it asks what part worries you most, reflects your answer back, and suggests one or two small things to try. It feels like talking to a patient, emotionally aware listener. It rarely dumps a wall of bullet points on you.

**ChatGPT** answers the question you asked, often at length. Tell it you're stressed about an interview and you'll probably get a structured list: research the company, prepare STAR stories, practice common questions, get sleep. It's useful, but it reads like a well-organized article rather than a conversation.

Neither approach is better in general. If you want to think out loud, Pi's style is more pleasant and often more helpful. If you want a checklist you can act on right now, ChatGPT gets there faster. You can ask Pi for a list, and it will give you one, but it keeps drifting back toward dialogue.

## Voice and accessibility

Pi's voice experience is one of its best features. You can pick from several natural-sounding voices, and hearing replies read aloud makes it feel more like a phone call than a chat window. Pi's short turns also suit voice well: you don't sit through a two-minute monologue.

ChatGPT has added voice conversations to its mobile apps this fall, and the voices are very good. But ChatGPT's answers are longer, so spoken replies can run on. For hands-free, casual conversation, Pi still feels more natural. For voice-driven Q&A where you want detailed answers, ChatGPT is catching up fast.

## Knowledge, accuracy, and reasoning

On factual questions and multi-step reasoning, ChatGPT with GPT-4 is the stronger model. It handles complicated explanations, math word problems, and requests like "compare these three options against these criteria" more reliably.

Pi is fine for general knowledge and explaining concepts in plain language, and it's good at checking that you understood before moving on. It's more likely to stay vague when a question needs precision, and it's not the tool we'd trust for detailed technical or financial reasoning.

Both can make things up. Verify anything important, especially statistics, citations, and medical or legal claims. Pi's friendly tone can make wrong answers sound more believable, which is worth keeping in mind.

## Productivity: writing, coding, and files

This is where ChatGPT pulls far ahead.

- **Writing:** ChatGPT drafts emails, reports, blog outlines, and marketing copy quickly and follows formatting instructions well. Pi can help you think through what to say, but it's not built to produce long, polished documents.
- **Coding:** ChatGPT, especially GPT-4, writes, explains, and debugs code across many languages. Pi can explain programming concepts but isn't a practical coding assistant.
- **Files and data:** ChatGPT Plus includes Advanced Data Analysis, which runs Python on uploaded files: CSV cleanup, charts, quick statistics. Pi has nothing comparable.
- **Images:** ChatGPT Plus now includes DALL·E 3 image generation. Pi is text and voice only.

If your main question is "which one will help me get work done," the answer is ChatGPT, and it isn't close. For a comparison of work-focused assistants, see [Claude 2 vs ChatGPT](/compare/claude-2-vs-chatgpt-2023/).

## Emotional support and wellbeing

Pi is designed for this, and it shows. It's patient, non-judgmental, and good at helping you sort out your feelings or rehearse a hard conversation. Many users treat it as a daily check-in or a place to decompress.

ChatGPT can be supportive, but it defaults to advice mode and adds disclaimers more often. It works, but it feels more clinical.

An important caveat for both: neither is a therapist or a crisis service. They can't assess risk, they can be wrong, and they don't replace professional help. Pi's warmth is a strength for low-stakes reflection, but it shouldn't be anyone's only support.

If companionship and character-driven chat is what you're after, [Character.AI](/reviews/character-ai-review-2023/) is another option. It's more about roleplay and personas, while Pi is one consistent, supportive personality.

## Memory and continuity

Pi keeps a running conversation instead of separate chat threads, so it feels continuous. You pick up where you left off, and it can refer to earlier parts of the conversation. That supports the "personal AI" idea, though its recall of older details is imperfect.

ChatGPT organizes conversations into separate threads. Custom Instructions let you tell it who you are and how you want replies formatted, which persists across chats. That's more practical for work, where you want separate threads for separate projects.

## Privacy

Both services store your conversations on their servers. ChatGPT lets you turn off chat history, which also keeps those conversations out of model training. Pi's appeal as a confidant means people often share personal details with it, so read Inflection's privacy policy and think about what you're comfortable storing. With either tool, avoid sharing sensitive personal, financial, or health information you wouldn't want kept.

## Pricing (approximate, as of October 2023)

- **Pi:** Free to use at the moment, with no paid tier.
- **ChatGPT:** Free tier with GPT-3.5. **ChatGPT Plus** is about $20/month and adds GPT-4, Advanced Data Analysis, DALL·E 3, voice, and priority access. OpenAI also offers an Enterprise plan for businesses.

Pi's price is hard to beat, but free products can change their business model later.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Pi if:**
- You want a conversational companion for reflection, journaling-style chats, or low-stakes emotional support.
- You like talking to an AI by voice and want short, natural back-and-forth.
- You want to learn about a topic through gentle, question-driven dialogue.
- You don't want to pay anything.

**Choose ChatGPT if:**
- You need real output: documents, code, analysis, summaries, or images.
- You want precise, structured answers to complex questions.
- You work with files and data.
- You're willing to pay about $20/month for GPT-4 and the Plus features.

**Use both if:** you want a work tool and a thinking partner. Many people end up with ChatGPT for tasks and Pi for talking things through. Since Pi is free, adding it costs nothing.

## Bottom line

Pi and ChatGPT aren't really competing for the same job. ChatGPT is the better assistant by almost every productivity measure. Pi is the better conversation partner, and it's the most pleasant AI to simply talk to that we've tried this year. Pick based on whether you want something done or someone to talk it through with.
