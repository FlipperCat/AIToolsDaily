Email clients have quietly turned into AI products. Two of the most talked-about — **Superhuman** and **Shortwave** — both promise to drag your inbox into the AI era, but they come at it from very different angles. Superhuman is the polished, speed-obsessed premium client built for people who treat their inbox like a sport. Shortwave is the AI-first reinvention of email from a team of ex-Google Inbox engineers.

If you're choosing between them in 2025, here's a clear-eyed comparison across the dimensions that actually matter. For the broader landscape, our overview of [AI tools for email management](/ai-for-email-management/) puts both in context.

## Quick Comparison

| Dimension | Superhuman | Shortwave |
|---|---|---|
| Core identity | Premium, speed-first email client | AI-first email reinvention |
| Email support | Gmail and Outlook | Gmail only |
| Standout feature | Blazing speed + keyboard shortcuts | Deep AI assistant + AI search |
| AI writing | Write/Improve with AI, instant replies | AI compose, tone control, drafts |
| AI summaries | Auto-summarize threads | Summarize threads and your whole inbox |
| Learning curve | Steep (shortcut-driven) | Gentle |
| Free tier | No | Yes |
| Approx. price | ~$30/user/month | Free; paid from ~$7–25/month |

## Speed and Interface

This is Superhuman's home turf. The entire product is engineered around making email *fast*. Every action has a keyboard shortcut, the UI responds instantly, and once you've memorized the commands you can triage a packed inbox in a fraction of the usual time. There's an onboarding session designed to teach you the shortcuts, and the whole experience feels meticulously crafted.

Shortwave is fast and modern too, but it doesn't worship speed the way Superhuman does. Its interface leans into bundling, AI panels, and a cleaner reading experience rather than raw keyboard velocity. If shaving seconds off every keystroke is your obsession, Superhuman is unmatched. If you'd rather the app do thinking for you than make you faster at clicking, Shortwave's design philosophy fits better.

**Edge:** Superhuman for raw speed; Shortwave for a calmer, AI-forward layout.

## AI Features

Both apps have leaned hard into AI, but their emphasis differs.

**Superhuman** treats AI as an accelerant bolted onto an already-fast client. You get AI-assisted writing (draft and improve replies in your own voice), instant one-line replies, automatic thread summaries, and smart categorization that splits your inbox so important mail surfaces first. It's tasteful and tightly integrated — AI that helps you move faster without taking over.

**Shortwave** treats AI as the centerpiece. Its assistant can search your inbox conversationally ("find the contract Sarah sent last quarter"), summarize entire threads or even your day's mail, draft replies with adjustable tone, and answer questions about your email history. The AI search in particular is a genuine differentiator — it understands intent rather than just matching keywords.

If you want AI as a quiet copilot, Superhuman. If you want AI as the main event — something you actively converse with — Shortwave pulls ahead. Either way, pairing these with solid prompting habits helps; our guide on [using ChatGPT for email writing](/tutorials/how-to-use-chatgpt-email-writing/) covers techniques that transfer to both.

**Edge:** Shortwave for depth and ambition of AI; Superhuman for polish and restraint.

## Email Platform Support

A practical dealbreaker for some: **Shortwave only works with Gmail.** If you're on Outlook, Microsoft 365, or a non-Google business domain, Shortwave is simply not an option in 2025.

**Superhuman supports both Gmail and Outlook**, which makes it the only choice of the two for Outlook-based teams and a safer pick for organizations that aren't fully on Google.

**Edge:** Superhuman, clearly, for anyone not living entirely in Gmail.

## Learning Curve and Onboarding

Superhuman is powerful but demanding. Its value is locked behind learning the keyboard shortcuts, and there's a real adjustment period before it feels faster than your old client. The guided onboarding helps, but you have to commit. People who don't lean into the shortcuts often feel they're overpaying for a pretty Gmail skin.

Shortwave is far gentler. The AI features are discoverable, the interface is familiar, and you get value on day one without memorizing anything. For most people, the time-to-benefit is much shorter.

**Edge:** Shortwave for ease; Superhuman rewards those willing to invest.

## Pricing and Value

Pricing is approximate and changes, so confirm current plans, but **as of early 2025**:

- **Superhuman:** roughly **$30/user per month** (cheaper billed annually), with business and enterprise tiers. There's no free plan — you're paying a premium for the experience.
- **Shortwave:** has a **free tier**, with paid plans starting around **$7/month** and climbing toward **$25/month** for the more capable AI and business features.

The gap is stark. Superhuman is a clear premium product with premium pricing and no free entry point. Shortwave lets you start free and pay only as you need more AI horsepower. For budget-conscious individuals and small teams, that difference alone can decide it. Teams weighing the spend may also want our roundup of [AI tools for executive assistants](/best-ai-tools-for-executive-assistants/), where inbox automation often earns its keep.

**Edge:** Shortwave on value and flexibility; Superhuman if money is no object and the experience justifies it.

## Who Each Is Best For

**Choose Superhuman if you:**

- Live in your inbox and want the fastest possible triage.
- Use Outlook, or a mix of Outlook and Gmail.
- Will actually learn and use keyboard shortcuts.
- Value a flawless, premium-feeling experience and can absorb the cost.

**Choose Shortwave if you:**

- Are on Gmail and want AI to be the star, not a side feature.
- Want conversational AI search across your email history.
- Prefer a gentle learning curve and a free starting point.
- Care about getting strong AI for a fraction of Superhuman's price.

## Which Should You Choose?

For most individuals and small teams in 2025, **Shortwave is the better-value pick** — assuming you're on Gmail. You get genuinely useful AI search, summaries, and drafting, a real free tier, and far lower paid pricing. The AI-first design feels like where email is heading, and you don't have to retrain your hands to benefit from it.

**Superhuman wins on two clear axes:** raw speed for keyboard power users, and platform support for anyone on Outlook. If your inbox is a high-volume battlefield and you'll commit to the shortcuts — or you simply can't use a Gmail-only app — Superhuman's polish is worth its premium. It's the better tool for the person whose entire job runs through email and who will use every shortcut it offers.

The honest summary: **Shortwave for AI depth and value, Superhuman for speed and Outlook support.** Both are strong; the right answer depends mostly on which email platform you're on and whether you want AI as a copilot or the captain.
