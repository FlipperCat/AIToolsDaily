For almost a year, the "should I pay $20 a month for an AI chatbot" question had one answer: ChatGPT Plus. Bard was free, often charming, and clearly a step behind GPT-4. That changed on February 8, when Google renamed Bard to Gemini and launched **Gemini Advanced**, a paid tier running its largest model, Gemini Ultra 1.0.

We have spent the days since switching between both. This is a snapshot as of late February 2024, and both products are moving fast, so treat the feature lists as a starting point rather than a permanent verdict.

## Quick comparison

| Dimension | Gemini Advanced | ChatGPT Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Price (approx., Feb 2024) | $19.99/mo via Google One AI Premium | $20/mo |
| Flagship model | Gemini Ultra 1.0 | GPT-4 (Turbo) |
| Free trial | 2 months | None |
| Image generation | Yes (Imagen 2) | Yes (DALL-E 3) |
| Image input | Yes | Yes (GPT-4 with vision) |
| Web access | Yes, via Google Search | Yes, via Bing browsing |
| Code execution / data analysis | Limited | Advanced Data Analysis (Python sandbox) |
| Custom assistants | No | GPTs and the GPT Store |
| Voice conversation | Android/iOS app (reads aloud) | Mobile voice chat |
| App integrations | Gmail, Docs, Drive, YouTube, Maps, Flights, Hotels | Plugins (fading), GPTs with Actions |
| Extras | 2 TB Google One storage | None |
| Usage limits | Not published | Capped GPT-4 messages per 3 hours |

## Model quality

Google's launch material claims Ultra beats GPT-4 on most published benchmarks. Treat that the way you would treat any vendor chart. In everyday use, the two feel like peers in a way Bard and GPT-4 never did.

Where Gemini Advanced impressed us: long, structured writing tasks, brainstorming, and anything that benefits from fresh information from Google Search. Explanations tend to be well organized with sensible headings, and the model is less prone to the terse "here are five bullet points" habit ChatGPT has developed.

Where ChatGPT Plus still leads: multi-step reasoning, careful instruction following, and code. When we asked both to refactor a messy Python script with specific constraints, GPT-4 respected every constraint on the first pass. Gemini Advanced produced clean code but ignored one requirement and needed a follow-up. On tricky logic puzzles, GPT-4 remains more reliable.

Gemini also still hedges and refuses more than it should on harmless requests. It is better than Bard was, but you will hit "I'm just a language model" walls that ChatGPT walked past long ago.

Worth knowing: Google announced Gemini 1.5 Pro on February 15 with a one million token context window. It is in limited preview for developers and enterprise, not inside Gemini Advanced. Do not buy the subscription expecting that model today.

## Features and tools

**ChatGPT Plus** is the more complete toolbox. Advanced Data Analysis lets you upload a spreadsheet and get real Python running against it, with charts you can download. DALL-E 3 produces the best text rendering of any mainstream image model. The vision feature handles screenshots, whiteboards, and photos of handwriting well. And GPTs, custom versions of ChatGPT with their own instructions and files, have quietly become the killer feature. The [GPT Store launched in January](/openai-gpt-store-launch-2024/) and already hosts thousands of them, from Canva to Consensus to niche study tools.

**Gemini Advanced** is thinner on tooling but smarter about integration. The Extensions system lets it read your Gmail, Docs, and Drive files when you reference them with an @ mention, pull YouTube transcripts, and check flights and hotels. Asking "summarize the thread with my accountant from last week" and getting a real answer is a different kind of useful than anything ChatGPT does out of the box. The "double-check" button, which highlights claims Google Search supports or contradicts, is genuinely helpful for research drafts.

Image generation with Imagen 2 is competent for photographic and illustrative prompts but behind DALL-E 3 on text in images and prompt adherence. There is also no equivalent of a Python sandbox, so data analysis means describing your spreadsheet rather than uploading it.

## Ecosystem and bundles

This is where the two diverge most. The Gemini Advanced subscription is really the Google One AI Premium plan: you get 2 TB of cloud storage that would cost about $10 a month on its own, plus Google promises Gemini features inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Meet are coming to this plan soon. If you already pay for Google storage, Gemini Advanced effectively costs you about $10 more a month.

ChatGPT Plus is a standalone subscription. OpenAI's ecosystem play is the GPT Store and the Team plan, not bundling. If you live in Microsoft 365, Copilot Pro is the comparable bundle; see our [Gemini Advanced vs Copilot Pro comparison](/compare/gemini-advanced-vs-copilot-pro-2024/) for that matchup.

## Limits and reliability

OpenAI caps GPT-4 usage on Plus. The exact number has shifted over time and currently sits around 40 messages every three hours. Heavy users hit it, especially when using vision or data analysis. OpenAI also announced a Memory feature on February 13, but it is only rolling out to a small test group.

Google has not published a message cap for Gemini Advanced, and we never hit one. Availability is the catch instead: Gemini Advanced is English-only at launch and not offered in every country. ChatGPT Plus is available more widely and in more languages.

Both services have had outages. ChatGPT's are more publicized because more people notice.

## Privacy

Neither plan is a privacy haven. Google uses Gemini conversations to improve its models unless you turn off Gemini Apps Activity, which also disables some personalization. OpenAI trains on consumer conversations unless you disable chat history or use the Team plan. If your work involves sensitive client data, read the data policies carefully before pasting anything in. Our [ChatGPT review](/reviews/chatgpt-review/) covers OpenAI's settings in detail.

## Pricing reality check

At list price they are a dollar apart. The real differences:

- **Gemini Advanced** gives a two-month free trial, bundles 2 TB of storage, and promises Workspace integration on the same bill.
- **ChatGPT Plus** has no trial and no bundle, but you can cancel after a month, and a free tier with GPT-3.5 remains available if you want to downgrade.

Prices are approximate as of February 2024 and will change.

## Which should you choose?

**Pick ChatGPT Plus if:**

- You write or debug code regularly. GPT-4 is still the stronger coding partner.
- You analyze data and want a real Python sandbox.
- You want custom assistants (GPTs) for repeat workflows.
- You need the best image generation with legible text.

**Pick Gemini Advanced if:**

- Your work lives in Gmail, Docs, and Drive, and you want an assistant that can see them.
- You already pay for Google One storage, which makes the upgrade cheap.
- You want current web information pulled from Google Search by default.
- You want to try before buying. Two free months is a real offer.

**If you are undecided:** start the Gemini Advanced trial, run your actual weekly tasks through it for a month, and keep ChatGPT's free tier open beside it. If you find yourself opening ChatGPT free to get a better answer, you know where your $20 should go.

A year ago we wrote that [Bard was not a ChatGPT replacement](/compare/bard-vs-chatgpt-2023/). Gemini Advanced is. It is not better across the board, but for the first time the choice depends on your workflow rather than on which model is smarter.
