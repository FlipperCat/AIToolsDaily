Pictory and Lumen5 both promise the same thing: paste in text, get back a watchable video with stock footage, captions, and music. Both are popular with bloggers and marketers who want video without filming anything. After a few weeks using both for blog repurposing and social clips, we found they are built for different jobs.

The short version: **Pictory is better at working with video you already have.** **Lumen5 is better at producing polished, on-brand videos from written content at team scale.** The details below should tell you which matters more for you.

For the full single-tool breakdowns, see our [Pictory review](/reviews/11-pictory-review/) and [Lumen5 review](/reviews/lumen5-review-2023/).

| Edit video by editing transcript | Yes | No (scene-based editing) |
| Auto highlight clips from long videos | Yes | No |

## Text-to-Video Quality

Both tools take a script or article URL, summarize it into scenes, and match each scene with stock footage.

**Lumen5** does this step a little better. Its templates are more designed, its text animations look cleaner, and the result usually looks like a branded social video with little extra work. The summarization step highlights key sentences and lets you choose which to keep. That gives you control without making you rewrite everything.

**Pictory** does a solid job but its default output looks a bit more generic. Stock matches are fine for broad topics, but for niche subjects both tools pull loosely related footage. You'll swap visuals by hand either way.

**Winner: Lumen5**, for polish straight out of the generator.

## Editing Existing Video

This is where the tools differ most.

**Pictory** can take a long recording, such as a webinar, Zoom call, or podcast video, transcribe it, and let you **edit by deleting words from the transcript**. It can also suggest highlight clips automatically. If you record long-form content and want short clips, this is a big time-saver. The approach is similar to what [Descript](/reviews/04-descript-review/) does, but packaged for repurposing rather than full production.

**Lumen5** is built around scenes. You can upload your own clips and arrange them, but it isn't designed to cut a 60-minute recording into a 60-second highlight. It's a video *builder*, not a video *editor*.

**Winner: Pictory**, clearly. If repurposing recordings is your main use case, this alone decides it.

## Voiceovers and Audio

Both offer AI text-to-speech voiceovers and a background music library.

- **Pictory** offers a choice of AI voices and lets you upload your own recording and sync it to scenes. The better voices sound acceptable for explainer content, though they won't fool anyone listening closely.
- **Lumen5** also has text-to-speech voices and music, and its timing tools make it easy to match scene length to narration.

Neither tool's voices match a dedicated voice generator or a real human read. For anything customer-facing, recording your own voiceover is still worth the effort.

**Winner: Tie.** Pictory has a slight edge if you bring your own audio.

## Branding and Team Features

**Lumen5** is aimed at marketing teams. Brand kits with logos, fonts, colors, and custom templates let several people produce videos that look consistent. Higher tiers add more brand control, larger stock libraries, and team collaboration. For an agency or in-house content team producing many videos a week, that consistency matters.

**Pictory** has brand kits too, with logo, colors, and intro/outro clips, but its team features are more basic. It works best for a single creator or a small team.

**Winner: Lumen5.**

## Captions and Social Formats

Both tools auto-generate captions and export in landscape, square, and vertical formats for YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok.

**Pictory's** captions come from its transcription, so they're usually accurate for recorded speech, and you can restyle them. **Lumen5** handles on-screen text as part of its scene design, which looks good but is less suited to word-for-word subtitles on talking-head footage.

If captioned clips of real speakers are your main output, a dedicated subtitle editor like [VEED](/reviews/veed-io-review-2023/) is also worth a look.

**Winner: Pictory** for spoken-word captions. **Lumen5** for designed text overlays.

## Pricing (Approximate, as of October 2023)

Prices change often and annual billing is noticeably cheaper than monthly, so treat these as rough ranges:

**Pictory**
- **Free trial:** a few video projects to test the workflow.
- **Standard:** roughly $19/month billed annually, around $23 monthly.
- **Premium:** roughly $39/month annually, around $47 monthly. Adds more video minutes, more AI voices, and extra features.
- **Teams:** higher-priced plan for groups.

**Lumen5**
- **Community (free):** watermarked videos at lower resolution. Fine for testing.
- **Basic:** roughly $19/month annually, around $29 monthly. Removes the watermark.
- **Starter:** roughly $59/month annually. Adds brand kits and more media.
- **Professional:** roughly $149/month annually. Aimed at teams that need custom templates and bigger stock libraries.
- **Enterprise:** custom pricing.

At the entry level the two cost about the same. Lumen5 gets expensive faster as you move up to the team and brand features that are its main advantage.

## Limitations of Both

- **Stock footage feels generic.** Viewers notice when every video uses the same handshake and laptop clips.
- **AI summaries miss nuance.** Both tools sometimes pick the wrong sentences from an article. Review every scene.
- **Rendering takes time** for longer videos, and previews aren't always accurate.
- **Neither replaces a real editor** for anything more ambitious than social clips and explainers.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Pictory if:**
- You record webinars, podcasts, interviews, or Zoom calls and want short clips from them.
- You like editing video by editing a transcript.
- You're a solo creator or small team watching costs.
- Accurate captions on spoken content matter more than visual polish.

**Choose Lumen5 if:**
- Your main input is written content, such as blog posts, press releases, or announcements.
- You need many on-brand videos from multiple team members.
- You want polished templates and don't want to fiddle with design.
- You can afford the mid or upper tiers where its brand features are.

**Our pick:** for most bloggers and solo creators, **Pictory** offers more value because it handles both text-to-video *and* repurposing existing recordings. For marketing teams focused on brand consistency across lots of social video, **Lumen5** is worth the higher price.

Either way, start with the free trial or free plan, run the same blog post through both, and compare the results. That 30-minute test will tell you more than any feature list.
