## Practical Comparison

**Ease of Use**: Evernote is dramatically simpler. You can be productive in minutes. Notion requires hours of learning. This is Evernote's greatest strength - you open it and immediately understand what to do.

**Note Capture**: Evernote's web clipper is exceptional, automatically capturing web content with excellent formatting. Notion has a web clipper but it's less refined. For researchers and information gatherers, Evernote's capture is unmatched.

**Organization**: Notion offers unlimited flexibility with databases, relations, and custom views. Evernote offers simplicity with notebooks and tags. This fundamental difference defines everything else about the platforms.

**Search**: Evernote's search is significantly faster and more intuitive. It finds notes instantly. Notion's search works but is slower with large workspaces. For someone who relies on search to retrieve information, Evernote is superior.

**Databases**: Notion's database capabilities are revolutionary, allowing you to treat data like a spreadsheet with advanced filtering, relations, and rollups. Evernote has none of this - it's purely hierarchical note storage.

**Collaboration**: Notion excels at real-time team collaboration on projects. Evernote's collaboration is basic and more suited to sharing notes than collaborative workspaces.

**Mobile**: Evernote's mobile apps are best-in-class, with full functionality on iPhone and Android. Notion's mobile is very good but not as refined and slower on weaker connections.

**OCR Quality**: Evernote's OCR is the best available, accurately reading handwriting and scanned documents. Notion's OCR is basic. If you scan documents or use handwriting, Evernote is essential.

**Performance**: Evernote is fast and responsive. Notion can slow with large databases and many pages. If responsiveness is crucial, Evernote wins.

**Price Value**: Notion's unlimited free tier with full database access is exceptional value. Evernote's free tier is more limited but still useful.

## Deep Dive: When Each Excels

**Evernote excels with**: Research workflows (capturing web content and organizing it), personal knowledge management (simple filing of information), students taking notes, professionals archiving documents, and anyone who captures more information than they create new content.

**Notion excels with**: Building personal systems (databases tracking habits, projects, books), team knowledge bases, project management, managing databases of contacts or content, and anyone building a customized workspace.

## Feature-Specific Details

**Web Clipper**: Evernote's web clipper is specifically optimized for capture - just click and save. Notion requires more steps and web content doesn't capture as cleanly.

**Offline Access**: Evernote offers strong offline access on desktop. Notion has limited offline functionality, requiring internet for sync.

**Import/Export**: Both allow exporting notes, but Evernote's export options are more comprehensive.

**Templates**: Notion has hundreds of templates for all kinds of workflows. Evernote has none.

**Integrations**: Notion integrates with 1000+ apps via Zapier. Evernote has fewer direct integrations.

**API Access**: Notion's official API is powerful. Evernote's API exists but is less popular.

**File Storage**: Evernote allows attaching and storing files directly. Notion encourages using external storage and linking.

## Use Case: The Research Researcher

Sarah is a researcher who reads 20 papers per week and needs to capture key quotes, link them to sources, and later retrieve them. For Sarah, Evernote is clearly superior. The web clipper captures cleanly, OCR reads PDFs, and search finds information instantly.

## Use Case: The Personal Productivity Builder

James wants to build a system tracking his goals, projects, books he's reading, contacts, and habits in one place with custom views. For James, Notion is clearly superior. He can build databases with relations between entities, creating a custom productivity system.

## Hybrid Approach

Many professionals use both: Evernote for capturing and archiving information, Notion for organizing and building databases of information. Evernote is the capture tool; Notion is the dashboard.

## Final Verdict

**Choose Notion** if you need a flexible all-in-one workspace, plan complex projects, want team collaboration, value unlimited customization, or want to build a custom productivity system. It's the powerful all-in-one platform for workspace builders.

**Choose Evernote** if you want simple, fast, reliable note-taking with excellent OCR and search, prefer minimalist design, use web clipping frequently, need offline access, or prioritize capture over organization. It's the specialist note-taking tool for information hoarders.

**Best Strategy**: Evernote for personal note capture, research, and information archival. Notion for teams, projects, and building custom workspaces. Many professionals use both platforms for complementary strengths - Evernote for intake, Notion for organization.
