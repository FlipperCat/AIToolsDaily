AI meeting assistants went from novelty to near-mandatory over the past year. Instead of scribbling notes while half-listening, you let a bot join the call, transcribe everything, and hand you a summary with action items. Two of the most popular options are **Fathom** and **Otter.ai**—and while they overlap, they're built around different philosophies.

Fathom is laser-focused on recording and summarizing video meetings (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams). Otter.ai is a broader transcription platform that handles meetings but also live conversations, interviews, and audio you upload. Here's how they actually compare in 2024, and which one you should pick.

## Quick Comparison

| Dimension | Fathom | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Core focus | Video meeting recording + summaries | General transcription + meetings |
| Best at | Clean call summaries & highlights | Live transcription & searchable notes |
| Free tier | Generous (recording + summaries) | Yes, with monthly minute limits |
| Live transcription | During the meeting | Real-time, including in-person via app |
| Summaries | Strong, action-item focused | Good, plus "Otter chat" Q&A |
| CRM integrations | Solid (sales-oriented) | Available on higher tiers |
| Ideal user | Sales teams, founders, call-heavy roles | Students, journalists, broad note-taking |

## Transcription Accuracy

Both are good, and both stumble in the same places: heavy accents, crosstalk, and jargon-dense calls. In clean conditions with clear audio, accuracy on both is high enough that you'll only occasionally need to fix a word.

Otter has a longer history as a pure transcription product, and it shows in edge cases—it tends to handle messier audio and in-person recording slightly more gracefully, partly because real-time transcription has always been its headline feature. Fathom's transcription is very solid for digital meetings, which is where it's designed to live. If your use case is mostly Zoom and Meet calls, you won't notice a meaningful gap. If you transcribe in-person conversations or uploaded audio, Otter has the edge.

## Summaries and Notes

This is where the philosophies diverge.

**Fathom** is built to make the *summary* the product. After a call, it generates a clean recap with key moments, and it's strong at surfacing action items and decisions. You can also clip highlights during the meeting with a click, which is great for pulling out a single answer to share later. The summaries feel tuned for "what happened and what do I need to do."

**Otter** gives you a summary too, plus a fully searchable transcript and a Q&A feature that lets you ask questions about the meeting after the fact. If your value is in *searching and revisiting* what was said—across many meetings—Otter's library and search are more developed.

## Integrations and Workflow

Fathom leans into sales and customer-facing workflows, with strong connections to CRMs so call notes and recordings flow into your pipeline automatically. If your meetings are sales calls, demos, or customer interviews, that integration story is a real advantage.

Otter integrates with calendars and collaboration tools and offers automatic meeting joining, with deeper integrations gated to higher tiers. It's more of a general productivity citizen than a sales-specific one.

Both auto-join scheduled meetings and both let you share recordings and notes with teammates, so the basic collaboration flow is covered either way. ## Pricing

Pricing below is approximate and as of mid-2024—always confirm on the current pages, because tiers shift often.

**Fathom** is notably generous on the free side: you can record and get summaries for individual use without paying, which is a big part of its popularity. Paid plans (roughly the mid-teens to ~$30 per user per month) add advanced summary features, unlimited highlights, and team capabilities.

**Otter.ai** offers a free tier with a monthly transcription-minute cap, a Pro plan in the ~$10–17 per month range with more minutes and features, and Business plans (~$20–30 per user per month) that add admin controls and deeper integrations. Because Otter meters by minutes, heavy transcription users climb tiers faster.

The headline: if you want the most capable *free* meeting recorder, Fathom is hard to beat. If you need lots of transcription minutes and search across a large archive, price out Otter's tiers against your actual volume.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Fathom if** you're in a call-heavy, customer-facing role—sales, founders, consultants. Its free tier is excellent, its summaries are action-oriented, and its CRM integrations make it feel purpose-built for turning conversations into pipeline. If "join my Zoom calls and give me a clean recap" is 90% of what you want, Fathom nails it.

**Choose Otter.ai if** your needs go beyond video meetings: live in-person transcription, interviews, uploaded audio, or a searchable archive you'll revisit constantly. Students, journalists, and researchers in particular get more from Otter's transcription-first design and library.

For many people the honest answer is "start with Fathom's free tier for meetings, and add Otter if you need broader transcription."
