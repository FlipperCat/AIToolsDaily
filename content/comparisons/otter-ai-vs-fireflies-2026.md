## Practical Comparison

**Accuracy**: Both are 95%+ accurate; Otter.ai slightly better overall with cleaner transcripts. Fireflies.ai's accuracy is excellent but sometimes has more formatting inconsistencies.

**Ease of Use**: Otter.ai is more intuitive out of the box. You can connect your calendar, start recording, and transcripts appear. Fireflies.ai requires slightly more setup but offers more customization.

**Integration**: Otter.ai integrates with more platforms (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Slack). Fireflies.ai has strong integrations but slightly fewer overall connections available.

**Search**: Fireflies.ai's search is more powerful for meeting intelligence. The "Ask" feature (ask questions about your meeting) is superior. Otter.ai's search is good but more basic keyword-based.

**AI Features**: Fireflies.ai's AI is more specialized for meeting analysis and intelligence extraction. Otter.ai's AI features are broader (transcription, note generation) but less specialized for meetings.

**Real-time**: Otter.ai better for live meeting transcription. It can transcribe as you're speaking, with updates in real-time. Fireflies.ai transcribes but less in real-time.

**Summaries**: Otter.ai generates better summaries on average. They're more concise and actionable. Fireflies.ai's summaries are good but sometimes verbose.

**Action Items**: Both automatically extract action items; Otter.ai slightly more reliable at identifying who's responsible. Fireflies.ai's action items need more manual verification sometimes.

**Pricing**: Otter.ai is more affordable, especially at the free tier (600 monthly minutes vs Fireflies' limited free tier). This makes Otter.ai better for trying before buying.

**Team Collaboration**: Fireflies.ai edges ahead with team features. Multiple team members can organize and share transcripts more smoothly. Otter.ai's team features are more limited.
