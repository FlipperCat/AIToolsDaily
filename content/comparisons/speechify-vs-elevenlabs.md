Speechify and ElevenLabs both live under the "AI voice" umbrella, which leads a lot of people to compare them directly. But they are built for opposite jobs, and picking the wrong one is the most common mistake here.

Speechify is a listening tool: it reads content you already have out loud. ElevenLabs is a voice studio: it generates and clones voices for content you are making. Choose Speechify to consume text hands-free; choose ElevenLabs to produce voiceovers, narration, or app audio.

## Quick Comparison

| Factor | Speechify | ElevenLabs |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Primary job** | Read content aloud to you | Generate and clone voices |
| **Best for** | Reading, accessibility, learning | Production audio and developers |
| **Voice quality** | Very good for listening | Winner for expressive output |
| **Voice cloning** | Available, not the focus | Core strength |
| **Where you use it** | App, browser extension | Web app, Reader app, API |
| **Languages** | Many, focused on reading | Strong multilingual output |
| **API** | Limited | Robust, developer-friendly |
| **Pricing model** | Consumer subscription | Character/credit tiers |

**TL;DR:** Speechify for consuming text. ElevenLabs for creating audio.

## What Each Tool Actually Does

### Speechify

Speechify turns text into speech so you can listen instead of read. Point it at an article, a PDF, an email, or an ebook, and it reads the content aloud at whatever speed you set, often well above natural talking pace once you are used to it.

It lives where your reading lives: a mobile app, a desktop app, and a browser extension that can read the page you are on. There is optical character recognition too, so you can snap a photo of printed text and have it read back. The whole product is oriented around accessibility, studying, and getting through a reading backlog. If that sounds familiar, our older [Speechify review](/reviews/speechify-review-2023/) covers the reading experience in depth.

### ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is a generation platform. You give it a script and it produces audio in a chosen or cloned voice, with a focus on expressiveness and realism. Beyond basic text-to-speech, it offers voice cloning, dubbing across languages, long-form "Projects" for narrating books and articles, a shared Voice Library, and a developer API. It also ships a Reader app for listening, but its center of gravity is clearly creation.

## Voice Quality

For finished, published audio, ElevenLabs is the stronger performer. Its best voices carry emotion, pacing, and emphasis that hold up in a video or a podcast, which is why so many creators reach for it. See how it stacks up against rivals in [ElevenLabs vs Murf](/compare/elevenlabs-vs-murf/) and [ElevenLabs vs PlayHT](/compare/elevenlabs-vs-playht/).

Speechify's voices are genuinely good and have improved a lot, including some recognizable celebrity options, but they are tuned for comfortable long-session listening rather than dramatic delivery. That is the right call for its use case; you do not want theatrical narration when you are plowing through a research paper.

## Voice Cloning

Cloning is where the gap is widest. ElevenLabs treats it as a headline feature, with fast instant cloning from a short sample and professional cloning from more audio for a closer match. If reproducing a specific voice is important, this is the platform. Our [AI voice cloning guide](/ai-voice-cloning-guide-2024/) explains the workflow and the ethics.

Speechify offers voice choices and some cloning, but it is not why people subscribe. Do not choose Speechify expecting a cloning studio.

## Workflow and Integration

The tools fit into different parts of your day.

- **Speechify** integrates with your reading. The browser extension and mobile app mean you can start listening to whatever is in front of you with one tap. It is a consumption layer over content you did not create.
- **ElevenLabs** integrates with your production pipeline. The API and Projects features mean it slots into content workflows and software, generating audio that becomes part of something you ship.

If you are a developer, this is nearly decisive: ElevenLabs' API is built for programmatic use, while Speechify's strength is its consumer apps.

## Pricing

Both use different models, and pricing is approximate as of early 2025.

- **Speechify:** A free tier with standard voices, plus a Premium subscription in the low-teens per month (billed annually) that unlocks higher-quality voices and faster speeds. Studio/voiceover plans are separate.
- **ElevenLabs:** A free tier with a monthly character allowance, then paid tiers scaling by characters generated, roughly from a few dollars per month at entry level up to $99 per month for Pro, with higher business tiers above that.

The lesson: match the pricing model to the job. A heavy reader gets more from Speechify's flat consumer plan; a creator who generates a lot of audio should budget around ElevenLabs' character tiers.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Speechify if you:**

- Want to listen to articles, PDFs, emails, and books hands-free
- Use text-to-speech for accessibility, studying, or a reading backlog
- Value a polished app and browser extension over production features
- Care more about comfortable listening than expressive performance

**Choose ElevenLabs if you:**

- Produce voiceovers, narration, podcasts, or app audio
- Need voice cloning or multilingual dubbing
- Are a developer who wants speech generation via an API
- Want the most expressive, production-grade voices available

For most people the decision is not close once you name the job. If you are consuming text, Speechify is purpose-built for it. If you are creating audio, ElevenLabs is the studio. Plenty of professionals happily pay for both, because they solve genuinely different problems.
