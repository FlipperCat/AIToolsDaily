Midjourney has released V7, their most advanced image generation model yet, setting a new standard for AI-generated photorealism.

## Major Improvements in V7

### Photorealism
V7 produces images that are virtually indistinguishable from photographs:
- Accurate skin textures and pores
- Realistic lighting and shadows
- Proper physics for reflections and refractions
- Natural environmental details

### Text Rendering
One of AI art's biggest weaknesses has been text. V7 addresses this:
- Accurate spelling in most cases
- Consistent font rendering
- Better integration of text into scenes
- Support for multiple languages

### New Style Controls
V7 introduces granular control over aesthetics:
- **Style Slider** - Adjust from photographic to artistic
- **Detail Control** - Fine-tune complexity level
- **Color Palette** - Specify dominant colors
- **Era Setting** - Match specific time periods

### Consistency Features
Generate coherent series and variations:
- Character consistency across images
- Scene continuation
- Style locking for brand consistency

## How to Access V7

V7 is available now to all Midjourney subscribers:
- Type `--v 7` at the end of any prompt
- Or set V7 as default with `/settings`
