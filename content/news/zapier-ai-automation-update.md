Zapier has unveiled a major platform update featuring AI-powered automation creation, making complex workflows accessible to everyone.

## New AI Features

### Natural Language Zap Creation
Describe what you want in plain English:
> "When I get a new lead in HubSpot, add them to my Mailchimp list and create a follow-up task in Asana"

Zapier's AI:
1. Identifies the apps needed
2. Creates the trigger and actions
3. Maps the fields automatically
4. Suggests error handling

### Smart Field Mapping
AI now predicts which fields should connect:
- Name to Name (obvious)
- "Primary Contact" to "Customer Name" (semantic)
- Date reformatting handled automatically
- Address parsing and standardization

### Error Prevention
AI analyzes your Zaps for potential issues:
- Missing required fields
- Data type mismatches
- Rate limit risks
- Circular automation warnings

### Optimization Suggestions
Receive AI recommendations to:
- Reduce task usage
- Improve reliability
- Add useful filters
- Consolidate redundant Zaps
