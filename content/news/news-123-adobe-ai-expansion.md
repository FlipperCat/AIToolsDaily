Adobe announced major AI expansions across its entire Creative Cloud suite, positioning itself as the leader in AI-assisted creative work. New features in Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and After Effects transform how designers and creators work.

## Major New Features

**Generative Fill in Illustrator:**
Illustrator gets a generative fill tool matching Photoshop's capabilities. Describe what you want and Illustrator generates vector shapes matching your description. Creates native, editable vectors—not rasterized images.

Impact: Designers can quickly generate complex vector shapes, saving hours in manual drawing.

**AI Video Editing in Premiere Pro:**
Automatic scene detection, intelligent cuts, color grading suggestions. The system:
- Detects scene changes automatically
- Suggests optimal cut points
- Recommends color grade based on scene
- Removes silence and awkward pauses
- Auto-generates captions

**Generative Audio:**
Create voiceovers, music, and sound effects via description:
- "Upbeat electronic background music"
- "Female voiceover in professional tone"
- "Subtle ambient background noise"

Built on partnerships with audio AI companies. Licenses original music and audio.

**Smart Asset Management:**
Firefly (Adobe's generative model) now:
- Auto-tags assets by content
- Suggests related assets across projects
- Recommends reusable elements
- Organizes brand assets automatically

Saves hours in asset organization.

**Enhanced Image Enhancement:**
AI automatically:
- Removes unwanted objects
- Fixes lighting issues
- Improves composition
- Suggests crops and framing

**Intelligent Layout Generation:**
For design projects, AI suggests:
- Layout options
- Typography pairing
- Color schemes
- Asset sizing and positioning

Accelerates design exploration phase.

## Integration Points

**Across Creative Cloud:**
- Photoshop: Enhanced object removal, generative fill, style transfer
- Illustrator: Vector generation, design assistance
- Premiere Pro: Automatic video editing, effects suggestion
- After Effects: Motion graphics AI assistance
- InDesign: Layout intelligence, design suggestions
- XD: UX/UI generation from wireframes

**Cloud-Integrated Workflow:**
All features sync across apps. Start in Photoshop, refine in Illustrator, finalize in Premiere Pro—all using consistent AI assistance.

## Pricing and Availability

**Included in Creative Cloud subscriptions** - No additional cost for existing subscribers.

**Free Generative Credits:** 100 monthly generative credits included (roughly 100 small edits or 10-15 large generations).

**Premium Generative Credits:** Additional credits available for purchase ($9.99/month for 1000 credits).

## Privacy and Copyright Considerations

Adobe trained Firefly on:
- Adobe Stock imagery (licensed)
- Public domain works
- Works where creators opted in
- NOT crawling web indiscriminately

This addresses copyright concerns plaguing other AI image tools. Works generated retain Adobe commercial license.
