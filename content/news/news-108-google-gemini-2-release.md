Google has released Gemini 2.0, its next-generation AI model, with unprecedented integration across the company's product ecosystem.

## Universal Integration

Unlike previous rollouts, Gemini 2.0 launches simultaneously across:

**Google Search**: AI Overviews become the default for most queries, with Gemini 2.0 providing comprehensive answers with citations.

**Workspace**: Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Slides gain advanced AI features including automated report generation, email drafting, and data analysis.

**Android**: Gemini 2.0 powers system-wide AI assistance, accessible from any app or screen.

**YouTube**: AI-powered video summarization, chapter generation, and interactive Q&A about video content.

## Technical Capabilities

Google reports Gemini 2.0 achieves:
- 1 million token context window
- Native code execution
- Real-time multimodal understanding
- 50% faster inference than Gemini 1.5 Pro

## Enterprise Features

Google Workspace Enterprise receives additional capabilities:
- Meeting AI that takes notes and generates action items
- Project management AI that tracks tasks across tools
- Custom AI agents for organization-specific workflows

## Pricing

Consumer features remain free with Google One subscriptions. Business plans start at $20 per user per month. Enterprise pricing requires consultation.

## Competitive Positioning

Google emphasized its unique advantages in AI infrastructure and data. "No other company can offer AI at this scale and integration," said Sundar Pichai. The launch puts pressure on Microsoft's Copilot and standalone AI tools to demonstrate comparable integration.
