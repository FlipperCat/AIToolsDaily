Google has completed the rollout of Gemini AI across its entire Workspace suite, bringing AI assistance to over 3 billion users.

## New AI Features by App

### Gmail
- **Smart Compose 2.0** - Full email drafting from brief prompts
- **Email Summarization** - Condense long threads instantly
- **Action Extraction** - Automatic to-do list from emails
- **Tone Adjustment** - Rewrite for different audiences

### Google Docs
- **Help Me Write** - Generate content from outlines
- **Smart Formatting** - Auto-structure documents
- **Citation Assistant** - Find and format references
- **Translation** - Real-time document translation

### Google Sheets
- **Formula Generation** - Natural language to formulas
- **Data Analysis** - Ask questions about your data
- **Chart Recommendations** - Smart visualization suggestions
- **Anomaly Detection** - Automatic error flagging

### Google Slides
- **Presentation Generator** - Create decks from documents
- **Image Suggestions** - AI-recommended visuals
- **Speaker Notes** - Auto-generate talking points
- **Design Polish** - One-click professional styling

### Google Meet
- **Live Captions** - 100+ language support
- **Meeting Summary** - Automatic notes and action items
- **Translation** - Real-time spoken translation
- **Background Enhancement** - AI-powered video quality

## Pricing Changes

Google has restructured Workspace pricing to include Gemini:

| Plan | Price | Gemini Features |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Business Starter | $7.20/user/mo | Basic AI |
| Business Standard | $14.40/user/mo | Full AI |
| Business Plus | $21.60/user/mo | Advanced AI + Security |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited + API |

Existing customers get a 6-month grace period before the new pricing takes effect.
