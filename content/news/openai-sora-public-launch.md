OpenAI has publicly launched Sora, its text-to-video AI model, after months of limited testing with creators and safety researchers.

## What Sora Can Do

### Video Generation
- Generate up to 60-second videos from text
- 1080p resolution
- Various aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1)
- Consistent characters and physics

### New Capabilities
- **Image-to-video** - Animate still images
- **Video extension** - Extend existing clips
- **Video editing** - Modify specific elements
- **Storyboard mode** - Control scenes individually

## Safety Measures

OpenAI has implemented safeguards:
- Content moderation on all prompts
- No photorealistic humans without consent
- Watermarking on all outputs
- C2PA metadata for provenance
- No CSAM, violence, or election content

## Limitations

Current restrictions:
- No real person likenesses
- Audio generation separate (coming soon)
- Some physics inconsistencies
- Queue times during peak hours

## What This Means

Sora's launch democratizes video production. Marketing teams, content creators, and filmmakers now have access to capabilities that required massive budgets. Expect rapid adoption—and ongoing debates about authenticity and disclosure.
