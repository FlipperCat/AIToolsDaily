Anthropic has launched Claude 4, emphasizing breakthrough reasoning capabilities combined with the company's signature focus on AI safety and alignment.

## Key Improvements

**Constitutional AI 2.0**: Claude 4 introduces an advanced version of Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach, providing more nuanced ethical reasoning while reducing over-cautious responses that frustrated users.

**400K Context Window**: Claude 4 expands its context window to 400K tokens, maintaining its lead in document processing capabilities.

**Artifact Execution**: Building on Claude 3.5's Artifacts feature, Claude 4 can now execute code, run simulations, and create interactive applications directly within conversations.

**Enterprise Controls**: New features allow organizations to customize Claude's behavior within safety boundaries, addressing a key enterprise concern.

## Three Model Tiers

Anthropic introduced three versions:
- **Claude 4 Opus**: Most capable model for complex reasoning
- **Claude 4 Sonnet**: Balanced performance and cost
- **Claude 4 Haiku**: Fast, efficient for simple tasks

## Safety Leadership

Anthropic emphasized its continued investment in AI safety, announcing a new $100 million safety research initiative. The company claims Claude 4 exhibits improved resistance to jailbreaks while providing more helpful responses for legitimate use cases.

## API Pricing

Claude 4 Sonnet is priced at $15 per million input tokens and $45 per million output tokens. Opus pricing will be announced at general availability.

## Availability

Claude 4 Sonnet is available immediately on claude.ai and through the API. Opus will launch in April 2026.
