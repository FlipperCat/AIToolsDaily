## 1. Elicit

### Overview
Elicit is an AI research assistant designed specifically for literature review. It finds, summarizes, and synthesizes research papers using advanced AI language models.

### Key Features
- Literature review automation
- Paper search and ranking
- Key findings extraction
- Effect size analysis
- Citation network visualization
- Evidence synthesis
- Table generation from papers
- Custom paper analysis

### Pricing
- Free: 50 papers/month
- Pro: $12/month (unlimited papers)
- Teams: Custom pricing

### Pros & Cons
**Pros:**
- Specifically designed for research
- Excellent synthesis capability
- Effect size analysis helpful
- Good free tier
- Great documentation
- Research-focused features
- Strong academic partnerships

**Cons:**
- Smaller corpus than general search
- May miss recent papers
- Requires structured research questions
- Limited to specific domains
- Learning curve present

### Best For
Academic researchers, PhD students, evidence synthesis projects, and systematic reviews. Perfect for literature review automation.

## Conclusion

AI research tools in 2026 have transformed literature review from weeks of manual searching to hours of AI-assisted synthesis. No single tool does everything—the most effective researchers combine 3-4 tools based on their specific research needs.

For systematic literature reviews, combine Elicit with Consensus. For exploratory research, Perplexity and Connected Papers work well. For understanding complex papers, SciSpace and Claude/ChatGPT excel.

The trend in 2026 is toward more specialized tools rather than general-purpose options. Rather than using ChatGPT for all research, successful researchers use specialized research tools (Elicit, Consensus) for their specific task and use general AI for synthesis and explanation.

Start with free tiers of multiple tools to understand your needs, then invest in the 2-3 tools matching your research workflow. The productivity gains from effective AI research tools are enormous, reducing research time by 50-70% while improving quality.
