## Marketing and Menus

### ChatGPT for Restaurant Marketing

Write everything customers see:

**Menu descriptions:**
> "Write appetizing menu descriptions for these dishes: [list dishes]. Make them appealing without being overly flowery. Include key ingredients."

**Social media:**
> "Create 10 Instagram captions for a Italian restaurant. Mix posts about dishes, behind-the-scenes, and seasonal specials. Include relevant hashtags."

**Event promotions:**
> "Write promotional copy for our Valentine's Day prix fixe menu. Include: menu highlights, price, reservation info. Make it romantic but not cheesy."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Canva for Visual Marketing

Design without a designer:

- Daily specials graphics
- Event flyers
- Social media posts
- Menu inserts

**Cost:** Free / $13/mo Pro

### Popmenu

Digital presence:

- AI-powered website
- Online ordering
- Marketing automation
- Menu management

All-in-one digital presence.

**Cost:** Starting $149/mo

## Restaurant-Specific Tips

### Menu Engineering with AI

Ask ChatGPT:
> "Based on these menu items and their food costs [provide data], which items should I promote more prominently? Which should I consider removing or repricing?"

### Handling Bad Reviews

Don't ignore them. AI can help draft responses that:
- Acknowledge the concern
- Stay professional
- Offer resolution
- Show you care

### Social Media Consistency

Batch create content:
> "Give me 30 days of Instagram post ideas for a family pizza restaurant. Mix educational (how pizza is made), promotional (specials), and engaging (questions, polls)."

## The Restaurant Reality

AI won't fix:
- Food quality
- Staff attitude
- Cleanliness
- Location issues

AI can help with:
- Marketing efficiency
- Operational organization
- Customer communication
- Time savings

Focus AI on back-office tasks. Focus yourself on the customer experience.
