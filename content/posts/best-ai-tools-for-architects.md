## Concept & Visualization

### Midjourney

Rapid concept exploration:

- Generate hundreds of concepts in hours
- Explore styles before committing
- Client presentations
- Design inspiration

**Prompt examples:**
> "modern minimalist house, concrete and glass, cantilevered design, forest setting, architectural photography --ar 16:9 --v 6"

> "adaptive reuse warehouse to residential lofts, exposed brick, steel beams, large industrial windows, natural light --ar 3:2"

Midjourney won't design your building, but it'll help you explore directions faster than sketching.

**Cost:** $10/mo Basic, $30/mo Standard

### Lumion

Real-time visualization:

- Quick renders from models
- AI-enhanced materials
- Environment generation
- Animation capabilities

The standard for architectural visualization.

**Cost:** ~$1,600/year

### Enscape

Live rendering:

- Real-time walkthrough
- VR support
- Works inside Revit/SketchUp
- AI lighting

Quick visualization without leaving your modeler.

**Cost:** $56/month

## Generative Design

### Autodesk Forma (includes Spacemaker)

Site analysis and optimization:

- Analyze thousands of design options
- Wind, sun, and noise analysis
- Density optimization
- Regulatory compliance checking

AI evaluates site options before you commit to a direction.

**Cost:** Part of Autodesk AEC Collection

### TestFit

Real estate feasibility:

- Instant unit layouts
- Parking optimization
- Zoning compliance
- Financial analysis

Know if a project is viable in minutes, not weeks.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing

### ARCHITEChTURES

AI residential design:

- Automated floor plans
- Building massing
- Optimization engine
- BIM export

Particularly strong for residential multifamily.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

### Finch

Site optimization:

- Generative site planning
- Real-time feedback
- Multiple scenarios
- Design constraints

Test site strategies quickly.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

## Documentation & Communication

### Claude for Architects

Complex document work:

**Specifications:**
> "Write a specification section for exterior aluminum curtain wall systems. Include performance requirements, quality assurance, and coordination requirements."

**Code research:**
> "Summarize the egress requirements for a 50,000 SF office building under IBC 2021. Include corridor widths, exit distances, and stair requirements."

**RFI responses:**
> "Draft a response to this RFI about the waterproofing detail at the plaza level. Explain the design intent and suggest a modification."

Claude handles long, complex documents well.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Pro

### ChatGPT

Quick writing tasks:

- Project descriptions
- Client communications
- Meeting summaries
- Marketing content

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Plus

**High Value:**

- Visualization (faster iterations)
- Regulatory research (instant answers)

**Growing Value:**
- Documentation automation
- Drawing production

## The Human Element

AI won't design great buildings. But it will:

- Explore more options than you could manually
- Analyze sites before expensive decisions
- Visualize ideas for client communication
- Handle documentation grunt work

The architect's judgment — understanding clients, sites, culture, materials — remains irreplaceable. AI amplifies it.
