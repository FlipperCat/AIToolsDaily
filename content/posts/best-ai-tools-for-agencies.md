## Content Production

### Claude for Strategic Work

Deep thinking and strategy:

**Brand strategy:**
> "We're positioning a fintech startup. Their competitors are [list]. Help me identify white space in their messaging and develop a differentiated positioning statement."

**Content strategy:**
> "Create a 3-month content calendar for a B2B SaaS client. Their goals are [goals], audience is [audience], and they want to own [topics]."

**Client presentations:**
> "Structure a pitch deck for [client opportunity]. Include slide concepts and key points for each section."

**Cost:** $20/mo team plan

### Jasper for Scale

When you need volume:

- Blog posts at scale
- Multiple ad variations
- Email sequences
- Landing page copy

Templates and brand voice settings help maintain consistency.

**Cost:** Starting $49/mo

### ChatGPT for Everything Else

Daily content tasks:

- Social media copy
- Email drafts
- Headlines variations
- Quick rewrites

**Cost:** $20/mo per seat (Team plan)

## The Agency Business Case

### Without AI
- 10 blog posts/month capacity per writer
- 1 pitch deck per week
- High fixed costs
- Limited scaling

### With AI
- 20-30 blog posts/month capacity per writer
- 2-3 pitch decks per week
- Higher margins
- Scalable delivery

**The math:** If AI tools cost $100/person/month and increase output by 50%, that's massive ROI.

## The Hard Truth for Agencies

AI commoditizes execution. Anyone can generate content now.

What AI can't do:
- Strategy based on deep client knowledge
- Creative direction
- Client relationships
- Judgment and taste

The agencies winning with AI focus on:
1. Strategic value (AI supports, doesn't replace)
2. Speed and efficiency (deliver more, faster)
3. Quality control (AI drafts, humans perfect)
4. Client experience (more attention, not less)

Use AI to do more of what clients actually value.
