## Lesson Planning

### MagicSchool AI

Built by teachers, for teachers. The most comprehensive education AI platform:

- **Lesson Plan Generator** — standards-aligned in seconds
- **Rubric Creator** — customizable to any assignment
- **IEP Goal Writer** — SMART goals from descriptions
- **Email Composer** — parent communication drafts
- **Quiz Generator** — multiple formats
- **Text Rewriter** — adjust reading levels instantly

40+ tools in one platform. The free tier is genuinely useful.

**Cost:** Free for core features, $10/mo for premium

### Eduaide.AI

Generates teaching resources in 100+ languages:

- Lesson hooks and closures
- Assessment questions
- Vocabulary activities
- Differentiated materials
- Standards alignment

Particularly good for generating varied question types.

**Cost:** Free tier, $5/mo for unlimited

### Almanack

Focuses on curriculum alignment:

- Backward design lesson planning
- Standards mapping
- Unit sequencing
- Resource suggestions

Saves hours on curriculum documentation.

**Cost:** $15/month

## Differentiation & Reading

### Diffit

This one's a game-changer for differentiation. Paste any text and it:

- Adjusts reading level (elementary to high school)
- Generates comprehension questions
- Creates vocabulary lists
- Produces summaries
- Makes it accessible for ELL students

Turn any article into leveled reading materials in 30 seconds.

**Cost:** Free tier is generous, $10/mo for unlimited

### Twee

Built specifically for ELL and ESL teachers:

- Generate texts at specific CEFR levels
- Create cloze exercises
- Make vocabulary activities
- Design dialogue practice

If you teach English learners, this saves hours weekly.

**Cost:** Free tier, $12/mo for premium

### Class Companion

## Writing Assistance

### ChatGPT for Teachers

The Swiss Army knife. Use it for:

**Lesson Planning:**
> "Create a 5E lesson plan for teaching photosynthesis to 7th graders, including a hands-on activity and formative assessment."

**Parent Communication:**
> "Write a positive email to parents about a student who's improved in participation but still struggles with homework completion."

**Differentiation:**
> "Rewrite this passage at a 4th-grade reading level while maintaining key concepts."

**Assessment:**
> "Generate 10 multiple choice questions on the American Revolution, ranging from recall to analysis."

**Cost:** Free tier works, $20/mo for GPT-4

### Claude for Feedback

Claude excels at nuanced writing feedback. Upload student work and ask:

> "Provide constructive feedback on this essay focusing on thesis development and evidence use. Use encouraging language appropriate for a 10th grader."

The longer context window means it can analyze multiple papers or longer documents.

**Cost:** Free tier, $20/mo for Pro

## Important Considerations

### Student Privacy
- Check your district's approved tool list
- Review data policies before using with student data
- Get necessary permissions
- Use school email for signups

### Academic Integrity
- Teach students about appropriate AI use
- Design AI-resistant assessments when needed
- Focus on process, not just product
- Use AI detection tools thoughtfully (they're imperfect)

### Your Time
Start with ONE tool that solves your biggest pain point. Master it before adding more. The goal is less work, not more tools to manage.

## The Reality Check

These tools won't:
- Replace relationship-building with students
- Eliminate all grading
- Make classroom management easier
- Work perfectly every time

They will:
- Give you time back for what matters
- Help you create better materials faster
- Make differentiation actually doable
- Reduce repetitive admin work

The best teachers I know use AI for the routine stuff so they can focus on the human stuff.
