## 1. Gamma

**Best For:** Creating polished presentations instantly

Gamma uses AI to automatically design beautiful presentations from text or outlines. Simply describe what you want, and Gamma generates a complete presentation with professional design, layout, and imagery in minutes.

**Features:**
- AI auto-design and layout
- Automatic content formatting
- Image generation integration
- Real-time collaboration
- Presenter mode with speaker notes
- Multiple export formats
- Mobile-responsive slides
- Brand customization

**Pricing:** Free tier, Pro $12/month, Teams custom pricing

**Pros:**
- Fastest creation speed
- Beautiful default designs
- Free tier is quite useful
- No design experience needed
- Real-time collaboration
- Excellent mobile support
- Regular feature updates

**Cons:**
- Free tier has limitations
- Limited customization on free
- Smaller integrations ecosystem
- Can feel generic
- Learning curve for advanced use

**Who It's Best For:** Busy professionals, startup founders, and anyone needing presentations fast without design skills.

Canva offers the largest template library with AI-assisted design. While not AI-first, its AI features like Magic Write and Magic Resize make design accessible to non-designers.

**Features:**
- 10,000+ presentation templates
- Magic Write (AI copywriting)
- Magic Resize (auto-resize for platforms)
- Drag-and-drop simplicity
- Stock images and music
- Brand kit for consistency
- Collaboration tools
- Real-time editing

**Pricing:** Free tier (limited), Canva Pro $119.99/year, Teams $180/month

**Pros:**
- Largest template library
- Easiest to use
- Affordable pro pricing
- Magic Write is useful
- Great brand kit
- Excellent mobile app
- Strong community

**Cons:**
- Design templates can feel overused
- Limited AI content generation
- Not truly AI-first design
- Free tier quite limited
- Smaller integrations than Office

**Who It's Best For:** Small businesses, social media creators, non-designers, and anyone wanting quick, template-based presentations.
