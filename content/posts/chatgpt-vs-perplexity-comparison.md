ChatGPT and Perplexity both answer questions. But they do it very differently.

Here's when to use each.

## The Core Difference

**ChatGPT:** Generates answers from training data
**Perplexity:** Searches the web and provides sources

This matters more than any feature comparison.

---

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | ChatGPT | Perplexity |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **Sources** | No citations | Cites sources |
| **Real-time info** | Knowledge cutoff | Live web search |
| **Best for** | Generation | Research |
| **Writing help** | Excellent | Basic |
| **Accuracy** | Variable | Verifiable |
| **Free tier** | Good | Good |
| **Pro price** | $20/mo | $20/mo |

---

## When to Use Perplexity

### Research with Sources

Need to cite your sources? Perplexity shows you exactly where information came from.

"What are the current interest rates from major banks?"
- Perplexity: Shows current rates with source links
- ChatGPT: May give outdated information

### Current Events

Anything that changes frequently:
- News and recent events
- Current prices
- Latest developments
- Company information

Perplexity searches live. ChatGPT uses training data.

### Fact-Checking

When accuracy matters:
- Medical information
- Legal questions
- Technical specifications
- Statistics

Perplexity lets you verify. ChatGPT asks you to trust it.

### Academic Research

Finding sources for papers:
- Perplexity finds actual papers
- Shows publication dates
- Provides citation-ready info

---

## When to Use ChatGPT

### Creative Writing

Generating original content:
- Blog posts
- Marketing copy
- Creative ideas
- Story writing

ChatGPT is a better writer. Perplexity is a better researcher.

### Brainstorming

Open-ended ideation:
- Business ideas
- Problem-solving
- Strategy planning
- Creative exploration

ChatGPT riffs more freely. Perplexity sticks to what it finds.

### Coding Help

Writing and debugging code:
- ChatGPT handles code well
- More conversational debugging
- Better at understanding context

### Conversation and Explanation

When you need a tutor:
- Explaining complex concepts
- Back-and-forth discussion
- Learning new topics

ChatGPT is more conversational.

---

## Accuracy Comparison

### ChatGPT's Issue

ChatGPT can confidently state incorrect information. It has no way to verify what it says.

This is fine for creative work. Dangerous for research.

### Perplexity's Advantage

Every claim links to a source. You can verify.

Not perfect — web sources can be wrong — but at least you can check.

---

## The Combined Workflow

Smart users use both:

1. **Perplexity** for research and fact-gathering
2. **ChatGPT** for writing and creation
3. **Perplexity** for fact-checking the output

Example workflow:
- Research topic on Perplexity
- Write content with ChatGPT
- Verify facts on Perplexity

## Free Tier Comparison

**ChatGPT Free:**
- GPT-3.5 (good for most tasks)
- Daily limits
- No web access

**Perplexity Free:**
- Basic AI search
- Limited Pro searches
- Web access included

Both free tiers work for casual use.

---

## Pro Tier Comparison ($20/mo each)

**ChatGPT Plus:**
- GPT-4 access
- Higher limits
- Code interpreter
- Image generation

**Perplexity Pro:**
- More Pro searches
- Better AI models
- File upload
- API access

---

## My Recommendation

**Get Perplexity if:**
- You research frequently
- Accuracy matters for your work
- You need current information
- You cite sources

**Get ChatGPT if:**
- You write content
- You need creative help
- You code regularly
- You want conversational AI

**Get both if:**
- Information work is central to your job
- $40/month is reasonable for your use
- You want the best of both worlds

Perplexity replaced Google search for me.

Different tools, different jobs.
