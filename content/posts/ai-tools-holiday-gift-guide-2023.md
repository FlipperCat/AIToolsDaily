# AI Tools Holiday Gift Guide 2023

Forget socks. Gift the future.

AI tool subscriptions make excellent gifts for the curious, creative, and productive people in your life.

## For Writers & Content Creators

### ChatGPT Plus — $20/month

**Who it's for:** Anyone who writes emails, articles, or creative content.

**Why it's great:** GPT-4 access transforms brainstorming, drafting, and editing. The person who "hates writing" will suddenly not mind it.

**Gift tip:** Get them 3 months ($60) to build the habit.

### Jasper — $39/month

**Who it's for:** Marketers and content professionals.

**Why it's great:** Templates for every content type. Blog posts, ads, emails, social—all optimized.

**Gift tip:** Great for small business owners who struggle with marketing copy.

## For Visual Creators

### Midjourney — $10-30/month

**Who it's for:** Artists, designers, content creators, anyone visual.

**Why it's great:** The best AI image generation available. Creates stunning artwork from text.

**Gift tip:** Start with the $10/month plan. It's enough to explore.

### Canva Pro — $13/month

**Who it's for:** Non-designers who need to design.

**Why it's great:** Now includes AI features—image generation, background removal, magic resize.

**Gift tip:** Perfect for the small business owner or social media manager.

## For Productivity Nerds

### Notion AI — $10/month (add-on)

**Who it's for:** Notion users (or people you want to convert).

**Why it's great:** AI assistance built into the best productivity tool. Summarize notes, brainstorm, draft content.

**Gift tip:** If they already use Notion, this is an easy win.

### Otter.ai Pro — $17/month

**Who it's for:** Anyone who attends meetings.

**Why it's great:** Transcribes meetings, creates summaries, makes everything searchable.

**Gift tip:** The person who always asks "can you repeat that?" needs this.

## For Coders

### GitHub Copilot — $10/month

**Who it's for:** Developers of any level.

**Why it's great:** AI pair programming. It suggests code as you type. Dramatically speeds up development.

**Gift tip:** Even skeptics become converts after a week.

### Replit Pro — $20/month

**Who it's for:** Beginners learning to code, or devs who want a cloud environment.

**Why it's great:** Code anywhere, AI assistance built in, deploy instantly.

**Gift tip:** Great for the person who wants to learn programming.

## For The Curious

### Perplexity Pro — $20/month

**Who it's for:** Researchers, students, curious minds.

**Why it's great:** AI-powered search with sources. Better than googling for complex questions.

**Gift tip:** The person who's always going down Wikipedia rabbit holes.

### Claude Pro — $20/month

**Who it's for:** Power users who want an alternative to ChatGPT.

**Why it's great:** Excellent for analysis, long documents, nuanced writing.

**Gift tip:** For the person who already has ChatGPT Plus and wants more options.
