## AI Video Editing

### 8. Descript — Best Editing Innovation

**Price:** Free tier / $12/mo Creator

**What it does:** Edit video by editing text transcript.

**Key features:**
- Edit video like a document
- Remove filler words automatically
- AI voice cloning for corrections
- Overdub: AI voice for changes
- Screen recording included

**Revolutionary feature:** Delete a word from transcript, it's deleted from video.

**Best for:** Podcasters, YouTubers, anyone editing talking videos.

**Limitations:**
- Less useful for non-talking video
- Learning curve for overdub

## Quality Expectations

### What AI Does Well
- B-roll and ambient footage
- Avatar talking heads
- Simple animations
- Content transformation
- Editing assistance

### What AI Struggles With
- Complex human movement
- Specific actor performances
- Long coherent scenes
- Photorealistic humans in motion
- Precise direction following

### Realistic Assessment
AI video is supplementary, not replacement for traditional production. Use it for:
- Elements within larger productions
- Content at scale
- Situations where traditional production isn't feasible
- Experimentation and concepting
