## Idea Validation

### ChatGPT for Market Research

Before building anything:

**Market sizing:**
> "Help me estimate the market size for [product idea]. Who are the potential customers, how many exist, and what would they pay?"

**Competitive analysis:**
> "Who are the main competitors in [space]? What are their strengths and weaknesses? Where's the opportunity?"

**Customer discovery:**
> "I'm building [product]. What are the most important questions to ask potential customers to validate this idea?"

Don't build in a vacuum.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Strategy Docs

Pitch decks and strategy:

> "Help me write a problem statement for my pitch deck. Our product is [describe]. The problem we solve is [describe]. Make it compelling."

> "Create a business model canvas for [startup idea]. Include all nine building blocks."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Building Product

### GitHub Copilot

Code faster:

- Autocomplete that understands context
- Generate boilerplate
- Write tests
- Explain unfamiliar code

"Like pair programming with someone who knows every library."

**Cost:** $10/mo

### ChatGPT for Development

When you're stuck:

> "Debug this error: [paste error and code]. Explain what's happening."

> "What's the best way to implement [feature] in [framework]?"

> "Review this architecture decision: [describe]. What could go wrong?"

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Cursor

AI-native code editor:

- Built-in AI assistance
- Codebase-aware suggestions
- Refactoring help

Alternative to Copilot with different approach.

**Cost:** $20/mo Pro

## What AI Replaces

### Hire Later
- Full-time content writer (use AI + part-time editor)
- Customer support team (AI-augmented support)
- Design agency (Canva + occasional freelancer)
- Market research firm (Perplexity + AI analysis)

### Hire Now
- Core engineering (AI helps but doesn't replace)
- Sales (relationships matter)
- Domain expertise (your unique value)

---

## The Startup Reality

AI doesn't guarantee startup success.

It does:
- Reduce burn rate
- Increase output per person
- Speed up iteration
- Allow you to punch above your weight

It doesn't:
- Find product-market fit
- Build relationships with customers
- Make hard decisions
- Replace domain expertise

Use AI for leverage, not laziness.
