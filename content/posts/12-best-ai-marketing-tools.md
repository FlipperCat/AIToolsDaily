## 1. HubSpot Marketing Hub

**Best For:** Growing companies wanting unified marketing platform

HubSpot Marketing Hub combines email marketing, landing pages, ads, social, and analytics in one platform. Its free tier is robust, making it perfect for startups and small businesses.

**Features:**
- Email marketing and automation
- Landing page builder
- Social media scheduling
- Ad management (Facebook, LinkedIn, Google)
- SEO tools
- Content calendar
- Workflows and automation
- Analytics and reporting
- CRM integration

**Pricing:** Free (limited), Starter $50/month, Professional $800+/month

**Pros:**
- Excellent free tier
- All-in-one platform
- Easy to use
- Good for SMBs
- Workflow automation
- Analytics solid
- Regular updates
- Great support

**Cons:**
- Less powerful than specialized tools
- Can feel basic for enterprises
- Free tier limited
- Scaling costs increase
- Not best-in-class for any single feature

**Who It's Best For:** Startups, small-to-medium businesses, and companies wanting unified marketing without complexity.
