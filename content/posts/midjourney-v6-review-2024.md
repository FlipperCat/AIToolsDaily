## What's New in V6

V6 can render text accurately. Signs, posters, logos—they actually work now.

Put words in quotes: `a coffee shop sign that says "Joe's Coffee"`

Photorealistic outputs improved significantly. Less "AI look."

V6 understands complex, detailed prompts more accurately.

More logical scenes. Fewer weird artifacts and impossible geometry.

Still not perfect, but noticeably better.

## Prompting Tips for V6

### Be Specific

V6 handles detail better. Use it.

**Basic:** "a forest"
**Better:** "dense Pacific Northwest forest, morning fog, sunlight filtering through tall cedar trees, ferns covering the forest floor"

### Style References

Describe the look:
- "in the style of a 1970s movie poster"
- "hyperrealistic photography"
- "watercolor illustration"

### Use Parameters

- `--ar 16:9` for aspect ratio
- `--s 250` for stylization level
- `--c 20` for chaos/variation

### Text in Quotes

For text in images: `sign that says "OPEN"`
