## Deal Analysis

### ChatGPT for Quick Analysis

Run numbers fast:

**Cash flow analysis:**
> "Analyze this rental property deal: Purchase price $250,000, expected rent $2,200/month, property taxes $3,600/year, insurance $1,800/year, assuming 8% vacancy and 10% for maintenance/capex. Calculate monthly cash flow, cash-on-cash return with 25% down, and cap rate."

**Comparable analysis:**
> "I found a 3-bed/2-bath in [neighborhood]. Recent sales show similar properties at $280-320K. List price is $250K. What questions should I be asking about why it's priced lower?"

**Exit strategy evaluation:**
> "I'm considering a flip at $200K purchase, $50K rehab, $300K ARV. What's my potential profit? What should I budget for holding costs and selling costs?"

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Deals

Multi-unit or commercial analysis:

> "Analyze this 8-unit apartment building: [paste financials]. Identify potential value-add opportunities, realistic expense ratios, and what rent increases would be needed to hit a 10% cash-on-cash return."

> "Compare these two investment strategies for $500K: (1) One turnkey rental vs (2) BRRRR on a distressed property. Model the 5-year returns with assumptions for each."

Claude handles longer, more detailed analysis.

**Cost:** $20/mo Pro

## AI Analysis Tips

### What to Verify
AI can help with analysis, but verify:
- Rent estimates (check actual listings)
- Comparable sales (verify with MLS/public records)
- Expense assumptions (talk to local investors)
- Market projections (multiple sources)

### What AI Does Well
- Quick initial analysis
- Scenario modeling
- Research compilation
- Checklist generation
- Pattern identification in data

### What AI Doesn't Know
- Local market nuances
- Neighborhood-level trends
- Relationship-based information
- Your specific goals and constraints

## The Competitive Edge

AI doesn't find deals. Relationships, marketing, and hustle find deals.

AI helps you:
- Analyze faster than competitors
- Research markets efficiently
- Model multiple scenarios quickly
- Systematize your process

Speed matters in real estate. AI provides speed.
