Remote teams need the right AI tools to stay connected and productive. Here's the essential stack for 2026.

## Communication

### Otter.ai ($16.99/month)

Meeting transcription for teams:
- Automatic recording and transcription
- Shared meeting notes
- Action item extraction
- Searchable meeting history

**Use for**: All team meetings

### Loom + AI (Free / $15/month)

Async video communication:
- Quick video updates
- AI-generated summaries
- Auto-transcription
- Replace unnecessary meetings

**Use for**: Updates, demos, feedback

### Grammarly Business ($15/user/month)

Team writing standards:
- Consistent communication
- Brand voice guidelines
- Error-free documents
- Team analytics

**Use for**: All written communication

## Collaboration

## Best Practices

1. **Standardize tools** - Everyone uses the same stack
2. **Train the team** - Investment in learning pays off
3. **Async first** - Use AI for better async communication
4. **Document everything** - AI makes it easier
5. **Measure impact** - Track productivity gains

Remote teams with AI tools outperform co-located teams without them. The right stack makes geography irrelevant.
