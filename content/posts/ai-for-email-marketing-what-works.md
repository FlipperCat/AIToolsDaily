# AI for Email Marketing: What Actually Works

I send about 40,000 emails monthly across various projects. I've been using AI for email marketing for 18 months.

Here's what actually moves the needle.

## Where AI Helps Most

### Subject Lines (Biggest Impact)

Subject lines are short, testable, and hugely impactful. Perfect for AI.

**My process:**
1. Tell AI the email topic and goal
2. Generate 15-20 subject line options
3. Pick 2-3 best ones
4. A/B test in actual campaign
5. Learn what works for my audience

**Example prompt:**
"Generate 15 email subject lines for a product launch announcement. Product: AI writing assistant for marketers. Audience: small business owners. Goal: drive clicks to landing page. Include: curiosity-based, benefit-focused, and question-based options. Keep under 50 characters."

**Results:** My open rates improved 12% after three months of AI-assisted subject line testing. Not because AI writes better subject lines than humans - because it lets me test more variations.

### Email Sequences (Time Saver)

Welcome sequences, abandoned cart, re-engagement - these follow patterns. AI handles patterns well.

**My process:**
1. Outline the sequence goals and flow
2. Have AI draft each email
3. Heavy editing for voice and specifics
4. Add real customer examples and data
5. Test and iterate

**Time saved:** A 5-email welcome sequence used to take me a full day. Now it's 2-3 hours including editing.

### A/B Test Variations

Need three versions of the same email? AI generates variations faster than I can think of them.

"Rewrite this email with a more urgent tone."
"Rewrite this email for a skeptical audience."
"Make this email 40% shorter."

Each variation takes 30 seconds instead of 20 minutes.

## Where AI Doesn't Help (Or Hurts)

### Personal Relationship Emails

When I'm emailing my engaged subscribers who know me, AI sounds fake.

These readers recognize my voice. AI outputs sound slightly off. They notice.

For relationship-based email marketing, I write personally. AI can help with structure, not voice.

### Sensitive Topics

Refund announcements, bad news, apologies - these need human judgment.

AI struggles with appropriate tone for difficult messages. Write these yourself.

### Your First Emails to a New List

Before AI understands your audience and voice, its suggestions are generic. You need to write enough yourself first to have patterns AI can learn.

## The Tools I Actually Use

### Claude Pro ($20/month) - Primary Copy

I use Claude for almost all email copy drafting. The writing quality is better than ChatGPT for marketing content.

**Why Claude over ChatGPT for emails:**
- More natural tone
- Better at maintaining voice consistency
- Fewer clichés and buzzwords

### Klaviyo (Various pricing) - AI Send Time

Klaviyo's AI predicts optimal send times for each subscriber. I stopped guessing when to send.

**Results:** 8% improvement in open rates from send time optimization alone.

### Mailchimp AI Features (Free-$20/month)

If you're smaller scale, Mailchimp's AI features are solid:
- Subject line helper
- Content optimization suggestions
- Predicted demographics

Not as powerful as dedicated tools, but good for starting out.

**Campaign:** Product launch email sequence

## My Email Marketing AI Workflow

**Step 1: Strategic Planning (Human)**
- What's the goal?
- What's the sequence structure?
- What's the offer?

AI can't do strategy. This is human work.

**Step 2: Draft Generation (AI)**

```
Prompt template:
Write an email for [audience] about [topic].
Goal: [specific action]
Tone: [your brand tone]
Length: [target word count]
Include: [specific elements]
Avoid: [things that don't fit your brand]
```

**Step 3: Human Editing (Critical)**
- Add personal stories and specific examples
- Remove generic phrases
- Check facts and claims
- Adjust for brand voice
- Add real customer quotes/results

**Step 4: Subject Line Generation (AI)**
- Generate 15-20 options
- Human selects top 2-3
- A/B test

**Step 5: Send Optimization (AI Tool)**
- Let Klaviyo or similar optimize timing
- Trust the algorithm on send times

## What I'd Tell Someone Starting

1. **Start with subject lines.** Highest impact, lowest risk.

2. **Don't publish AI drafts directly.** Always edit. Always add your perspective.

3. **Learn what works for your audience.** AI gives you options. Testing tells you what actually works.

4. **Invest in good email software.** Klaviyo's AI features are worth the price if you're serious about email.

5. **Keep your voice.** The emails that convert best are authentic. Use AI for efficiency, not replacement.

Email marketing is still about relationships. AI helps you show up more consistently. The connection is still human.
