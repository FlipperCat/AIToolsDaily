## Listing Descriptions That Actually Sell

### ChatGPT

The secret weapon most agents don't use properly. Don't just ask for a "listing description." Give it context:

**Better prompt:**
> Write a listing description for a 3BR/2BA ranch in [neighborhood]. Key selling points: updated kitchen with quartz counters, large backyard, new HVAC 2024. Target buyer: young families. Tone: warm but not cheesy. 150 words max.

You'll get descriptions that sound human, not template.

**Cost:** Free tier works fine, $20/mo for GPT-4

### Write.homes

Built specifically for real estate. Paste in MLS data and it generates:
- Listing descriptions
- Social media posts
- Email campaigns
- Neighborhood guides

The templates understand real estate terminology and buyer psychology.

**Cost:** $29/month

## Property Marketing

### Canva AI

Create scroll-stopping social media posts, flyers, and virtual tour covers in minutes. The Magic Write feature generates copy, and Magic Edit fixes photos.

**Real estate templates include:**
- Just Listed/Just Sold posts
- Open house flyers
- Property brochures
- Instagram Stories templates

**Cost:** Free tier is generous, Pro at $13/mo

### Beautiful.ai

For listing presentations that don't look like every other agent's. AI suggests layouts as you add content. Import property photos and it builds a professional deck automatically.

**Cost:** $12/month

## Lead Follow-Up

### Follow Up Boss

The industry standard CRM, now with AI features:
- Smart lists prioritize hot leads
- AI suggests best time to reach out
- Automated drip campaigns
- Tracks lead source ROI

The AI learns from your successful deals and applies patterns to new leads.

**Cost:** $69/month per user

### Lofty (formerly Chime)

All-in-one platform with an AI assistant that:
- Qualifies leads via text
- Books showings automatically
- Sends personalized follow-ups
- Identifies ready-to-buy signals

Expensive but replaces multiple tools.

**Cost:** $449/month (includes IDX website)

## Time Savers

### Calendly

Stop the back-and-forth on showing times. Clients book directly into your calendar. Set buffer time between appointments for travel.

**Pro tip:** Create different booking types for:
- First-time buyer consultations (60 min)
- Property showings (30 min)
- Listing appointments (90 min)

**Cost:** Free for basic, $10/mo for teams

### Zapier

Connect your tools so leads flow automatically:
- New Zillow lead → CRM → welcome email
- Signed contract → calendar event → task list
- Closed deal → review request → social post

Eliminates manual data entry.

**Cost:** Free for 100 tasks/mo, then $20/mo

### Otter.ai

Record client meetings and get instant transcripts. Never forget what a buyer said they wanted. Search past conversations by keyword.

**Cost:** Free 300 min/mo, $17/mo unlimited

## Property Intelligence

### Restb.ai

AI that analyzes listing photos and automatically:
- Tags room types
- Identifies features (fireplace, pool, hardwood)
- Suggests improvements
- Flags photo quality issues

Saves hours on MLS data entry.

**Cost:** Custom pricing for brokerages

### HouseCanary

Market intelligence powered by AI:
- Property valuations
- Market forecasts
- Investment analysis
- Comp selection

Know the market better than your competition.

**Cost:** Starts at $35/report
