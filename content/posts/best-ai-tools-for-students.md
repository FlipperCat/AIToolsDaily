## Research

### Perplexity (Free)

Research with citations:

- Answers with sources
- Academic and web search
- Follow-up questions
- Free tier is generous

Better than ChatGPT when you need to cite sources.

**Use for:** Research papers, fact-checking, starting points

### Consensus (Free)

Academic paper search:

- AI-powered paper discovery
- Summarizes findings
- Shows scientific consensus
- Peer-reviewed sources only

When you need actual academic sources.

**Use for:** Literature reviews, evidence-based arguments

### Elicit (Free tier)

Research workflow:

- Find papers
- Extract key information
- Summarize abstracts
- Compare findings

Like a research assistant.

## Writing Help

### Claude (Free)

Writing partner:

**Get feedback:**
> "Review my thesis statement and introduction. Tell me if my argument is clear and what I could strengthen."

**Understand concepts:**
> "Explain [complex topic] like I'm an undergrad who just learned the basics."

**Outline help:**
> "Help me structure a 10-page paper on [topic]. What should each section cover?"

Don't have Claude write your papers. Have it help you write better.

### ChatGPT (Free)

Quick explanations:

> "Explain the difference between [concept A] and [concept B]."
> "What are the main arguments for and against [position]?"
> "Help me understand this passage: [paste text]"

Great for clarifying concepts.

### Quillbot (Free tier)

Paraphrasing tool:

- Rewrite sentences
- Find different ways to say things
- Grammar checking
- Citation generator

When you understand a concept but struggle to express it differently.

**Use ethically:** To improve YOUR writing, not avoid plagiarism

### Grammarly (Free + edu discount)

Writing polish:

- Grammar and spelling
- Clarity suggestions
- Tone adjustment
- Works everywhere

Many schools provide premium access free.

## Academic Integrity

### The Honest Conversation

AI tools are powerful. Using them ethically matters.

**Appropriate uses:**
- Understanding concepts
- Getting feedback on YOUR writing
- Research and discovery
- Brainstorming ideas
- Checking your work
- Learning how to improve

**Inappropriate uses:**
- Submitting AI writing as your own
- Having AI do assignments for you
- Circumventing the learning process
- Cheating on tests

**The test:** Would you be comfortable telling your professor how you used AI?

### School Policies

- Check your institution's AI policy
- Ask professors about their expectations
- When in doubt, disclose
- The rules are evolving

## The Real Value

AI won't learn for you. It can't:
- Understand concepts deeply (that's your job)
- Build critical thinking skills
- Develop your voice as a writer
- Replace doing the work

AI can:
- Speed up research
- Clarify confusing concepts
- Provide feedback on your writing
- Help you study more effectively

Use it as a learning accelerator, not a shortcut.
