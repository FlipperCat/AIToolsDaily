# Best AI Video Editing Tools in 2026

AI video editing finally delivers on promises. These tools actually work.

## Editing and Production

### Descript — Best Overall

**What it does:** Edit video by editing text

**Key features:**
- Transcript-based editing
- Remove filler words automatically
- AI voice cloning for corrections
- Automatic captions
- Multi-track editing

**Why it's great:** Edit like a document. Delete words from transcript, footage disappears.

**Price:** $12-24/month

**Best for:** Podcasters, educators, course creators

### CapCut — Best Free

**What it does:** Full video editor with AI features

**Key features:**
- Auto-captions (excellent accuracy)
- Background removal
- AI effects and filters
- Music recommendations
- Template-based editing

**Why it's great:** Professional features at no cost

**Price:** Free (Pro available)

**Best for:** Social media creators, beginners

### Runway — Best for AI Effects

**What it does:** AI-powered creative video tools

**Key features:**
- Gen-2 video generation
- Motion tracking
- Object removal
- Style transfer
- Green screen alternative

**Why it's great:** Things that used to require VFX expertise

**Price:** $12-76/month

**Best for:** Creative professionals, effects work

## Specific Use Cases

### Opus Clip — Best for Repurposing

**What it does:** Turn long videos into short clips

**How it works:**
- Upload long video
- AI identifies best moments
- Auto-generates shorts with captions
- Ready for TikTok/Reels/Shorts

**Time saved:** Hours per video

**Price:** Free tier, $15+/month paid

**Best for:** YouTube creators repurposing content

### Munch — Best for Social Clips

**What it does:** AI-powered clip selection

**Features:**
- Identifies viral-worthy moments
- Auto-reframes for vertical
- Adds captions
- Multi-platform export

**Similar to:** Opus Clip, different AI approach

### Synthesia — Best for AI Avatars

**What it does:** Create videos with AI presenters

**How it works:**
- Write script
- Choose AI avatar
- Generate video

**Best for:** Training videos, explainers, localization

**Price:** $22+/month

### HeyGen — Best Avatar Alternative

**What it does:** AI avatar videos with your face

**Features:**
- Clone your own appearance
- Lip sync in any language
- Professional quality

**Best for:** Global content, personal avatar videos

## Captions and Subtitles

### Captions (App)

**What it does:** Automatic captions for social video

**Why it's good:** Fastest, most accurate auto-captions

**Price:** Free with limits, subscription for more

### Kapwing

**What it does:** Online video editor with AI captions

**Best for:** Quick web-based editing with subtitles

### Built-in Options

CapCut, Descript, and Premiere all have solid auto-captions now.

## What AI Editing Does Well

- **Captions:** Faster and more accurate than ever
- **Clip selection:** Finds good moments reliably
- **Audio cleanup:** Background noise removal
- **Reframing:** Vertical/horizontal conversion
- **Tedious tasks:** Silence removal, filler word cuts

## What It Doesn't Do Well

- **Creative decisions:** What story to tell
- **Complex edits:** Multi-layer compositions
- **Brand consistency:** Still needs human oversight
- **Perfection:** 90% there, needs human polish
