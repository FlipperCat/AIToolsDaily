# Loom AI Features: Worth the Upgrade?

Loom used to be simple: record screen, share link, done. Now it's pushing AI features hard.

I've used Loom AI for 3 months. Here's what's actually useful.

## The AI Features

Loom's AI adds:
1. **Auto-generated titles** - AI names your video
2. **Summary** - Key points in written form
3. **Chapters** - Automatic section markers
4. **Transcript** - Full text of what you said
5. **Filler word removal** - Cuts "ums" and "uhs"
6. **Written recap** - Email-ready summary

Let's assess each.

## Auto-Generated Titles

**What it does:** AI suggests a title based on your video content.

**Reality:** Sometimes good, often generic. "Team Update Discussion" isn't helpful when all your videos are team updates.

**My usage:** I ignore it and write my own titles 90% of the time.

**Verdict:** Minor convenience. Not a selling point.

## Video Summaries

**What it does:** Creates a bullet-point summary of your video's key points.

**Reality:** Actually useful. Viewers can scan the summary before watching. Saves time.

**My usage:** I check the summary and sometimes edit it. Viewers appreciate it.

**Verdict:** Genuinely useful feature.

## Automatic Chapters

**What it does:** Breaks your video into sections with timestamps.

**Reality:** Works well for structured videos (updates, tutorials). Less useful for rambling messages.

**My usage:** I leave them on. Viewers use them to jump to relevant parts.

**Verdict:** Useful for longer videos.

## Transcription

**What it does:** Full text transcript of your video.

**Reality:** Accuracy is good (90%+). Searchable. Good for reference.

**My usage:** I don't read transcripts but they're there if needed.

**Verdict:** Nice to have, not essential.

## Filler Word Removal

**What it does:** AI edits out "um," "uh," etc.

**Reality:** Works but makes videos sound choppy if you say "um" a lot.

**My usage:** I keep it off. I'd rather sound natural than choppy.

**Verdict:** Only useful if you're a heavy filler-word user.

## Written Recaps

**What it does:** Creates an email-ready summary to share with the video.

**Reality:** Good for sending video + context together. Format is acceptable.

**My usage:** Occasionally useful for important updates.

**Verdict:** Nice for formal communication.

## The Pricing Reality

**Loom Free:**
- 5 minute video limit
- 25 video limit
- No AI features

**Loom Business ($15/user/month):**
- Unlimited videos
- AI features
- Team features

**Loom Enterprise (custom):**
- Advanced admin
- Security features

**The math:** $15/month is a lot for video messaging if you could use free alternatives.

## Is AI Worth the Upgrade?

### Yes, if:

- You record 10+ Looms per week
- Viewers value summaries and chapters
- Professional communication matters
- Your team uses Loom extensively

### No, if:

- You record occasionally
- 5-minute limit works for you
- Budget is tight
- You don't care about AI summaries

### The honest take:

The AI features are nice additions, not game-changers. The core value of Loom is still quick video messaging. AI makes it slightly better.

If you were already paying for Loom, AI is a nice bonus.

If you were on free tier, AI alone doesn't justify upgrading.

## Compared to Alternatives

### Tella

- Similar AI features
- Newer, less polished
- Cheaper ($12/month)
- Worth trying if Loom feels expensive

### Screenpal (formerly Screencast-O-Matic)

- Free tier is more generous
- Fewer AI features
- Good for basic needs

### Vimeo Record

- Included if you have Vimeo
- Basic AI features
- Worth it if you're in Vimeo ecosystem

### Just recording yourself

- Quicktime/OBS are free
- No AI, no sharing magic
- Works for simple needs

## My Verdict

**Loom is still the best async video tool.** The polish, the sharing, the viewer experience - all excellent.

**The AI features are useful additions.** Summaries and chapters genuinely help. But they're not revolutionary.

**Pricing is the question.** $15/user/month adds up for teams. Worth it if async video is central to your work.

## Who Should Use Loom AI

### Definitely worth it:

- Remote teams using async video heavily
- Client-facing communication where polish matters
- Anyone recording 10+ videos per week
- Companies that can expense it

### Maybe worth it:

- Frequent recorders who want time savings
- People who hate their own "ums"
- Teams evaluating async tools

### Probably not worth it:

- Occasional video senders
- Budget-conscious individuals
- People fine with 5-minute limit

## What I Actually Do

I pay for Loom Business. I record 15-20 Looms per week. The AI features save me maybe 10 minutes per day.

Is it worth $15/month? For me, yes. Time savings compound.

Would I pay just for AI features? No. I pay for the core Loom experience. AI is a bonus.

## Bottom Line

Loom's AI features are solid additions to an already-good product. Summaries and chapters are genuinely useful. Other features are minor conveniences.

Don't upgrade just for AI. Upgrade if async video is important to your work and Loom is your tool of choice. The AI is a nice bonus, not the main event.
