## Client Communication

### ChatGPT for Client Messages

Draft professional communications:

**Policy explanations:**
> "Explain the difference between comprehensive and collision coverage to someone who knows nothing about car insurance. Keep it under 100 words and avoid jargon."

**Follow-up emails:**
> "Write a follow-up email to a prospect who got a quote a week ago but hasn't responded. Be helpful, not pushy. Mention I'm available to answer questions."

**Claims support:**
> "Draft a reassuring email to a client who just filed their first claim. Explain the next steps and set expectations for timeline."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Explanations

When policies get complicated:

> "A client is comparing two whole life policies with different dividend options. Create a side-by-side comparison they can understand without a finance degree."

> "Explain why an umbrella policy makes sense for a homeowner with [scenario]. Include specific examples of what it would cover that their home and auto wouldn't."

Claude handles nuanced, educational content well.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Loom

Video explanations:

- Walk through policy documents
- Explain coverage visually
- Personalized quotes
- Claims process guidance

More personal than email, easier than scheduling.

**Cost:** Free tier / $15/mo Business

## The Insurance Agent's Edge

AI won't replace insurance agents. People want a human for:
- Trust decisions
- Complex situations
- Claims support
- Life changes

But AI can help you:
- Communicate more clearly
- Follow up consistently
- Explain policies better
- Reach more prospects

More efficiency = more time for relationships that close deals.

## Compliance Notes

- Never use AI to give personalized advice without review
- All quotes still need proper underwriting
- Document client communications
- Follow carrier and state regulations

AI assists. You're still responsible.
