Zapier remains the most accessible automation platform. Recent AI additions make it more powerful than ever.

**AI Capabilities:**
- ChatGPT built into workflows (summarize, extract, transform)
- AI Formatter automatically structures messy data

**Use Case:** Automatically summarize new support tickets, categorize them, and route to the right team member.

**Best for:** Users who want proven reliability with AI enhancements.

## 2. Make.com — Best for Power Users

**Price:** Free tier (1,000 ops), Core at $9/month
**AI Features:** OpenAI/Claude modules, AI text analysis

Make's visual builder handles complex automations better than Zapier, at a lower price point.

**AI Capabilities:**
- Native OpenAI, Claude, and other AI modules
- Chain multiple AI calls in one workflow
- Parse and analyze documents with AI

**Use Case:** Process invoices — extract data with AI, validate against database, create accounting entries, flag anomalies.

**Best for:** Technical users building sophisticated workflows with AI components.

## 3. Bardeen — Best for Browser Automation

**Price:** Free tier, Pro at $10/month
**AI Features:** AI scraping, smart fill, natural language commands

Bardeen automates browser tasks that other tools can't touch. The AI features make web scraping much easier.

**AI Capabilities:**
- Describe what to scrape in plain English
- AI auto-fills forms based on context
- Natural language automation building

**Use Case:** "Go to LinkedIn, find posts from my connections about AI, summarize the key points, save to Notion."

**Best for:** Research, lead generation, web-based workflows.

## 4. n8n — Best Self-Hosted Option

**Price:** Free (self-hosted), Cloud from $20/month
**AI Features:** LangChain nodes, vector databases, custom AI chains

n8n is the open-source alternative to Zapier, with powerful AI integrations for technical users.

**AI Capabilities:**
- LangChain integration for complex AI workflows
- Vector database connections (Pinecone, Weaviate)
- Build RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) systems

**Use Case:** Build a custom AI chatbot that answers questions using your company documents.

**Best for:** Developers, privacy-conscious users, teams wanting full control.

## 5. Relay.app — Best for Human-AI Collaboration

**Price:** Free tier, Pro at $9/month
**AI Features:** AI decision steps, approval workflows

Relay combines automation with human oversight — AI handles the work, humans approve critical decisions.

**AI Capabilities:**
- AI drafts responses for human approval
- Smart routing based on AI analysis
- Confidence thresholds determine human involvement

**Use Case:** AI drafts customer support responses. High-confidence answers send automatically; uncertain ones go to a human.

**Best for:** Teams wanting AI assistance while maintaining oversight.

## 6. Lindy.ai — Best for AI Assistants

**Price:** Starting at $49/month
**AI Features:** Full AI agents that work autonomously

Lindy creates AI "employees" that handle entire workflows, not just individual tasks.

**AI Capabilities:**
- AI agents that make decisions independently
- Multi-step task completion
- Learn from feedback over time

**Use Case:** AI sales assistant that researches prospects, writes personalized outreach, handles responses, and books meetings.

**Best for:** Businesses wanting to delegate entire workflows to AI.

## 7. Cassidy — Best for Enterprise

**Price:** Custom pricing
**AI Features:** Secure AI with company knowledge

Cassidy brings AI automation to enterprise environments with security and compliance features.

**AI Capabilities:**
- AI trained on your company data
- SOC 2 compliance
- Custom AI workflows with enterprise integrations

**Use Case:** Analyze contracts against company policies, flag non-compliant clauses, suggest revisions.

**Best for:** Large organizations with security requirements.

## 8. Levity — Best for Custom AI Models

**Price:** Starting at $49/month
**AI Features:** Train AI on your data, no code required

Levity lets you train custom AI models for your specific needs, without writing code.

**AI Capabilities:**
- Upload examples, Levity learns the pattern
- Email classification, document sorting, image tagging
- Connects to common apps via integrations

**Use Case:** Train AI to categorize incoming emails by topic and urgency based on your past decisions.

**Best for:** Businesses with unique classification/categorization needs.

## 9. Obviously AI — Best for Predictions

**Price:** Starting at $75/month
**AI Features:** No-code predictive analytics

Obviously AI turns your data into predictions — churn risk, sales forecasts, demand prediction — without data science expertise.

**AI Capabilities:**

- Explainable AI shows what drives predictions

**Use Case:** Predict which customers are likely to churn next month based on usage patterns.

**Best for:** Business analysts, operations teams, anyone making data-driven decisions.

## Getting Started

1. **Identify your most repetitive task** — What do you do manually that follows a pattern?

2. **Start simple** — Don't try to automate everything at once. Pick one workflow.

3. **Use free tiers** — Test before paying. Most tools offer enough for proof-of-concept.

4. **Add AI gradually** — Get basic automation working first, then layer in AI features.

5. **Measure results** — Track time saved. This justifies future automation investments.
