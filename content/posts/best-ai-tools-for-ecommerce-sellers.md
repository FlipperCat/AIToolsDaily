## Product Listings

### ChatGPT for Descriptions

Write listings that convert:

**Amazon listing:**
> "Write an Amazon product listing for a stainless steel water bottle. Features: 32oz, double-wall insulation, keeps drinks cold 24hr/hot 12hr, leak-proof lid, fits cup holders. Include title (under 200 chars), 5 bullet points, and description. Use keywords: water bottle, insulated, BPA-free."

**Shopify product page:**
> "Write a product description for a premium yoga mat. Target audience: serious practitioners who care about grip, thickness, and sustainability. Tone: confident, not salesy. Include features, benefits, and a subtle urgency element."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Jasper

Scale your content:

- Product descriptions at volume
- Email campaigns
- Ad copy
- Blog content

Better for teams producing lots of content.

**Cost:** $49/mo Creator

### Copy.ai

Quick listing generation:

- Product description templates
- Ad copy
- Email sequences
- Social captions

Good balance of quality and speed.

**Cost:** Free tier, $49/mo Pro
