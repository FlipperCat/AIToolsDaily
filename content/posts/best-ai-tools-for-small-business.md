## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Trying to automate everything at once**
Start with one high-impact workflow. Get it working. Then expand.

❌ **Choosing tools that don't integrate**
Check Zapier compatibility before buying any tool.

❌ **Not reviewing AI outputs**
AI makes mistakes. Always have a human review customer-facing content.

❌ **Ignoring the learning curve**
Budget time to learn tools properly. The ROI comes after setup.
