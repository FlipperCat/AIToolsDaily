## 1. Gmail with Gemini

**Best For:** Google Workspace users wanting integrated AI

Google's Gemini integration into Gmail provides native AI capabilities for composition, summarization, and response suggestions. Perfect for existing Google Workspace users.

**Features:**
- AI-assisted email composition
- Automatic summarization of long emails
- Smart reply suggestions
- Tone adjustment (formal, casual, etc.)
- Draft improvement suggestions
- Integration with Google Docs and Sheets
- Multi-language support
- Real-time collaboration

**Pricing:** Free (limited), $20/month (Gemini in Workspace)

**Pros:**
- Seamless Gmail integration
- No switching platforms needed
- Free option available
- Good composition help
- Excellent summarization
- Tone adjustment useful
- Regular updates

**Cons:**
- Limited without Workspace subscription
- Less powerful than specialized tools
- Fewer advanced features
- Smaller feature set
- Limited to Gmail ecosystem

**Who It's Best For:** Google Workspace users, professionals already in Google ecosystem, and anyone wanting AI without platform switching.

## 2. Superhuman

**Best For:** Email power users and professionals

Superhuman is the premium email client for professionals who live in email. With AI-powered features like split inbox, email loops, and command palette, it's designed for speed and efficiency.

**Features:**
- Split inbox by importance
- Email loop detection
- AI-powered search
- Follow-up reminders
- Snooze and schedule
- Keyboard shortcuts (extensive)
- Team shared inboxes
- Integration with 50+ tools

**Pricing:** $30/month personal, $40/month business

**Pros:**
- Exceptional speed and efficiency
- Split inbox is game-changing
- Keyboard shortcuts are powerful
- Loop detection prevents duplicates
- Great integrations
- Strong team features
- Mobile app excellent

**Cons:**
- High cost ($30-40/month)
- Significant learning curve
- Steep productivity curve for first month
- Not for casual email users
- Requires commitment to get ROI

**Who It's Best For:** High-email-volume professionals, executives, sales teams, and anyone spending 4+ hours daily in email.
