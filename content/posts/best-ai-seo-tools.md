## How AI SEO Tools Work

### Content Optimization (Surfer, Frase, Clearscope)

1. **Analyze SERP** — Tool scans top-ranking pages
2. **Extract patterns** — NLP identifies common terms, structure
3. **Create recommendations** — Suggested terms, headings, length
4. **Score content** — Real-time optimization feedback
5. **Compare** — Show how you stack up vs competitors

### Why This Works
Google ranks comprehensive content. AI tools ensure you cover topics thoroughly by analyzing what already ranks.

## AI SEO Workflow

### Research Phase
1. **Perplexity:** Research topic, find angles
2. **SEMrush/Ahrefs:** Keyword research, competition
3. **Frase:** Create content brief

### Writing Phase
1. **Claude/ChatGPT:** Write first draft
2. **Surfer/Frase:** Optimize in real-time
3. **Aim for 80+ optimization score**

### Post-Publish
1. **SEMrush:** Track rankings
2. **Surfer:** Audit and update
3. **Iterate based on performance**

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Don't Over-Optimize
AI tools suggest terms—use them naturally. Keyword stuffing still hurts.

### 2. Combine Tools
AI writing (Claude) + AI SEO (Surfer) = best results.

### 3. Add Human Value
AI optimizes for algorithms. Add expertise, stories, unique insights for humans.

### 4. Update Content
Use AI tools to identify optimization opportunities in existing content.
