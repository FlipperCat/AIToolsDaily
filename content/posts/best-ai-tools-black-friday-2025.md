## Deal-Hunting Tips

### 1. Don't Buy Monthly
Annual billing almost always saves money. If you'll use the tool, commit.

### 2. Stack Deals
Some tools allow discount codes + Black Friday pricing.

### 3. Check Email
Many deals go to existing users first. Check newsletters.

### 4. Extended Periods
Black Friday deals often run through Cyber Monday or beyond.

### 5. Try Before Buying
Use free trials before committing to annual plans.

## When to Skip

Don't buy AI tools just because they're discounted. Skip if:
- You haven't used the free tier
- You're not sure you need it
- It duplicates what you have
