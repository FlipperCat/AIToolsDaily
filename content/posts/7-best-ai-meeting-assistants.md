AI meeting assistants now handle transcription, summarization, action item extraction, and follow-up coordination automatically, freeing professionals to focus on actual discussion and decision-making. ## 1. Otter.ai

**Best For:** Meeting transcription and general recording

Otter.ai offers real-time transcription and automatic meeting notes across Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet. With excellent accuracy and searchable transcripts, it's the foundation of smart meeting management.

**Features:**
- Real-time transcription
- 99.1% accuracy rate
- Speaker identification
- Searchable transcripts
- Automatic summaries
- Keyword highlighting
- Integration with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet
- Collaborative note-taking
- Mobile app for on-the-go access

**Pricing:** Free tier (limited), Pro $8.99/month, Business $20/month

**Pros:**
- Exceptional transcription accuracy
- Calendar integration
- Free tier is genuinely useful
- Affordable pro pricing
- Great search functionality
- Speaker identification works well
- Works across platforms

**Cons:**
- Limited free tier (600 minutes/month)
- Manual action items still needed
- Integration limitations on free
- Support could be faster

**Who It's Best For:** Professionals with frequent meetings, teams needing searchable meeting archives, and anyone wanting reliable meeting recordings.
