# AI in 2025: What We Think Will Happen

As 2024 wraps up, here are our predictions for AI in 2025. We'll revisit this in December to see how we did.

## Confident Predictions

### AI Assistants Get Better at Following Instructions
The biggest frustration with current AI is inconsistency. Ask the same question twice, get different answers. We expect major improvements in reliability and instruction-following.

### Prices Continue Dropping
API costs fell 10x in 2024. Expect similar drops in 2025. This means more AI-powered apps and features everywhere.

### Local AI Becomes Practical
Running AI on your own computer is getting easier. By end of 2025, running a capable AI assistant locally (no cloud required) will be realistic for most users.

### Multi-Modal Gets Real
AI that handles text, images, audio, and video together - not as separate tools - will become standard. Think: upload a video, ask questions about it.

## Hopeful Predictions

### AI Coding Assistants Improve Dramatically
Current tools are helpful but inconsistent. We expect 2025 versions to understand entire codebases, not just single files.

### AI-Generated Content Gets Harder to Detect
This is a double-edged sword. Content quality will improve to where detection becomes nearly impossible. This will force new approaches to trust and verification.

### Personalized AI Becomes Normal
AI that genuinely learns your preferences and adapts over time. Not just ChatGPT remembering facts, but truly personalized assistance.

## What Won't Happen

### AGI Won't Arrive
Despite the hype, artificial general intelligence isn't coming in 2025. We'll see incremental improvements, not a singularity.

### AI Won't Replace Most Jobs
AI will change jobs, eliminate some tasks, but mass unemployment from AI? Not in 2025. Probably not in 2030 either.

### Perfect AI Video Won't Emerge
We'll see better AI video tools, but Hollywood-quality productions from text prompts? Not yet.

## What to Prepare For

### Learn Prompting (If You Haven't)
The skill of communicating with AI matters more each year.

### Expect AI in Your Tools
Your existing software will add AI features. Some will be useful, some will be marketing fluff.

### Budget for AI Subscriptions
$20-50/month for AI tools will become as normal as Netflix subscriptions.

### Stay Flexible
The tools that are best today might not be best in six months. Stay curious.

## Our Scorecard

We'll return to these predictions in December 2025. Our track record so far: about 70% accuracy on confident predictions, 40% on hopeful ones.

The only certain prediction: AI will continue surprising us.
