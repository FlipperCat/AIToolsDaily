# Best AI Tools for Job Search

Job hunting is brutal. AI makes it less so.

Here's the complete toolkit for 2025 job seekers.

## Resume Building

### Teal

**What it does:** AI-powered resume optimization

**Features:**
- Keyword matching for job descriptions
- ATS optimization
- Multiple resume versions
- Application tracking

**Best for:** Tailoring resumes to specific jobs

**Price:** Free tier, $29/month Pro

### Kickresume

**What it does:** AI resume and cover letter generator

**Features:**
- AI writing assistant
- Professional templates
- LinkedIn import
- Cover letter matching

**Best for:** Creating resumes from scratch

**Price:** Free tier, $19/month Pro

### ChatGPT/Claude for Resumes

**Use it for:**
- Rewriting bullet points
- Tailoring for specific roles
- Quantifying achievements
- Keywords optimization

**Prompt example:**
```
Rewrite this bullet point to be more impactful and quantified:
"Managed customer service team"

Context: Applying for Senior Customer Success Manager role
```

## Cover Letters

### ChatGPT/Claude

**Approach:**

**For:** Polishing your draft. Grammar, tone, clarity.

## The Process

### Week 1: Foundation

1. Update resume with AI help
2. Optimize LinkedIn profile
3. Set up job tracking system
4. Research target companies

### Week 2: Applications

1. Apply with tailored resumes
2. Write customized cover letters
3. Track everything
4. Network strategically

### Week 3+: Interviews

1. Practice with AI mock interviews
2. Research companies deeply
3. Prepare specific examples
4. Follow up professionally

## Tips for Using AI in Job Search

### 1. Don't Sound Like AI

Edit everything. Add your voice. Hiring managers spot generic AI.

### 2. Verify Everything

AI can hallucinate details. Check your resume facts.

### 3. Use AI as a Tool, Not a Replacement

AI drafts. You refine. Your judgment matters.

### 4. Personalization Wins

Tailored applications beat volume. Use AI to personalize, not mass-produce.

### 5. Prepare, Don't Memorize

Use AI to prepare answers, but don't memorize scripts.

## What AI Can't Do

- Replace genuine enthusiasm
- Create real experience
- Build actual relationships
- Guarantee success

AI helps you compete. You still need to perform.
