## Proposals and Estimating

### ChatGPT for Client Proposals

Draft professional proposals:

**Scope of work:**
> "Write a scope of work for a kitchen remodel including demolition, new cabinets, countertops, flooring, and electrical updates. Make it clear what's included and what's not."

**Bid clarifications:**
> "A homeowner asked why my bid is higher than a competitor's. Help me draft a professional response explaining value, warranty, and what might be missing from lower bids."

**Change order explanations:**
> "Draft a change order explanation for additional framing work discovered after opening walls. Include why it's necessary and cost justification."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Bids

Detailed proposal documents:

> "Create a comprehensive bid document for a commercial office renovation. Include sections for: scope, timeline, payment schedule, allowances, exclusions, and terms."

Claude handles longer, more detailed documents well.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Joist

Estimating and invoicing:

- Quick estimates
- Professional invoices
- Client approval
- Payment processing

Simple estimating for smaller projects.

**Cost:** Free tier / $15/mo Pro

## Field Reality

Construction happens on job sites, not at desks.

Good AI tools for contractors:
- Work on phones
- Don't require constant attention
- Save time on paperwork
- Improve client communication

What AI won't do:
- Show up on time
- Do quality work
- Manage subs effectively
- Build relationships

AI handles the office work. You handle the job site.
