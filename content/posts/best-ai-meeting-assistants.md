# Best AI Meeting Assistants: Real Testing Results

I tested 6 AI meeting assistants over two months of actual work meetings - client calls, team syncs, interviews.

Here's what actually works.

## What These Tools Do

AI meeting assistants:
- Join your video calls automatically
- Transcribe what's said
- Identify who's speaking
- Generate summaries
- Create searchable records

The value: Never miss details, find information later, skip relistening to recordings.

## The Tools I Tested

1. **Otter.ai** - Most popular
2. **Fireflies.ai** - Good integrations
3. **Grain** - Sales-focused
4. **tl;dv** - Free option
5. **Fathom** - Note-taking focused
6. **Avoma** - Enterprise features

## Otter.ai - Best Overall

**Price:** Free (300 min/mo) | $17/month Pro

### What I liked:

**Transcription accuracy:** 88-92% in my testing. Best among consumer options.

**Real-time transcription:** Watch text appear during the meeting. Useful for catching things.

**Search:** "What did Sarah say about budget?" - finds it in seconds.

**OtterPilot:** Joins meetings automatically, no manual start.

**Summaries:** Decent AI summaries of key points.

### What I didn't like:

**Speaker identification:** Good but not perfect. Requires training.

**Mobile app:** Functional but clunky.

**Export options:** Limited on lower tiers.

### Best for:
- Individual professionals
- Teams who need reliable transcription
- Anyone wanting to search past meetings

### Verdict: 8/10
The go-to choice for most people. Good accuracy, fair price, easy to use.

## Fireflies.ai - Best Integrations

**Price:** Free (limited) | $10/month Pro | $19/month Business

### What I liked:

**CRM integration:** Syncs meeting notes to Salesforce, HubSpot automatically.

**Multiple bot options:** Can send bot or use browser extension.

**Topic detection:** Auto-categorizes discussion topics.

**Team features:** Shared workspace, meeting analytics.

### What I didn't like:

**Accuracy:** 85-88%. Slightly below Otter.

**Interface:** More complex than necessary.

**Free tier:** Very limited.

### Best for:
- Sales teams
- Anyone using CRM extensively
- Teams wanting meeting analytics

### Verdict: 7.5/10
Good for teams, especially sales. Integrations are the selling point.

## Grain - Best for Sales

**Price:** Free (limited) | $19/month Pro

### What I liked:

**Highlight clips:** Easily clip key moments to share.

**Salesforce/HubSpot native:** Built for sales workflows.

**Coaching features:** Tag moments for review, training.

**Snippet sharing:** Send specific moments, not whole recordings.

### What I didn't like:

**Accuracy:** 82-86%. Lower than Otter/Fireflies.

**Price:** Expensive for individual use.

**Learning curve:** Takes time to use effectively.

### Best for:
- Sales teams who share call clips
- Sales coaching and training
- Revenue teams specifically

### Verdict: 7/10
Specialized for sales. If that's you, it's great. Otherwise, too expensive.

## tl;dv - Best Free Option

**Price:** Free (generous) | $20/month Pro

### What I liked:

**Free tier:** Unlimited recordings, decent transcription.

**Easy setup:** Chrome extension, minimal configuration.

**Timestamp notes:** Mark moments during call.

**Integrations:** Works with Notion, Slack, etc.

### What I didn't like:

**Accuracy:** 80-85%. Below paid competitors.

**Features:** Basic compared to paid options.

**Speaker ID:** Struggles with multiple speakers.

### Best for:
- Budget-conscious users
- Anyone wanting to try AI meeting tools
- Light meeting loads

### Verdict: 7/10
Best free option. Try this before paying for anything.

## Fathom - Best Note-Taking

**Price:** Free (limited) | $15/month Pro

### What I liked:

**Action items:** Automatically extracts to-dos.

**Summary quality:** Best AI summaries in my testing.

**Highlight moments:** Easy to mark and find key points.

**Clean interface:** Simple, not overwhelming.

### What I didn't like:

**Accuracy:** 83-87%. Middle of the pack.

**Integration limits:** Fewer than competitors.

**Team features:** Limited collaboration.

### Best for:
- Individual users wanting action items
- People who hate meeting notes
- Those wanting clean summaries

### Verdict: 7.5/10
Good for personal productivity. Less suited for teams.

## Avoma - Best Enterprise

**Price:** $19/month Starter | $49/month Plus

### What I liked:

**Revenue intelligence:** Built for sales/CS teams.

**Coaching scorecards:** Rate call quality.

**Pipeline visibility:** See deal discussions.

**Compliance features:** Enterprise-ready.

### What I didn't like:

**Price:** Most expensive option.

**Complexity:** Overkill for small teams.

**Setup:** Requires significant configuration.

### Best for:
- Enterprise sales teams
- Revenue operations
- Companies needing compliance

### Verdict: 7/10
Good enterprise tool. Way overkill for small teams.

### For most people: **Otter.ai**

Best accuracy, reasonable price, easy to use. Start with free tier.

### For sales teams: **Fireflies.ai** or **Grain**

CRM integration matters more than marginal accuracy differences.

### For budget-conscious: **tl;dv**

Genuinely useful free tier. See if AI meeting tools help before paying.

### For enterprise: **Evaluate all**

At this scale, get demos. Security, compliance, and team features matter more.

## Tips for Better Results

### Improve accuracy:
- Use good microphones
- Minimize background noise
- Train speaker identification
- Speak clearly (obvious but matters)

### Get more value:
- Actually search past meetings
- Share relevant clips with team
- Review summaries same-day
- Use action item extraction

### Privacy considerations:
- Always inform participants
- Know your local laws
- Check company policy
- Consider client sensitivities

## Bottom Line

AI meeting assistants work. They save time, capture details, and make meetings searchable.

**Worth it if:**
- 5+ meetings per week
- Need to reference past discussions
- Miss important details regularly
- Want searchable meeting history

**Not worth it if:**
- Few meetings
- Never need to look back
- Simple meetings that don't need notes

Start with tl;dv (free) or Otter (free tier). See if you actually use the transcripts before paying.
