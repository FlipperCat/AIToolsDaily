## Recruiting & Hiring

### Paradox (Olivia)

The leading recruiting chatbot:

- 24/7 candidate engagement
- Interview scheduling automation
- Pre-screening questions
- Multi-language support

Candidates interact via text, web, or WhatsApp. Olivia handles the back-and-forth.

**Results:** Companies report 90% reduction in time-to-schedule and 3x more applicants completing the process.

**Cost:** Custom (mid-market to enterprise)

### HireVue

Video interviewing with AI assessment:

- Structured video interviews
- Game-based assessments
- AI-assisted evaluation
- Builder for interview guides

The AI features help standardize evaluation, though some controversy exists around AI scoring.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing

### Eightfold AI

Talent intelligence platform:

- Skills-based candidate matching
- Internal mobility recommendations
- Diversity insights
- Career pathing

Goes beyond keyword matching to understand actual capabilities.

**Cost:** Enterprise

### HiredScore

AI-powered candidate screening:

- Resume scoring
- Bias detection
- Compliance monitoring
- ATS integration

Helps surface qualified candidates faster while reducing bias.

**Cost:** Enterprise

## Job Descriptions & Employer Branding

### Textio

AI that improves job postings:

- Real-time writing guidance
- Gender-neutral language suggestions
- Performance predictions
- Competitor benchmarking

Shows you exactly which phrases attract diverse candidates and which turn people away.

**Cost:** ~$12,000/year

### ChatGPT for Job Descriptions

Solid free alternative:

**Prompt example:**
> "Write a job description for a Senior Software Engineer. The role requires Python and AWS experience. Our culture is collaborative and flexible. Avoid gendered language and corporate jargon. Include a section on growth opportunities."

Then refine:
> "Make it more concise and add a compelling opening line about our mission."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Everyday HR Tasks

### Claude for HR Writing

Handle complex HR tasks:

**Policy Writing:**
> "Draft a remote work policy for a 200-person tech company. Include eligibility, equipment, communication expectations, and home office setup requirements."

**Employee Communications:**
> "Write an announcement about changes to our healthcare benefits. Tone should be transparent and empathetic. Highlight what's improving."

**Investigation Notes:**
> "Create a template for documenting workplace investigation interviews."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo for Pro

### ChatGPT for HR

**Interview Questions:**
> "Generate 10 behavioral interview questions for a customer service manager role, focusing on conflict resolution and team leadership."

**Offer Letters:**
> "Draft an offer letter for a Marketing Coordinator at $65,000/year with standard benefits, 3 weeks PTO, and a start date of [date]."

**Exit Interview Questions:**
> "Create exit interview questions that will uncover honest feedback about management and company culture."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Ethical Considerations

### Bias in AI Hiring
- AI can perpetuate bias if trained on biased data
- Audit algorithms regularly
- Maintain human oversight in hiring decisions
- Be transparent with candidates about AI use

### Employee Monitoring
- Balance productivity insights with privacy
- Clear communication about what's tracked
- Comply with local regulations
- Focus on outcomes, not surveillance

### Data Privacy
- GDPR, CCPA, and local law compliance
- Clear data retention policies
- Secure handling of sensitive information
- Vendor due diligence
