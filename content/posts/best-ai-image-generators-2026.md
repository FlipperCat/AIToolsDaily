## 2. DALL-E 3 - Best for Editing & ChatGPT Users

**Price:** Included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) | [Try DALL-E 3 →](https://chat.openai.com)

DALL-E 3 is built into ChatGPT. That integration is its superpower.

### Why DALL-E 3 Stands Out

- **Conversational prompting** - Describe what you want naturally
- **Edit images** - "Remove the background" or "Make it more blue"
- **Understands context** - Uses your chat history
- **No separate subscription** - Included with ChatGPT Plus

### Best For

- Quick iterations
- Non-designers
- Integrated workflows
- Image editing

### Limitations

- Less artistic than Midjourney
- Fewer style options
- Rate limited
- Can refuse certain prompts

{{< pros-cons pros="Included with ChatGPT, Natural language prompts, Built-in editing, No learning curve" cons="Less artistic quality, Rate limits, Strict content policy, Can't match Midjourney aesthetics" >}}

**Verdict:** If you have ChatGPT Plus, you already have DALL-E 3. Use it for quick tasks. Add Midjourney for quality work.

## 3. Stable Diffusion - Best for Control & Free Use

**Price:** Free (self-hosted) or $10+/month (cloud) | [Try Stable Diffusion →](https://stability.ai)

Stable Diffusion is open-source. Run it free on your computer, or use cloud services.

### Why Choose Stable Diffusion

- **Free forever** - Download and run locally
- **Full control** - No content restrictions
- **Customizable** - Fine-tune on your own data
- **ControlNet** - Precise pose and composition control
- **Inpainting** - Edit specific image areas

### Best For

- Technical users
- Custom model training
- Unrestricted content
- Budget-conscious creators

### The Learning Curve

Stable Diffusion is powerful but complex:
- Local setup requires decent GPU
- Many settings to configure
- Best results need custom models

**Cloud alternatives:** RunDiffusion, Leonardo AI, Automatic1111 cloud

{{< pros-cons pros="Free and open-source, Maximum control, Custom fine-tuning, Active development" cons="Steep learning curve, Requires GPU for local, Quality varies by model, Time investment needed" >}}

**Verdict:** Worth learning if you need control or want to avoid subscriptions. But expect 10+ hours to get competent.

## 4. Leonardo AI - Best Free Option

**Price:** Free tier / $12-60/month | [Try Leonardo AI →](https://leonardo.ai)

Leonardo offers generous free usage with quality approaching Midjourney.

### Free Tier Includes

- 150 images/day
- Multiple models
- Basic editing features
- Commercial usage rights

### Best For

- Testing AI image generation
- Consistent characters (Phoenix model)
- Game assets
- Budget creators

{{< pros-cons pros="Generous free tier, Good quality, Multiple models, User-friendly interface" cons="Not quite Midjourney quality, Queue times on free, Some features paywalled" >}}

**Verdict:** Best way to start with AI images. Graduate to Midjourney when you hit limits.

{{< cta text="Try Leonardo Free" url="https://leonardo.ai" style="primary" >}}

## 5. Adobe Firefly - Best for Adobe Users

**Price:** Included with Creative Cloud / $5/month standalone | [Try Firefly →](https://firefly.adobe.com)

Adobe Firefly integrates directly into Photoshop, Illustrator, and other Adobe apps.

### Key Features

- **Generative Fill** - Remove/add elements in Photoshop
- **Text Effects** - Stylized typography
- **Commercially safe** - Trained on licensed content
- **Adobe integration** - Native in Creative Cloud

### Best For

- Existing Adobe users
- Commercial projects requiring licensing clarity
- Photo editing workflows
- Enterprise teams

{{< pros-cons pros="Adobe integration, Commercially safe, Great for editing, Professional workflow" cons="Less creative than Midjourney, Requires Adobe subscription, Limited standalone value" >}}
