AI tool subscriptions add up fast. $20 here, $30 there — suddenly you're spending $200/month on tools you barely use.

Here's how to get the value without the waste.

## Audit What You Actually Use

### The Brutal Honesty Test

For each AI subscription:
1. When did you last use it?
2. How often do you use it?
3. What would you lose without it?

If you can't answer quickly, that's a sign.

### The Usage Check

Most tools show usage stats:
- ChatGPT: Check your history
- Midjourney: See your image count
- Otter: Check minutes used

If you're paying for unlimited but using 10% of free tier limits, downgrade.

## Free Tiers Are Better Than You Think

### What You Get Free

| Tool | Free Tier |
|------|-----------|
| ChatGPT | GPT-3.5, generous limits |
| Claude | Daily limits, good model |
| Perplexity | Basic search, limited Pro |
| Canva | Core features, some limits |
| Notion | Personal use, AI features |
| Otter.ai | 300 min/month |
| Fathom | Unlimited calls |
| Grammarly | Basic grammar |

For casual use, free tiers often suffice.

### When to Stay Free

- You use it occasionally (weekly or less)
- The free tier covers your needs
- You can work around limitations
- It's not core to your work

## One AI Assistant, Not Three

### The Subscription Trap

- ChatGPT Plus: $20/month
- Claude Pro: $20/month
- Gemini Advanced: $20/month
- **Total:** $60/month

Do you actually need all three?

### The Solution

Pick one as your primary. Use free tiers for others.

**My approach:** Claude Pro (primary) + ChatGPT Free (backup) + Perplexity Free (research)

**Monthly cost:** $20 instead of $60

## Annual vs Monthly

### The Math

| Tool | Monthly | Annual | Savings |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| ChatGPT Plus | $20/mo | $200/yr | $40/year |
| Midjourney Basic | $10/mo | $96/yr | $24/year |
| Grammarly | $30/mo | $144/yr | $216/year |

Annual plans save 15-40%.

### When It Makes Sense

- You've used it for 3+ months
- You expect to use it all year
- You can afford the upfront cost

### When It Doesn't

- You're still testing
- Your needs might change
- The tool is new/unstable

## Bundle Deals

### Google One + Gemini

Google One (2TB storage) includes Gemini Advanced:
- 2TB cloud storage
- Gemini Ultra
- VPN included
- $20/month

If you need cloud storage anyway, this is value.

### Microsoft 365 + Copilot

Microsoft 365 Personal:
- Office apps
- OneDrive storage
- Copilot access (in apps)
- ~$7/month

### Adobe Creative Cloud

Adobe apps now include:
- Firefly (image generation)
- AI features throughout
- Part of existing subscription

Don't pay separately for AI tools you might already have.

## Sharing (When Allowed)

### Family/Team Plans

| Tool | Individual | Family/Team | Per Person |

| ChatGPT Team | $25/user | $25/user | $25 |
| Midjourney | $30/mo | Unofficial sharing | ~$15 |

### What's Allowed

- Check terms of service
- Family plans are for families
- Business plans are for businesses
- Don't share accounts that prohibit it

## DIY Alternatives

### Open Source Options

| Instead of... | Use... | Savings |
|---------------|--------|---------|
| Midjourney | Stable Diffusion (local) | $30/mo |
| ChatGPT Plus | Local LLM (Ollama) | $20/mo |
| Grammarly | LanguageTool | $25/mo |
| Jasper | ChatGPT + prompts | $49/mo |

Requires technical knowledge but can save significantly.

### API vs Subscription

ChatGPT API often costs less than Plus:
- Plus: $20/month flat
- API: Pay per use

If you use <$20 worth of API calls, the API is cheaper.

## Downgrade Strategically

### Seasonal Usage

Use AI tools more during busy periods?

- Subscribe during busy months
- Cancel/pause during slow periods
- Re-subscribe when needed

Most tools make this easy.

### Feature-Based Decisions

Premium features you might not need:
- Priority access (free is often fast enough)
- Higher limits (you might not hit free limits)
- Team features (if you work solo)
- Integrations (if you don't use them)

---

## The Monthly Review

Every month, spend 5 minutes:

1. Look at credit card AI charges
2. Ask: Did I use this?
3. Ask: Was it worth it?
4. Cancel or downgrade what doesn't pass

$5 saved monthly = $60/year
