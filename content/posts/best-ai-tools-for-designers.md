## Concept & Ideation

### Midjourney

The creative powerhouse:

- Concept exploration
- Mood boards
- Style development
- Art direction reference

Midjourney excels at creating visual directions you can refine.

**Prompt example:**
> "Corporate tech website hero image, abstract 3D shapes, gradient blue and purple, clean minimalist style, professional, depth of field --ar 16:9 --v 6"

**Cost:** $10/mo Basic, $30/mo Standard

### Adobe Firefly

Adobe's AI integrated into Creative Cloud:

- Generative fill in Photoshop
- Text effects
- Vector recoloring in Illustrator
- Template generation

Built into tools you already use.

**Cost:** Included with Creative Cloud

### DALL-E 3

Accessible via ChatGPT:

- Quick concept sketches
- Reference imagery
- Icon exploration
- Social graphics

Describe what you need in conversation.

**Cost:** Included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo)

## UI/UX Design

### Figma AI

AI features inside Figma:

- Auto-layout suggestions
- Rename layers intelligently
- Generate variations
- Visual search

Native to your workflow.

**Cost:** Figma Professional ($15/mo/editor)

### Galileo AI

Generate UI from text:

- Describe a screen
- Get editable designs
- Export to Figma
- Iterate with prompts

Still in development, but promising for rapid prototyping.

**Cost:** Waitlist / Custom

### Magician (Figma Plugin)

AI assistant for Figma:

- Generate icons
- Write copy
- Create images
- Magic suggestions

Little AI helper inside Figma.

**Cost:** $5/month

### Uizard

Sketch to UI:

- Hand-drawn wireframe → digital design
- Screenshot → editable design
- Text prompt → UI components
- Collaborative editing

Great for rapid prototyping.

**Cost:** Free tier, $19/mo Pro

## The Designer's Advantage

AI generates options. Designers curate excellence.

What AI can't do:
- Understand client psychology
- Navigate stakeholder politics
- Make strategic brand decisions
- Know when to break rules
- Defend design choices

What AI does well:
- Generate variations fast
- Handle repetitive production
- Create starting points
- Remove tedious tasks

The best designers use AI to spend more time on what matters: strategy, refinement, and client relationships.
