# Best AI Website Builders (2024)

Describe your website. AI builds it. That's the promise.

Here's which tools deliver.

## Top AI Website Builders

### Wix ADI

**Best for:** Small businesses wanting a complete solution

**How it works:**
1. Answer questions about your business
2. AI generates a complete website
3. Customize with drag-and-drop

**Strengths:**
- Full website ecosystem (hosting, domains)
- E-commerce ready
- Good template quality

**Weaknesses:**
- Lock-in to Wix platform
- AI-generated sites can feel generic

**Price:** Free tier, $16+/month premium

### Framer AI

**Best for:** Designers and modern websites

**How it works:**
1. Describe your site in text
2. AI generates landing page
3. Refine with powerful editor

**Strengths:**
- Beautiful default designs
- Advanced customization
- Great animations

**Weaknesses:**
- Learning curve for editor
- Better for landing pages than full sites

**Price:** Free tier, $15+/month

### Durable

**Best for:** Quick business websites

**How it works:**
1. Enter business type and location
2. AI generates complete site in seconds
3. Edit and publish

**Strengths:**
- Incredibly fast
- Includes CRM and invoicing
- Simple pricing

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited customization
- Sites look similar

**Price:** $15/month

### 10Web AI

**Best for:** WordPress users

**How it works:**
1. AI generates WordPress site
2. Full WordPress flexibility
3. AI-assisted editing

**Strengths:**
- WordPress ecosystem
- More customizable than most
- Good for SEO

**Weaknesses:**
- WordPress complexity
- Hosting additional

**Price:** $10+/month

### Hostinger AI Builder

**Best for:** Budget-conscious users

**How it works:**
1. AI generates site
2. Drag-and-drop customization
3. Hosting included

**Strengths:**
- Very affordable
- Hosting included
- Simple process

**Weaknesses:**
- Less sophisticated than competitors
- Limited templates

**Price:** $3/month (with hosting)

## How AI Website Building Works

### Text-to-Site

Describe what you want. AI interprets and creates.

**Example prompt:** "A modern portfolio website for a freelance photographer with dark theme and minimalist design"

### Question-Based

Answer questions about your business. AI fills in the blanks.

### Template Enhancement

Choose a template. AI customizes colors, content, images.

## What AI Does Well

- Initial layout generation
- Color scheme selection
- Content suggestions
- Basic copywriting
- Image selection

## What AI Struggles With

- Unique, creative designs
- Complex functionality
- Perfect brand alignment
- Nuanced copy
