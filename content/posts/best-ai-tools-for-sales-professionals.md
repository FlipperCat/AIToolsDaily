## Conversation Intelligence

### Gong

The gold standard:

- Records and transcribes calls
- Identifies winning patterns
- Tracks competitor mentions
- Coaches based on data

Gong tells you exactly what top performers say differently. Then you can replicate it.

**Key insight:** Companies using Gong report 15-25% higher win rates.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing (~$100-150/user/month)

### Chorus.ai (ZoomInfo)

Strong alternative to Gong:

- Call recording and analysis
- Deal intelligence
- Coaching scorecards
- CRM integration

Now part of ZoomInfo, which adds prospecting data.

**Cost:** Enterprise (often bundled with ZoomInfo)

### Fireflies.ai

Budget-friendly option:

- Meeting transcription
- AI summaries
- Action item extraction
- Searchable conversations

Good for teams not ready for enterprise pricing.

**Cost:** Free tier, $18/mo Pro

## Prospecting & Data

### Apollo.io

Best value in prospecting:

- 275M+ contact database
- Email verification
- Sequencing built-in
- Chrome extension

The free tier is genuinely useful. Paid plans unlock scale.

**Cost:** Free (limited), $49/mo Basic, $99/mo Professional

### ZoomInfo

The enterprise data leader:

- Most comprehensive B2B database
- Intent data (who's actively buying)
- Technographics (what tech they use)
- Org charts

Expensive but the data is unmatched.

**Cost:** Starts ~$15,000/year

### LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Social selling essential:

- Advanced search filters
- Lead recommendations
- InMail credits
- CRM sync

If you're in B2B, you need this.

**Cost:** $99/month (Core)

### Seamless.AI

Real-time contact finding:

- Finds emails and phones live
- Chrome extension
- CRM integration
- Unlimited searches on higher tiers

Good for high-volume prospecting.

**Cost:** Free (limited), $147/mo Basic

## Sales Engagement

### Outreach

The market leader:

- Multi-channel sequences
- AI-powered send times
- Task automation
- Revenue intelligence

Enterprise-grade sales engagement.

**Cost:** Custom (enterprise)

### Salesloft

Strong Outreach competitor:

- Cadence management
- Call recording
- Meeting scheduling
- Analytics

Some prefer Salesloft's interface and support.

**Cost:** Custom (enterprise)

### Reply.io

Mid-market alternative:

- Email sequences
- LinkedIn automation
- AI email assistant
- A/B testing

More accessible pricing than enterprise tools.

## Email Writing & Coaching

### Lavender

AI email coach:

- Real-time writing suggestions
- Personalization prompts
- Mobile optimization
- Performance tracking

Tells you exactly how to improve each email before you send.

**Cost:** Free basic, $29/mo Pro

### ChatGPT for Sales Emails

Personalization at scale:

**Prompt example:**
> "Write a cold email to [Title] at [Company]. They recently [trigger event]. My product helps [value prop]. Keep it under 100 words, casual tone, end with a soft CTA."

> "Rewrite this follow-up email to sound more conversational and less salesy. Add a personalized observation about their recent product launch."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Copy.ai

Sales copy generation:

- Cold email templates
- Follow-up sequences
- LinkedIn messages
- Sales scripts

AI-generated starting points you customize.

**Cost:** Free tier, $49/mo Pro

## CRM & Revenue Intelligence

### HubSpot Sales Hub

CRM with AI features:

- Lead scoring
- Predictive deal insights
- Email tracking
- Meeting scheduling
- Sequences

Great for SMB, scales to enterprise.

**Cost:** Free basic, $50/mo Starter, $500/mo Professional

### Salesforce Einstein

AI layer on Salesforce:

- Lead scoring
- Opportunity insights
- Activity capture
- Forecasting

If you're on Salesforce, Einstein is the natural AI add-on.

**Cost:** Varies by Salesforce edition

### Clari

Revenue intelligence platform:

- Pipeline inspection
- Forecast accuracy
- Deal health scoring
- Activity capture

Helps revenue leaders see what's actually happening in pipeline.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing

## Research & Preparation

### Claude for Sales Prep

Deep research assistant:

> "I'm meeting with the VP of Marketing at [Company] tomorrow. Based on their recent earnings call and press releases, what challenges are they likely facing? What questions should I ask?"

> "Analyze this company's 10-K filing and identify 3 potential pain points our software could address."

Claude's long context window handles complex research.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Pro

### ChatGPT for Quick Research

Faster research cycles:

> "What are the top 3 priorities for CFOs in the manufacturing industry in 2026?"

> "Summarize the competitive landscape in [industry] in 2 paragraphs."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Meeting Scheduling

### Calendly

Eliminate scheduling friction:

- Share availability link
- Automatic time zone handling
- Calendar sync
- Reminders

One less barrier to getting meetings.

**Cost:** Free basic, $12/mo Professional

### Chili Piper

Revenue-focused scheduling:

- Instant meeting booking from forms
- Round-robin distribution
- Handoff scheduling
- Salesforce integration

Higher conversion from inbound leads.

**Cost:** Starts $30/user/month
