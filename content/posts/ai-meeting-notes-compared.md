Meeting notes shouldn't be a full-time job. AI transcription tools have gotten good enough to handle this.

Here's how the top options compare.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Otter.ai | Fireflies.ai | Fathom |
|---------|----------|--------------|--------|
| **Accuracy** | Very Good | Very Good | Excellent |
| **Free tier** | 300 min/mo | 800 min/mo | Unlimited calls |
| **Pro price** | $17/mo | $10/mo | $19/mo |
| **Best for** | Individual use | Sales teams | Meetings focus |

| **Real-time** | Yes | Yes | Yes |

---

## Otter.ai

### Overview
The original AI transcription tool. Well-established with solid features.

### Strengths
- **Real-time transcription:** See words as they're spoken
- **Speaker identification:** Knows who said what
- **Search:** Find anything anyone said
- **Highlights:** Mark important moments
- **Sharing:** Easy to share transcripts

### Weaknesses
- **Limited free tier:** 300 minutes/month
- **Cost:** $17/month adds up
- **Accuracy:** Good but not perfect

### Best For
- Individual professionals
- Journalists
- Students
- Anyone who needs searchable meeting archives

### Pricing
- Free: 300 min/month
- Pro: $17/month (1,200 min)
- Business: $30/user/month

---

## Fireflies.ai

### Overview
CRM-focused meeting intelligence. Strong for sales teams.

### Strengths
- **CRM integration:** Auto-logs to Salesforce, HubSpot
- **AI summaries:** Smart meeting summaries
- **Action items:** Extracts tasks automatically
- **Analytics:** Meeting metrics and insights
- **Generous free tier:** 800 minutes/month

### Weaknesses
- **Interface:** Can feel cluttered
- **Learning curve:** More features = more complexity
- **Accuracy:** Similar to others

### Best For
- Sales teams
- Anyone using CRM
- Teams wanting meeting analytics
- Organizations tracking conversations

### Pricing
- Free: 800 min/month (limited features)
- Pro: $10/month
- Business: $19/month
- Enterprise: Custom

---

## Fathom

### Overview
Simple, focused on making meetings more useful.

### Strengths
- **Generous free tier:** Unlimited calls
- **Accuracy:** Best-in-class transcription
- **Simplicity:** Clean, focused interface
- **Clip creation:** Save important moments easily
- **Summary quality:** Excellent meeting summaries

### Weaknesses
- **Fewer integrations:** Less connected than Fireflies
- **Newer:** Less established
- **Features:** More focused, fewer bells and whistles

### Best For
- Anyone tired of complex tools
- Individual users
- Small teams
- People who want "just transcription done well"

### Pricing
- Free: Unlimited (basic features)
- Premium: $19/month (full features)
- Team: $29/user/month

## Real-World Accuracy

All three handle:
- Clear audio: 95%+ accuracy
- Multiple speakers: Good
- Technical jargon: Acceptable
- Accents: Good but variable

Accuracy drops with:
- Background noise
- Multiple people talking
- Very fast speech
- Heavy accents

---

## Privacy Considerations

These tools record your conversations. Consider:

- **Who has access?** Team settings matter
- **Where's data stored?** Check their policies
- **Consent:** Always inform meeting participants
- **Sensitive topics:** Maybe don't record everything

Most offer:
- Recording notifications
- Data deletion options
- Enterprise security (paid tiers)

## The Combined Approach

Some people use multiple:
- **Fathom** for internal meetings (unlimited free)
- **Fireflies** for sales calls (CRM integration)

At their price points, having both is still reasonable.

---

## Do You Even Need This?

Before subscribing, ask:
- Do I take meeting notes now? If not, will you review transcripts?
- How often do I search past meetings?
- Is my meeting volume worth the cost?

If you're in 2+ meetings daily and reference them later, these tools pay for themselves.

If you're in occasional meetings and rarely look back, maybe not.
