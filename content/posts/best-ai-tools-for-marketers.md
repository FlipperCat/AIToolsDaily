## Content Creation

### ChatGPT

The content marketer's daily driver:

- Blog post outlines
- Social media copy
- Email drafts
- Content repurposing

**Prompt examples:**

> "Create a detailed outline for a blog post about email marketing best practices. Target audience: small business owners. Include 5 main sections with 3 sub-points each."

> "Turn this 2,000-word blog post into 10 LinkedIn posts. Vary the formats: some questions, some tips, some quotes."

**Cost:** Free tier works, $20/mo for GPT-4

### Jasper

Purpose-built for marketing:

- Brand voice customization
- Campaign workflows
- Template library
- Team collaboration

Jasper understands marketing contexts better than general AI.

**Cost:** $49/mo Creator, $125/mo Pro

### Claude

For strategic thinking:

- Marketing strategy analysis
- Competitive research
- Campaign planning
- Long-form content review

Claude excels at nuanced, strategic work.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Pro

## SEO & Content Optimization

### Surfer SEO

Content optimization engine:

- Real-time SEO scoring
- Content structure recommendations
- NLP keyword suggestions
- Competitor analysis

Write content that ranks.

**Cost:** $89/mo Essential, $179/mo Advanced

### Clearscope

Enterprise-grade optimization:

- AI content recommendations
- Comprehensive keyword analysis
- Content grading
- Google Docs integration

Premium pricing, premium results.

**Cost:** $170/mo Essentials

### Semrush

All-in-one SEO platform:

- Keyword research
- Site auditing
- Backlink analysis
- Competitor tracking
- AI writing assistant

The most comprehensive SEO toolkit.

**Cost:** $130/mo Pro, $250/mo Guru

### Frase

Research + write workflow:

- SERP analysis
- AI content generation
- Answer engine optimization
- Content briefs

Good for teams focused on topical authority.

**Cost:** $15/mo Basic, $45/mo Team

## Video Marketing

### Synthesia

AI video without cameras:

- AI avatars deliver your script
- 140+ languages
- No filming required
- Quick turnaround

Great for training videos, product explainers, updates.

**Cost:** $29/mo Personal, $89/mo Business

### Descript

Video editing democratized:

- Edit video like a document
- AI transcription
- Overdub (clone your voice)
- Remove filler words

Text-based video editing is a game-changer.

**Cost:** Free tier, $12/mo Creator

### Pictory

Long-form to short-form:

- Turn blogs into videos
- Auto-caption videos
- Create highlight reels
- Stock footage library

Repurpose content into video format.

**Cost:** $19/mo Starter

## Social Media

### Buffer

Simple scheduling:

- Multi-platform posting
- AI writing assistant
- Best time suggestions
- Link tracking

Clean interface, gets the job done.

**Cost:** Free (3 channels), $6/mo/channel

### Lately

AI social from long content:

- Analyzes your best posts
- Generates variations
- Learns your brand voice
- Bulk content creation

Turn one piece of content into dozens of social posts.

**Cost:** $49/mo Starter

### Hootsuite

Enterprise social management:

- Comprehensive scheduling
- Listening and monitoring
- Analytics
- Team workflows

More robust for larger teams.

- AI subject line suggestions

## Content Workflow Example

**Monday:** Use Claude to plan week's content themes
**Tuesday:** Draft blog post with ChatGPT, optimize with Surfer
**Wednesday:** Create visuals in Canva, generate social posts with Lately
**Thursday:** Schedule social in Buffer, set up email in Mailchimp
**Friday:** Analyze performance, brief next week

AI touches every step, but you're still the strategist.
