# Runway Gen-2: Text-to-Video is Here

First came AI images. Now: AI video.

Runway's Gen-2 generates video clips from text. We've been testing it.

## What is Runway Gen-2?

Runway Gen-2 is a text-to-video AI. Describe what you want to see, and it generates a short video clip.

**Capabilities:**
- Text to video
- Image to video
- Video to video (style transfer)
- 4-second clips (extendable)

## What We Tested

### Text to Video

**Prompt:** "A golden retriever running through a field of flowers, sunny day"

**Result:** Recognizable dog, running motion, flowers visible. Not photorealistic, but clearly what we asked for.

**Quality:** 6/10 — Dreamlike, sometimes surreal, but impressive.

### Image to Video

**Input:** Static photo
**Prompt:** "Make it wave gently"

**Result:** Subtle motion added. Natural-looking for simple movements.

**Quality:** 7/10 — Best use case right now.

### Complex Scenes

**Prompt:** "A chef cooking in a busy restaurant kitchen"

**Result:** Identifiable as a kitchen, but details are fuzzy. Human movements are awkward.

**Quality:** 4/10 — Complex scenes are harder.

## What It Does Well

- **Atmospheric footage** — Landscapes, abstract visuals
- **Simple motion** — Panning, zooming, gentle movement
- **Stylized content** — Artistic, non-realistic video
- **B-roll generation** — Generic footage for projects

## What It Doesn't Do Well

- **Human faces** — Uncanny valley territory
- **Complex action** — Multi-person scenes get weird
- **Precise control** — You get what you get
- **Long duration** — 4-second clips, not movies

## Practical Use Cases

### Content Creators

## Pricing

Runway uses a credit system:

- **Free tier:** Limited credits to try
- **Standard:** $12/month (625 credits)
- **Pro:** $28/month (2,250 credits)
- **Unlimited:** $76/month

Each 4-second clip costs credits based on resolution and model.

## How to Get Started

1. Sign up at runway.ml
2. Go to Gen-2
3. Enter a text prompt or upload an image
4. Generate and iterate

**Tip:** Start with simple prompts. Add detail gradually.

## The Reality Check

Let's be honest: Gen-2 is impressive but limited.

**What you imagine:** Full cinematic scenes from text
**What you get:** 4-second abstract-ish clips

It's early. The technology is real. The applications are specific.

## When to Use Gen-2

**Good for:**
- Quick visual concepts
- Social media content
- Artistic projects
- When good enough is good enough

**Not for:**
- Professional video production (yet)
- Consistent characters
- Long-form content
- Photorealistic needs

## Our Verdict

Runway Gen-2 is the best text-to-video tool available right now. That's both exciting and limiting.

Exciting because: AI video generation works.
Limiting because: It's not what sci-fi promised yet.

Worth trying if you create visual content. Not worth over-investing in workflows yet.

**Rating:** 7/10 — Impressive for the tech, limited in practical application
