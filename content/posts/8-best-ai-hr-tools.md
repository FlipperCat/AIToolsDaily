## 1. Workable

**Best For:** Mid-market recruitment and hiring

Workable combines recruiting, applicant tracking, and candidate pipeline management. Its AI screens candidates, ranks them, and identifies top performers automatically.

**Features:**
- AI-powered candidate screening
- Job posting distribution
- Applicant tracking system (ATS)
- Interview scheduling
- Collaborative hiring
- Candidate assessment
- Integration ecosystem
- Analytics and reporting

**Pricing:** $99-400/month based on team size

**Pros:**
- Excellent candidate screening AI
- Easy to use for hiring teams
- Good job posting distribution
- Reasonable pricing
- Strong integrations
- Collaborative features strong
- Regular updates
- Good support

**Cons:**
- Limited HR functions beyond recruiting
- Can have slow interfaces
- Learning curve for advanced features
- Not full HRIS
- Smaller ecosystem than enterprise tools

**Who It's Best For:** Mid-market companies, growing teams, and organizations focused on improving hiring quality.
