## Brainstorming & Ideation

### Claude for Writers

My top pick for serious writing work:

- **Longer context** — Upload your entire manuscript for feedback
- **Nuanced analysis** — Understands character arcs and theme
- **Less formulaic** — Outputs feel more natural
- **Research partner** — Explores ideas thoughtfully

**Great prompts:**
> "I'm writing a mystery set in 1920s Chicago. Help me brainstorm three potential murder motives that would resonate with themes of prohibition and class inequality."

> "Read my first three chapters and identify any plot holes or character inconsistencies. Be specific and direct."

**Cost:** Free tier, $20/mo Pro (long documents)

### ChatGPT for Writers

The versatile assistant:

- Quick brainstorming
- Character development
- Plot problem-solving
- Research queries

**Great prompts:**
> "I'm stuck on Act 2. My protagonist has discovered the betrayal but I don't know how she should react given her backstory. She was raised by a narcissistic parent and has trust issues. Give me 5 possible reactions with emotional logic."

> "Generate 20 surnames for characters in a Victorian-era gothic novel."

**Cost:** Free tier works, $20/mo for GPT-4

## Fiction Writing

### Sudowrite

Built specifically for fiction writers:

- **Write** — Continues your prose in your style
- **Describe** — Expands sensory details
- **Brainstorm** — Generates plot ideas
- **Canvas** — Visual story planning

Doesn't write FOR you, writes WITH you. Helps when you're stuck.

**Cost:** $19/month (Hobby), $29/month (Professional)

### NovelAI

Creative writing with fewer guardrails:

- Fiction-focused models
- Custom training on your writing
- Image generation included
- No content restrictions

Popular with genre fiction writers.

**Cost:** $10/month (Tablet), $15/month (Scroll)

### Plottr

Visual story planning:

- Character templates
- Timeline visualization
- Series management
- Export to Scrivener/Word

AI helps generate character backstories and plot connections.

**Cost:** $25/month or $199 lifetime

## Editing & Revision

### ProWritingAid

The deep editor:

- Style suggestions
- Overused words
- Sentence structure
- Pacing analysis
- Fiction-specific checks

More comprehensive than Grammarly for long-form writing. Integrates with Scrivener.

**Cost:** $30/month or $399 lifetime

### Grammarly

The everyday editor:

- Grammar and spelling
- Tone detection
- Clarity suggestions
- Plagiarism check

Best for clean, professional prose. Less nuanced for creative writing.

**Cost:** Free basic, $12/month Premium

### Hemingway Editor

Readability focused:

- Highlights complex sentences
- Identifies passive voice
- Shows reading level
- Suggests simplifications

Forces clarity. Great for non-fiction and journalism.

**Cost:** Free online, $20 desktop app

### Wordtune

Rewriting assistant:

- Rephrase sentences
- Adjust tone
- Shorten or expand
- Multiple alternatives

When you know what you want to say but can't find the words.

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/month Premium

## The Ethics of AI Writing

**Where I draw the line:**

**Good use of AI:**
- Brainstorming and ideation
- Research assistance
- Getting unstuck
- Editing suggestions
- First draft feedback
- Marketing copy

**Questionable use:**
- Generating large portions of final copy
- Submitting AI work as solely yours
- Using AI to mimic specific authors

**The test:** Can you defend every word as your own creative choice?

---

## Why AI Won't Replace Writers

AI generates text. Writers create meaning.

AI can produce a perfectly grammatical paragraph about grief. It cannot know what it feels like to lose someone.

AI can structure a marketing email. It cannot understand why your specific audience needs to hear this specific message right now.

Use AI as a tool, not a crutch. The best writing still comes from lived experience, emotional truth, and human connection.
