## Academic Integrity Considerations

AI is a tool like any other. Guidelines:
- Use AI to **learn concepts**, not replace learning
- Paraphrase and understand, don't copy directly
- Cite AI assistance when required
- Check university AI policies
- Use for brainstorming and rough drafts
- Verify all information before using
- Focus on understanding over answers

## Conclusion

AI tools in 2026 dramatically enhance student learning and productivity. No student should struggle without accessing these tools—many are free or extremely cheap. The most effective students combine tools: ChatGPT/Claude for concept learning, Codeium for programming, Grammarly for writing, Anki for memorization, and specialized tools for their field.

The students winning in 2026 use AI thoughtfully: to explain concepts they don't understand, to improve their writing, to organize their learning, and to solve complex problems. They use these tools throughout their academic careers, building habits of smart learning.
