# AI Voice Cloning: How to Clone Your Voice

Want to create content without recording every word? Clone your voice.

Here's how, done ethically and effectively.

## Why Clone Your Voice?

**Legitimate uses:**
- Narrate content without recording
- Create consistent voiceovers at scale
- Accessibility (help those who've lost their voice)
- Localize content into other languages
- Audiobook production
- Podcast editing (fix mistakes)

**Not legitimate:**
- Impersonating others without consent
- Scams or fraud
- Non-consensual content

Be ethical. Only clone voices you have rights to use.

## Step-by-Step: Creating Your Voice Clone

### Step 1: Record Training Audio

**What you need:**
- Quiet room
- Decent microphone (phone mic works, but better is better)
- 3-5 minutes of varied speech

**Recording tips:**
- Read diverse content (articles, fiction, instructions)
- Use natural pacing
- Include questions, statements, different emotions
- Avoid background noise completely

**Script suggestion:**
Read this article out loud. Then read a Wikipedia article. Then a poem. Variety helps.

### Step 2: Prepare Audio

**Clean up:**
- Remove long silences
- Cut any mistakes
- Export as WAV or high-quality MP3

**Don't:**
- Add music or effects
- Over-process with EQ
- Include multiple speakers

### Step 3: Upload and Train

**ElevenLabs process:**
1. Upload your audio file(s)
2. Add description labels
3. Click "Add Voice"
4. Wait 30-60 seconds

**Test immediately:**
Enter test text and listen. Adjust if needed.

### Step 4: Refine

**If it doesn't sound right:**
- Record more varied content
- Check audio quality
- Try different samples
- Adjust stability/clarity settings

## Settings Explained

### Stability
Higher = more consistent, less emotional
Lower = more expressive, less predictable

**For narration:** Higher stability
**For conversational:** Lower stability

### Clarity + Similarity Enhancement
Higher = closer to original voice
Lower = more AI interpretation

Start high, adjust based on results.

## Ethics and Legal

### Always Get Consent

Only clone voices when you have explicit permission. That includes:
- Your own voice ✓
- Employees with written consent ✓
- Voice actors with contracts ✓
- Random people's voices ✗
- Celebrities ✗
- Deceased persons (complex) ⚠️

### Disclosure

Be transparent when content uses AI voices. Many platforms require disclosure.

### Terms of Service

Check each tool's terms. Most prohibit:
- Deceptive use
- Impersonation
- Adult content
- Political manipulation
