## Clinical Documentation

### Nuance DAX (Dragon Ambient eXperience)

The industry leader in ambient clinical documentation:

- Listens to patient conversations
- Generates clinical notes automatically
- Integrates with major EHRs (Epic, Cerner)
- HIPAA compliant

Physicians report saving 50% of documentation time. The technology behind it is being integrated directly into Microsoft products.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing, typically per-provider

### Abridge

Ambient AI that captures everything:

- Real-time conversation capture
- Structured note generation
- Patient summary creation
- Works with existing workflows

Strong Epic integration. Growing rapidly in hospital systems.

**Cost:** Enterprise (hospitals), practice plans available

### Suki

Voice-enabled EHR assistant:

- Dictate notes naturally
- AI completes documentation
- Learns your preferences
- Works across specialties

"Create a note for a 45-year-old male with type 2 diabetes presenting with fatigue." Suki does the rest.

**Cost:** Custom pricing per provider

### Freed

Affordable ambient scribe for smaller practices:

- Works from phone or computer
- Generates SOAP notes automatically
- Multiple EHR integrations
- Simple setup

Best value for independent practitioners.

**Cost:** $99/month per clinician

### Heidi Health

Free tier makes it accessible:

- Ambient note-taking
- Templates by specialty
- Patient instructions generation
- Referral letter writing

Australian company gaining US traction.

**Cost:** Free tier available, $99/mo for unlimited

## Diagnostic Support

### Glass AI

Clinical reasoning assistant:

- Input symptoms and findings
- Get differential diagnoses
- Evidence-based recommendations
- Treatment planning support

Not a replacement for clinical judgment, but a useful second opinion generator.

**Cost:** $40/month

### Regard

Automated diagnosis capture:

- Reviews patient data continuously
- Suggests missed diagnoses
- Improves CDI (clinical documentation improvement)
- Integrates with EHR workflows

Helps ensure nothing falls through the cracks in complex cases.

**Cost:** Enterprise

### Viz.ai

Radiology and imaging AI:

- Stroke detection
- Pulmonary embolism identification
- Aortic disease detection
- Automated specialist alerts

Speeds time-to-treatment for critical findings.

**Cost:** Enterprise (per-bed or per-scan pricing)

## Patient Communication

### Claude for Healthcare Communication

For patient education and communication (non-clinical use):

- Explain diagnoses in plain language
- Create after-visit summaries
- Draft patient education materials
- Answer common questions

**Example prompt:**
> "Explain type 2 diabetes management to a newly diagnosed patient at a 6th-grade reading level. Include diet, exercise, and medication basics."

Always review AI output before sharing with patients.

**Cost:** Free tier, $20/mo for Pro

### Luma Health

Patient engagement platform:

- Automated appointment reminders
- Waitlist management
- Patient intake forms
- Follow-up sequences

Reduces no-shows and improves patient experience.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

### Klara

Patient communication hub:

- Two-way messaging
- Automated responses to common questions
- Appointment scheduling
- Insurance verification

HIPAA-compliant patient texting.

**Cost:** Custom pricing per provider

## Implementation Considerations

### Regulatory Compliance
- Ensure HIPAA compliance for any tool touching PHI
- Verify BAA (Business Associate Agreement) availability
- Check with your compliance officer before deploying

### Clinical Validation
- FDA clearance status varies by tool
- Understand what's "approved" vs. "support tool"
- Never rely solely on AI for clinical decisions

### EHR Integration
- Check compatibility with your system
- Understand data flow and storage
- Consider implementation timeline

### Staff Training
- Budget time for learning curve
- Identify champions in your practice
- Plan for workflow changes
