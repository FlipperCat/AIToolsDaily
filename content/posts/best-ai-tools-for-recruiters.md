## Sourcing & Search

### LinkedIn Recruiter

Still the starting point:

- Boolean search
- InMail credits
- Pipeline management
- Talent insights

Every recruiter uses this. The question is what you layer on top.

**Cost:** ~$170/month (Recruiter Lite)

### hireEZ (formerly Hiretual)

AI-powered sourcing:

- Searches across 45+ platforms
- AI candidate matching
- Diversity filters
- Contact info enrichment

Finds candidates LinkedIn can't.

**Cost:** Custom (mid-market pricing)

### SeekOut

Diversity-focused sourcing:

- Diversity analytics
- Technical talent search
- Skills inference
- Talent pool management

When D&I isn't just a checkbox.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

### Fetcher

Automated outreach:

- AI finds candidates matching criteria
- Auto-sends personalized outreach
- Tracks responses
- Integrates with ATS

Set your criteria, Fetcher does the searching.

**Cost:** $149/mo (Starter)

## Candidate Engagement

### Paradox (Olivia)

Conversational AI:

- 24/7 candidate chat
- Interview scheduling
- Pre-screening questions
- Multi-language support

Candidates get instant responses. You handle only qualified ones.

**Cost:** Enterprise pricing

### Gem

Recruiting CRM:

- Talent pipeline management
- Sequenced outreach
- Analytics
- ATS integration

Treat candidates like customers with CRM functionality.

**Cost:** Custom (varies by team size)

### ChatGPT for Outreach

Write better messages:

**Cold outreach:**
> "Write a LinkedIn message to a Senior Data Engineer at Google. We're a Series B startup with interesting ML problems. Tone: casual, not desperate. Mention our recent funding and technical challenges without overselling."

**Follow-up:**
> "Write a follow-up to someone who opened my message but didn't respond. Keep it short, add value, don't be pushy."

**Rejection response:**
> "Write a gracious response to a candidate who declined our offer. Keep the door open for future opportunities."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Interview Intelligence

### Metaview

AI interview notes:

- Auto-joins interviews
- Transcribes conversations
- Generates structured notes
- Syncs to ATS

Never write interview notes again.

**Cost:** $30/user/month

### BrightHire

Interview recording + insights:

- Records interviews
- Highlights key moments
- Reduces bias
- Enables team review

Make better hiring decisions with better data.

**Cost:** Custom

## Job Descriptions

### Textio

AI-optimized job posts:

- Real-time guidance
- Bias detection
- Performance predictions
- Competitive insights

Know which phrases attract diverse candidates before you post.

**Cost:** ~$12,000/year

### ChatGPT Alternative

Budget-friendly option:

> "Write a job description for a Senior Product Manager at a fintech startup. Requirements: 5+ years PM experience, B2B SaaS background, SQL skills. Avoid gendered language. Include growth opportunities and our mission: democratizing investing."

Review and customize, but it gets you 80% there.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## The Human Element

AI won't:
- Build relationships with passive candidates
- Sell your company's vision
- Navigate counter-offers
- Make final hiring decisions
- Negotiate compensation

AI handles the volume. You handle the humans who matter.
