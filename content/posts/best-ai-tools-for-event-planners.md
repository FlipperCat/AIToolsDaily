## Planning and Organization

### ChatGPT for Event Communication

Handle the constant communication:

**Vendor outreach:**
> "Write a professional inquiry email to a catering company for a 200-person corporate gala on [date]. Include questions about menu options, dietary accommodations, and pricing structure."

**Guest communication:**
> "Draft a save-the-date email for a company holiday party. Tone should be festive but professional. Include date, location, and dress code."

**Timeline creation:**
> "Create a detailed timeline for a 4-hour corporate awards ceremony. Include setup, guest arrival, program segments, dinner service, and teardown."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Documents

Run-of-show documents:

> "Create a comprehensive run-of-show document for a conference with 3 breakout tracks. Include room assignments, AV needs, speaker transitions, and staff responsibilities."

Claude excels at long, detailed documents.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Airtable

Planning database:

- Guest list management
- Vendor tracking
- Budget monitoring
- Task assignments
- Timeline views

The backbone of organized planning.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Plus

## The Event Planning Truth

Events are chaos management. AI helps with:

- **Routine communication** — Drafts save hours
- **Documentation** — Consistent, detailed docs
- **Organization** — Better systems
- **Design** — Professional materials fast

What AI can't do:
- Handle on-site emergencies
- Build vendor relationships
- Read a room
- Make judgment calls

Use AI for the prep work. Save your energy for game day.
