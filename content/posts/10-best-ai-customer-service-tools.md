## 2026 Best Practices

1. **AI Deflection First:** Use AI to resolve 40-50% of tickets automatically
2. **Seamless Handoff:** Ensure smooth escalation to human agents
3. **Context is Key:** Provide agents full customer context for every ticket
4. **Measure Deflection:** Track CSAT and resolution rates
5. **Continuous Training:** Feed successful resolutions back to AI
6. **Omnichannel:** Support customers on their preferred channels
7. **Proactive Support:** Reach out before customers need help
