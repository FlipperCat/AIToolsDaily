## Limitations of Free Tools

All free tiers have tradeoffs:

- **Daily limits** — Credits reset daily but cap daily output
- **Queue times** — Free users wait longer during peak
- **Resolution limits** — Highest res often paid only
- **Features** — Advanced features may require payment
- **Support** — Limited support for free users

## When to Pay

Consider paid tools when:
- Free limits are consistently inadequate
- You need higher resolution
- Commercial use requires license
- Time is more valuable than money
- Specific features only available paid

**Good value paid upgrades:**
- Leonardo.ai subscription ($10/mo)
- Midjourney Basic ($10/mo)
- Ideogram Plus ($8/mo)
