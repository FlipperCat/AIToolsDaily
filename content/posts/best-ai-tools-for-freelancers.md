## Freelancer AI Workflows

### Workflow 1: Client Onboarding (5 mins vs 30 mins)

**Before:** Manual intake form, copy to project management, send welcome email, schedule kickoff.

**After with AI:**
1. Typeform collects client info
2. Zapier adds to Notion project database
3. Zapier sends personalized welcome email (template via ChatGPT)
4. Calendly link auto-included for scheduling

### Workflow 2: Weekly Client Updates

**Before:** Write individual updates for each client (1 hour)

**After with AI:**
```
Prompt: "Write a brief client update email. Project: [X].
Completed this week: [Y]. Next week: [Z].
Professional but warm tone."
```
Time per update: 2 minutes. Time for 5 clients: 10 minutes.

### Workflow 3: Portfolio Case Studies

**Before:** Spend hours writing case studies (or never do them)

**After with AI:**
1. Export project data/results
2. ChatGPT generates first draft
3. Edit for 15 minutes
4. One case study per week instead of per quarter

## Common Freelancer Mistakes with AI

❌ **Over-relying on AI for client communication**
Personal touch still matters. Use AI for drafts, but add personal details.

❌ **Not editing AI outputs**
Clients can tell. Always review and refine.

❌ **Telling clients you use AI**
They're paying for results, not process. Don't volunteer this information.

❌ **Using AI for things that need your expertise**
Strategy, creativity, and judgment are your value-add. AI handles execution.
