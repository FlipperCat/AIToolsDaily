# Claude 3 Launch: Everything Anthropic Announced

Anthropic just launched Claude 3. Three models. Major improvements. GPT-4 competition intensifies.

Here's everything that was announced.

## The Three Models

### Claude 3 Haiku — Fast

**Position:** Quick, cheap, efficient
**Best for:** Simple tasks, high-volume applications
**Speed:** Near-instant responses
**Price:** Cheapest of the three

### Claude 3 Sonnet — Balanced

**Position:** The everyday workhorse
**Best for:** Most tasks, general use
**Balance:** Good speed and capability
**Price:** Middle tier

### Claude 3 Opus — Powerful

**Position:** Maximum capability
**Best for:** Complex reasoning, professional use
**Claims:** Matches or beats GPT-4 on benchmarks
**Price:** Premium

## Key Improvements

### 1. Vision (Finally)

Claude can now see images. Analyze photos, read documents, understand charts.

This was missing before. Major addition.

### 2. 200K Context

Process ~150,000 words in a single conversation. Analyze entire books, large codebases, extensive documents.

Claude's context window is now larger than GPT-4's.

### 3. Better Reasoning

Benchmark scores suggest improved reasoning across math, logic, and analysis tasks.

### 4. More Honest

Anthropic claims Claude 3 is better at admitting uncertainty and avoiding hallucinations.

### 5. Faster

Significant speed improvements, especially with Haiku.

## Benchmark Claims

| Benchmark | Claude 3 Opus | GPT-4 |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| MMLU | 86.8% | 86.4% |
| HumanEval | 84.9% | 67.0% |
| GSM8K | 95.0% | 92.0% |

Benchmarks aren't everything, but these are competitive numbers.
