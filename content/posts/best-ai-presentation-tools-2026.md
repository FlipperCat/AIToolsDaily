## Tips for Better AI Presentations

### 1. Start with an Outline
Don't just say "create a presentation about X." Provide:
- Key points to cover
- Desired structure
- Target audience

### 2. Edit After Generation
AI gets you 80% there. Spend time on:
- Refining key messages
- Adding specific data
- Adjusting tone

### 3. Add Your Visuals
AI-generated images are good but generic. Add:
- Your own photos
- Screenshots
- Custom graphics

### 4. Practice the Flow
AI creates content, not delivery. Review:
- Slide transitions
- Story flow
- Time per slide
