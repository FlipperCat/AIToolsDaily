## Communication

### ChatGPT for Drafting

Handle the email volume:

**Routine responses:**
> "Draft a polite decline for this meeting request. Keep the door open for future conversations."

**Executive communications:**
> "Write an email from [Executive] thanking the team for Q4 performance. Tone: appreciative but not over-the-top. Mention specific achievements: [list]."

**Difficult messages:**
> "Draft a response to this vendor complaint. Be firm about our position but maintain the relationship."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Communication

When nuance matters:

- Board communications
- Sensitive HR matters
- Multi-stakeholder updates
- Executive correspondence

Claude handles complex tone better.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Grammarly

Final polish on everything:

- Grammar and style
- Tone adjustment
- Consistency
- Quick review

**Cost:** Free / $12/mo

## Calendar Management

### Calendly

Eliminate scheduling email:

- Share booking links
- Set availability rules
- Buffer time between meetings
- Team scheduling pages

"Book time with [Executive] here: [link]"

**Cost:** Free tier, $12/mo Professional

### Reclaim.ai

Protect executive time:

- Auto-blocks focus time
- Reschedules tasks around meetings
- Defends priorities
- Syncs across calendars

AI manages the calendar automatically.

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/mo Starter

### Clara

AI scheduling assistant:

- Natural language scheduling
- CC Clara on emails
- She handles the back-and-forth
- Works like a human assistant

For executives who get too many scheduling requests.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

### Motion

Task + calendar integration:

- Schedules tasks automatically
- Respects priorities
- Reschedules when things slip
- Protects deadlines

When to-dos need calendar time.

**Cost:** $34/mo

## Meeting Management

### Otter.ai

Never miss action items:

- Auto-joins meetings
- Real-time transcription
- Action item extraction
- Searchable archive

Review meetings for executive without attending.

**Cost:** Free 300 min/mo, $17/mo Pro

### Loom

Async video updates:

- Quick video messages
- Track who watched
- Replace some meetings
- Brief executives efficiently

"Here's your 3-minute briefing before the board meeting."

**Cost:** Free tier, $15/mo Business
