# Notion AI: Is It Worth $10/Month?

I've been paying for Notion AI for three months. That's $30 spent. Time to decide if I'm keeping it or if it's another subscription that sounds cool but doesn't deliver.

## What Notion AI Actually Is

It's ChatGPT-level AI built directly into Notion. You can:
- Ask it to write something on a page
- Highlight text and ask it to improve/summarize/translate
- Ask questions about your existing Notion content
- Generate content that fits your workspace context

The key difference from ChatGPT: it lives inside Notion. No copying and pasting between tools.

## What I Actually Use It For

### Meeting Notes → Action Items (Weekly)

I dump rough meeting notes into Notion. Ask AI: "Extract action items from this."

It pulls out tasks, often catches things I missed.

**Time saved:** 5-10 minutes per meeting. Maybe 30 minutes/week.

### Quick First Drafts (A Few Times Weekly)

I need a doc outline or first draft. Instead of context-switching to ChatGPT, I generate it right in Notion where I'll be writing anyway.

**Time saved:** Small per instance. Adds up.

### Summarizing Long Pages (Occasionally)

Some pages in my workspace are walls of text. AI summarizes them into key points.

**Time saved:** Maybe 10 minutes/week.

### Answering Questions About My Workspace (Rarely)

"When did we last discuss X project?" - It can search and synthesize.

I use this less than I expected to.

## What I Don't Use It For

### Heavy Writing Tasks

For serious writing, I still use Claude. The quality is noticeably better.

Notion AI is fine for quick drafts and routine content. For anything important, I go to Claude.

### Research

Notion AI doesn't search the internet. It only knows what's in my Notion workspace.

For research, Perplexity or ChatGPT is necessary.

### Complex Analysis

Anything requiring deep thinking, I use Claude. Notion AI handles simple tasks but isn't great at nuanced analysis.

## The Real Question: Is It Better Than Just Using ChatGPT?

**The integration argument:**

Notion AI is built in. No copying text to ChatGPT, waiting, copying back. For quick tasks, this saves a minute each time.

If I do 10 quick AI tasks per day in Notion, that's 10 minutes saved daily. Maybe 50 minutes/week. For $10/month, that's reasonable value.

**The counter-argument:**

The AI quality is roughly equal to ChatGPT, which I'm already paying for. Am I paying twice for the same thing?

Kind of. I'm paying for convenience, not capability.

## The Honest Assessment

**Worth it if:**
- You already use Notion as your primary workspace
- You do lots of quick AI tasks while writing/organizing
- The context-switching to ChatGPT genuinely bothers you
- $10/month is trivial for your budget

**Not worth it if:**
- You don't use Notion much
- You already have a ChatGPT Plus workflow that works
- You need higher quality AI output (use Claude)
- You're budget-conscious

## My Decision

I'm keeping it. But barely.

The time savings are real but small. Maybe 30-60 minutes per week. For $10/month, that's about $2-4/hour of time saved. Worth it for me, but not a slam dunk.

If I had to cut AI subscriptions, Notion AI would be among the first to go. It's convenient, not essential.

## The Alternatives

**If you use Notion but don't want to pay extra:**
- Keep ChatGPT open in another tab
- Copy/paste when needed
- It's annoying but works

**If you don't use Notion:**
- Don't buy Notion AI just for the AI
- Use ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro instead
- They're better AI for the same or lower price

## Quick Verdict

Notion AI is a nice-to-have, not a must-have.

It makes Notion better if you're already a Notion user. It's not worth switching to Notion for.

**Rating: 6.5/10** - Genuinely useful integration, but the value depends heavily on how much you use Notion. Not transformative.
