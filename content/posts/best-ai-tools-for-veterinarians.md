## Client Communication

### ChatGPT for Pet Owner Education

Draft educational content:

**Post-visit instructions:**
> "Write discharge instructions for a dog who just had a spay surgery. Include activity restrictions, medication instructions, and warning signs to watch for. Pet owner has no medical background."

**Condition explanations:**
> "Explain diabetes in cats to a concerned owner. Cover what it means, how it's managed, and quality of life expectations. Reassuring but honest tone."

**Preventive care:**
> "Create a client handout about flea and tick prevention. Include why it matters, prevention options, and what to look for. Keep it under 200 words."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Cases

Difficult conversations:

> "Help me draft an email to a client explaining that their pet's quality of life assessment indicates it may be time to discuss end-of-life options. Be compassionate but clear."

> "A client is questioning the cost of a recommended treatment plan. Help me explain the value and what goes into veterinary pricing without being defensive."

Claude handles nuanced, emotional communication well.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### PetDesk

Client engagement platform:

- Two-way texting
- Appointment reminders
- Vaccination due alerts
- Online booking
- Loyalty programs

Modern client communication.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

### Weave

All-in-one phone system:

- Caller ID with pet info
- Automated texts
- Review requests
- Payment collection
- Call recording

Know who's calling before you answer.

**Cost:** Starting ~$400/mo

## AI Diagnosis Tools (Use Carefully)

Some AI tools claim diagnostic assistance. Important notes:

- AI can suggest differentials
- AI cannot replace clinical judgment
- Always verify AI suggestions
- Document your reasoning
- Liability remains with the veterinarian

AI as a reference tool, not a decision-maker.

## The Veterinary Reality

You became a vet to help animals, not to write discharge notes.

AI helps with:
- Client communication that you'd otherwise rush
- Educational materials you don't have time to create
- Administrative tasks that eat into patient time

Every minute saved on paperwork is a minute available for patient care.
