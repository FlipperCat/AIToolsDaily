## Photo Culling

### Aftershoot

The culling problem solver:

- Analyzes thousands of images in minutes
- Groups similar shots
- Identifies best technically (sharpness, exposure, eyes open)
- Learns your selection preferences over time

Import, let it run, review its picks. What took 4 hours now takes 20 minutes.

**Cost:** $15/month (Culling), $30/month (Culling + Editing)

### Narrative Select

Speed-focused culling:

- Keyboard-driven workflow
- AI-suggested selects
- Color coding system
- Quick exports to Lightroom

Designed for high-volume shooters who need to move fast.

**Cost:** $9/month

### FilterPixel

Smart culling for events:

- Duplicate detection
- Face recognition
- Technical quality scoring
- Batch organization

Good budget option for culling large shoots.

**Cost:** Free tier, $8/month premium

## Editing Automation

### Imagen AI

Learns YOUR editing style:

- Analyzes your previous edits
- Creates custom AI profile
- Batch-applies your style to new images
- Works as Lightroom plugin

Send 3,000 photos to Imagen, get them back edited in your style. Then fine-tune.

**Cost:** $7/month subscription + $0.05/image (or bulk packs)

### Aftershoot Edits

Combined culling and editing:

- AI profiles based on your presets
- Detects image type (portrait, landscape, indoor)
- Applies appropriate adjustments
- Continuous learning

One tool for both culling and editing.

**Cost:** $30/month for both features

### Lightroom AI Features

Built into your existing workflow:

- **Adaptive Presets** — Adjust to each image
- **AI Masking** — Select subjects, sky, background
- **Denoise** — Impressive noise reduction
- **Content-Aware Remove** — Clean up distractions
- **AI Recommendations** — Suggested adjustments

If you're already in Lightroom, the AI features keep improving.

**Cost:** $10/month (Photography plan)

## Advanced Editing

### Photoshop Generative AI

Mind-blowing capabilities:

- **Generative Fill** — Extend images, add/remove objects
- **Generative Expand** — Extend backgrounds naturally
- **Neural Filters** — Portrait adjustments, style transfer
- **AI-Powered Selection** — One-click subject selection

Extend a crop, remove someone from background, change expressions — all AI-powered now.

**Cost:** $21/month (or $10/mo with Photography plan)

### Luminar Neo

One-click AI magic:

- Sky replacement (realistic)
- Portrait enhancement (eyes, skin, face)
- Atmosphere effects
- Structure and detail enhancement

Non-destructive, works as plugin or standalone.

**Cost:** $15/month or $149/year

### Topaz Photo AI

The technical fix specialist:

- AI upscaling (stunning results)
- Noise reduction (best in class)
- Sharpening
- Face recovery

Turn a cropped, high-ISO image into something printable. The upscaling is genuinely impressive.

**Cost:** $199 one-time purchase

## Quality Concerns

**Will AI make my work look generic?**

Not if you:
- Use AI that learns YOUR style (Imagen, Aftershoot)
- Treat AI as a starting point, not final output
- Fine-tune AI edits to maintain your vision
- Use AI for technical fixes, keep creative control

The best photographers use AI for the tedious work, then add their personal touch.
