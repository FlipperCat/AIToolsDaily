## Workout Programming

### ChatGPT for Programs

Generate workout templates:

**Custom programming:**
> "Create a 4-week hypertrophy program for an intermediate lifter. 4 days per week, upper/lower split. Focus on progressive overload with compound movements. Include sets, reps, and rest periods."

**Program modifications:**
> "My client has a shoulder impingement and can't do overhead pressing. Modify this upper body workout to work around that limitation while still targeting deltoids."

**Exercise alternatives:**
> "Give me 5 alternatives to barbell back squats for a client training at home with only dumbbells and resistance bands."

**Important:** Always review AI-generated programs. You're the expert on your client's specific needs.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Education

Client education materials:

> "Explain the concept of progressive overload to a complete beginner. Use simple analogies. Keep it under 200 words."

> "Create a one-page guide on protein intake for muscle building. Include how much, when, and good sources. For someone who knows nothing about nutrition."

Claude writes clear, educational content.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Client Management Platforms

### Trainerize

All-in-one platform:

- Workout delivery
- Nutrition tracking
- Messaging
- Progress photos
- Habit tracking

The most popular training platform.

**Cost:** $5/active client/month

### TrueCoach

Program delivery focus:

- Clean workout interface
- Video exercise library
- Client communication
- Progress tracking

Simpler, focused on programming.

**Cost:** $19-99/mo based on client count

### Caliber

Modern coaching platform:

- AI-assisted programming
- Form video review
- Integrated nutrition
- Clean interface

Growing alternative.

**Cost:** ~$10/active client/month

## Nutrition

### Cronometer

Detailed tracking:

- Micronutrient tracking
- Database accuracy
- Pro features for coaches
- Client sharing

More accurate than MyFitnessPal.

**Cost:** Free / $40/yr Pro

### MacroFactor

Smart nutrition coaching:

- Adaptive algorithms
- Adjusts based on progress
- No restrictive food lists
- Client-facing app

AI-powered nutrition adjustments.

**Cost:** $72/yr

### ChatGPT for Meal Ideas

> "Create a 2000 calorie meal plan with 150g protein for someone who hates cooking. Maximum 15 minutes per meal. No seafood."

Quick meal idea generation (not medical nutrition advice).

## Important Boundaries

### What AI Can Help With
- Workout template generation
- Exercise alternatives
- General nutrition information
- Content creation
- Client communication drafts

### What Requires Your Expertise
- Individual client assessment
- Form correction
- Injury modifications
- Nutrition protocols for medical conditions
- Motivation and accountability

AI generates. You customize, verify, and deliver.

## The Fitness Business Reality

Your value isn't programming (AI can generate that). Your value is:

- Assessment and individualization
- Accountability and motivation
- Form correction and safety
- Behavior change support
- Human connection

AI handles the template work. You handle the transformation.
