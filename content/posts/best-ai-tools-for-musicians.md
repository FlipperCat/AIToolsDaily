## AI Music Generation

### Suno

The viral music generator:

- Text prompts to full songs
- Multiple genres
- Vocals included
- Surprisingly good quality

"Upbeat indie rock song about Monday mornings" → Complete track.

**Use for:** Demos, inspiration, content creation
**Not for:** Replacing your own music

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/mo Pro

### Udio

Suno competitor:

- High-quality generation
- More control over style
- Longer outputs
- Growing fast

Similar capabilities, slightly different strengths.

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/mo Standard

### AIVA

Composition assistant:

- Classical and cinematic focus
- MIDI output
- Customizable styles
- Copyright ownership

Better for composers wanting MIDI to work with.

**Cost:** Free tier, $15/mo Standard

## Songwriting Assistance

### ChatGPT for Lyrics

Brainstorming partner:

> "Help me write lyrics for a verse about leaving a hometown. Tone: bittersweet, not sad. Style: indie folk."

> "I have this chorus: [lyrics]. Write a verse that builds to it."

> "Give me rhyming options for 'time' that aren't cliché."

Starting points, not finished lyrics.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Song Structure

Deeper analysis:

> "Analyze the structure of [song]. How does the tension build? What makes the chorus hit?"

> "I'm stuck on the bridge. The song is about [theme]. The verse is [description], chorus is [description]. What could the bridge explore?"

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## The Ethical Question

### AI-Generated Music

**For:**
- Background music
- Demos and sketches
- Learning and inspiration
- Content that needs music

**Against:**
- Passing off as your own
- Competing with human musicians
- Replacing session musicians

### My Take

AI tools that enhance human creativity: Great.
AI tools that replace human creativity: Problematic.

Use AI to get ideas flowing, but make the music yours.

## The Future

AI won't write the next "Bohemian Rhapsody." But it will:
- Handle tedious production tasks
- Provide infinite inspiration
- Make music creation more accessible
- Change how we think about originality

The musicians who thrive will use AI as a tool, not a replacement.
