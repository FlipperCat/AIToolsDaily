## Writing & Communication

### 1. Claude — Best for Client Work

**Cost:** Free / $20 month Pro

**Why freelancers love it:**
- Drafts proposals and emails quickly
- Writes content that sounds natural
- Handles long documents well
- Brainstorms ideas and solutions

**Use it for:**
- Client proposals
- Project updates
- Content deliverables
- Research and planning

### 2. ChatGPT — Most Versatile

**Cost:** Free / $20/month Plus

**Why freelancers love it:**
- Handles any task type
- Image generation included (Plus)
- Code assistance built-in
- Huge ecosystem of GPTs

**Use it for:**
- Varied task types
- Quick questions
- When you need images

### 3. Grammarly — Polish Everything

**Cost:** Free / $12/month Premium

**Why freelancers love it:**
- Catches errors you miss
- Works everywhere you type
- Makes all communication professional
- Tone suggestions

**Use it for:**
- Client emails
- Deliverables
- Proposals
- Everything written

## Productivity & Automation

### 4. Zapier — Connect Everything

**Cost:** Free / $20/month Starter

**Freelancer automations:**
- New client email → Add to CRM → Create project folder
- Project complete → Generate invoice → Send to client
- Calendar event → Create prep document
- Form submission → Add to pipeline → Send welcome email

### 5. Make.com — More Power, Less Cost

**Cost:** Free / $9/month Core

**Better than Zapier for:**
- Complex workflows
- Budget-conscious freelancers
- 10x more free operations

### 6. Motion — AI Scheduling

**Cost:** $19/month

**Why freelancers love it:**
- AI schedules your entire day
- Automatically reschedules when things change
- Balances multiple client projects
- Protects deep work time
