# AI Weekly: What Happened This Week (August 2024)

Another busy week in AI. Here's what you need to know.

## Big News

### Stability AI Releases Stable Diffusion 3
The new version brings major improvements to image quality, especially for hands and text in images. Available through their API, with local versions coming soon.

**Why it matters:** Free and open-source image generation gets another leap forward.

### Meta Llama 3.1 Now Available
Meta's latest open-source model matches GPT-4 on several benchmarks. Available for local use and commercial deployment.

**Why it matters:** Open-source AI continues closing the gap with proprietary models.

### Claude Gets Artifacts
Anthropic added a new "Artifacts" feature to Claude, letting it create and preview code, documents, and designs in a side panel.

**Why it matters:** Makes Claude more practical for development and content creation tasks.
