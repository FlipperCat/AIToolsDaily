## Session Support

### Claude for Session Prep

Prepare for client sessions:

**Session preparation:**
> "My client is a startup founder struggling with work-life balance and delegation. They mentioned feeling guilty when not working. Help me prepare 5 powerful questions for our session that address both the practical and emotional aspects."

**Framework application:**
> "Create a worksheet applying the GROW model to help a client work through a career transition decision. Include prompting questions for each stage."

**Resource creation:**
> "Develop a 'weekly reflection' template for a client working on time management. Include prompts about wins, challenges, and intentions for next week."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Otter.ai

Session notes:

- Record coaching sessions (with consent)
- Automatic transcription
- Searchable across all sessions
- Track progress over time

Review what clients said, not just what you remember.

**Cost:** Free tier / $17/mo Pro

## Content Creation

### ChatGPT for Content

Build your coaching brand:

**Social media:**
> "Write 10 LinkedIn posts about common challenges business owners face with delegation. Mix frameworks, personal insights, and questions. My voice is direct but warm."

**Lead magnets:**
> "Create an outline for a free PDF guide: '5 Questions to Ask Yourself Before Making Any Major Decision.' Include the questions and space for reflection."

**Email sequences:**
> "Write a 5-email welcome sequence for people who download my free guide on [topic]. Nurture them toward booking a discovery call."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## AI Coaching Assistants (Use Carefully)

Some tools offer "AI coaching" features. Be thoughtful:

**Appropriate uses:**
- Prompting questions between sessions
- Reflection exercises
- Accountability check-ins
- Resource delivery

**Inappropriate uses:**
- Replacing human coaching
- Giving advice AI shouldn't give
- Therapy-adjacent situations

AI can support your coaching. It shouldn't replace it.

## The Coaching Balance

Good coaching is presence and connection. AI can't do that.

But AI can:
- Handle scheduling friction
- Keep better notes
- Create resources faster
- Scale your content

More admin efficiency = more energy for actual coaching.

Use AI to do more of what matters.
