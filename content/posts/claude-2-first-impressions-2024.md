# Claude 2 First Impressions: Better Than ChatGPT?

Anthropic's Claude 2 has been getting serious attention. After two weeks of daily use, here's our honest assessment.

## What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant from Anthropic, a company founded by former OpenAI researchers. It's designed to be helpful, harmless, and honest - with a focus on safety.

## First Impressions

Opening claude.ai, the interface is clean and simple. No bells and whistles - just a chat interface. That's refreshing.

The first thing you notice: Claude can handle longer conversations. Where ChatGPT sometimes forgets earlier context, Claude maintains it better.

## What Claude Does Better

### 1. Long Document Analysis
Upload a 50-page PDF and ask questions about it. Claude actually reads and understands the whole thing. This is genuinely impressive.

### 2. Nuanced Writing
Claude's writing feels more thoughtful. It doesn't over-explain, doesn't pad content with fluff, and captures tone better than ChatGPT in our tests.

### 3. Following Complex Instructions
Give Claude a detailed prompt with multiple requirements, and it tends to hit all of them. ChatGPT sometimes ignores parts of complex prompts.

### 4. Admitting Uncertainty
Claude is better at saying "I'm not sure" rather than confidently making things up. This matters for accuracy.

## Where ChatGPT Still Wins

### 1. Speed
ChatGPT responds faster, especially with GPT-3.5.

### 2. Integrations
Plugins, browsing, code interpreter - ChatGPT has a richer ecosystem.

### 3. Code
For programming tasks, ChatGPT (especially with GPT-4) still edges ahead.

### 4. Familiarity
More people know ChatGPT, more tutorials exist, more prompts are shared.

## Our Testing

We used both for two weeks on real work:
- Writing blog posts
- Summarizing research
- Editing documents
- Brainstorming ideas
- Answering questions

**Verdict:** We reached for Claude more often for writing tasks. ChatGPT for quick coding help.

## Pricing Comparison

| Feature | Claude | ChatGPT |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Free tier | Yes, generous | Yes, GPT-3.5 only |
| Pro price | $20/month | $20/month |
| Best model access | Claude 2 (Pro) | GPT-4 (Plus) |

## Should You Switch?

**Use Claude if:**
- You work with long documents
- Writing quality matters most
- You value nuanced responses
- You're tired of ChatGPT's quirks

**Stick with ChatGPT if:**
- You need plugins and integrations
- Coding is your main use
- Speed matters more than nuance
- You've built prompts and workflows around it

## The Honest Truth

Both are excellent. The gap between them is smaller than the gap between either and not using AI at all. Try both, use what works for your tasks.

Competition is good. It makes both tools better.
