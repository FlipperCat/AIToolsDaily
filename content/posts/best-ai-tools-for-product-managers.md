## Writing and Documentation

### Claude for PRDs

Product requirements made easier:

**PRD drafting:**
> "Write a PRD for [feature]. Include: problem statement, user stories, acceptance criteria, success metrics, and dependencies. Target audience is [describe]. Technical constraints include [list]."

**User story generation:**
> "Generate user stories for [feature]. Include edge cases. Format as: As a [user], I want [goal], so that [benefit]."

**Spec review:**
> "Review this PRD and identify: missing requirements, ambiguous sections, potential edge cases, and questions engineering will ask."

Claude handles long, detailed documents well.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Notion

PM's home base:

- PRD templates
- Meeting notes
- Roadmap views
- Feature specs
- AI summarization

Everything in one searchable place.

**Cost:** Free / $10/mo Plus

## Research Synthesis

### Dovetail

User research at scale:

- Interview transcription
- Tagging and themes
- Insight extraction
- Collaboration

Turn research chaos into patterns.

**Cost:** Starting $29/mo

### Otter.ai for Interviews

Capture every user interview:

- Record calls
- Automatic transcription
- Searchable archive
- Share with team

Never miss what users said.

**Cost:** Free / $17/mo Pro

### ChatGPT for Synthesis

Analyze research quickly:

> "Here are notes from 10 user interviews about [feature] [paste]. What are the common themes? What patterns emerge? What surprised me?"

> "Categorize this feedback into: must-have, nice-to-have, and edge cases. [paste feedback]"

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Stakeholder Communication

### Claude for Status Updates

Draft communications:

**Executive updates:**
> "Write a monthly product update for executives. Key shipping: [list]. Key metrics: [data]. Upcoming: [roadmap]. Challenges: [issues]. Keep it to one page."

**Stakeholder emails:**
> "A stakeholder is asking why feature X isn't on the roadmap. Draft a diplomatic response explaining our prioritization logic."

**Launch announcements:**
> "Write internal launch communications for [feature]. Explain what shipped, why it matters, and how it impacts different teams."

### Loom

Show, don't tell:

- Feature demos
- Research findings
- Roadmap walkthroughs
- Decision explanations

Async video for stakeholder alignment.

**Cost:** Free / $15/mo

## PM Workflows with AI

### User Interview to Insight

1. **Record** with Otter.ai
2. **Transcribe** automatically
3. **Synthesize** with ChatGPT
4. **Document** in Notion
5. **Share** via Loom

### PRD Creation

1. **Research** synthesis
2. **Draft** with Claude
3. **Review** with engineering
4. **Iterate** based on feedback
5. **Finalize** in Notion

### Stakeholder Updates

1. **Gather** metrics and progress
2. **Draft** with Claude
3. **Visualize** key points
4. **Send** or present with Loom
