## Client Communication

### ChatGPT for Client Emails

Draft personalized client communications:

**Market update email:**
> "Write a client email explaining recent market volatility. Tone: calm and reassuring. Mention that their diversified portfolio is designed for long-term growth. Offer to schedule a call if they have concerns. Keep it under 200 words."

**Quarterly review prep:**
> "Create talking points for a quarterly review with a client approaching retirement in 5 years. They're concerned about sequence of returns risk. Include questions to gauge their comfort level."

Always review and personalize — but AI gets you 80% there.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Jump

AI-powered client messaging:

- Automated birthday/anniversary messages
- Market commentary distribution
- Compliant messaging
- CRM integration

Keeps you top-of-mind without manual effort.

**Cost:** $79/month

### Pulse360

Meeting documentation:

- AI meeting summaries
- Action item extraction
- CRM sync
- Compliance-ready notes

Never manually write meeting notes again.

**Cost:** $99/month

## Compliance Considerations

**Critical:** Financial services have strict compliance requirements.

**Before using AI:**
- Check with your compliance department
- Understand your firm's policies
- Never rely solely on AI for advice
- Always review and verify AI output
- Document your process

**Safe AI uses:**
- Draft communications (always review)
- Research and analysis
- Administrative tasks
- Internal notes

**Risky AI uses:**
- Direct client-facing advice
- Compliance documentation without review
- Investment recommendations

## The Human Element

AI makes you more efficient. It doesn't make you a better advisor.

What clients actually want:
- Someone who knows their situation
- Proactive communication
- Calm during volatility
- Honest advice

AI gives you time for those human moments by handling the administrative burden.
