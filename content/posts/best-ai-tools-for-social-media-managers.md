## Content Creation

### ChatGPT for Social Copy

Generate content at scale:

**Post ideas:**
> "Give me 20 post ideas for a [industry] brand on Instagram. Mix educational, entertaining, and promotional content."

**Caption writing:**
> "Write 5 caption options for this image: [describe]. Brand voice is [describe]. Include CTAs and relevant hashtags."

**Content calendars:**
> "Create a week of social media content for [brand]. Include posts for Instagram, Twitter, and LinkedIn. Theme is [topic]."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Longer Content

LinkedIn articles, threads, stories:

> "Write a LinkedIn article about [topic] from the perspective of [role]. Include personal insights and actionable takeaways. 800-1000 words."

> "Turn this blog post into a Twitter thread: [paste]. Make it engaging with hooks and visuals suggestions."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Canva

Visual content:

- Post templates
- Story graphics
- Carousel designs
- Video thumbnails
- Brand kit management

Most social graphics come from here.

**Cost:** Free / $13/mo Pro

## Workflow Tips

### Batch Content Creation

Don't create daily. Batch weekly:

1. **Monday:** Research and ideation (Perplexity, ChatGPT)
2. **Tuesday:** Content creation (ChatGPT, Claude)
3. **Wednesday:** Graphics (Canva)
4. **Thursday:** Schedule (Buffer, Later)
5. **Friday:** Engagement and analysis

### AI Voice Training

> "Here are 10 of our best-performing posts: [paste]. Analyze the voice, tone, and patterns. Then write new posts that match this style."

Teach AI your brand voice.

### Engagement Efficiency

Draft response templates:
- Praise/thanks
- Questions
- Complaints
- Product inquiries

Use AI to personalize templates, not write from scratch each time.

## What AI Can't Do

- Build genuine community
- Handle crisis PR (requires human judgment)
- Create truly original ideas
- Replace authentic connection

AI accelerates. Humans connect.
