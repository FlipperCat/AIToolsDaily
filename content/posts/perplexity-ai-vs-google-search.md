# Perplexity AI vs Google: After One Month

Everyone's talking about AI search replacing Google. I decided to actually test it.

One month using Perplexity as my primary search engine. Here's what I learned.

## The Experiment

**Rules:**
- Start every search in Perplexity
- Only use Google if Perplexity couldn't help
- Track what worked and what didn't

**My search habits:**
- ~40 searches per day
- Mix of work research, quick facts, local searches, shopping

## Where Perplexity Crushed Google

### Research Questions

**Search:** "What's the difference between REST and GraphQL for a mobile app backend?"

**Google:** 10 links. Different opinions. Had to read 4 articles to understand.

**Perplexity:** One comprehensive answer. Pros and cons listed. Sources cited. 5 minutes saved.

**Winner:** Perplexity, decisively.

### "Explain This To Me" Queries

**Search:** "Why is my sourdough bread dense?"

**Google:** Forum posts, recipes, videos. Mixed information.

**Perplexity:** Analyzed common causes, ranked by likelihood, gave actionable fixes.

**Winner:** Perplexity. Felt like asking an expert.

### Comparison Research

**Search:** "Should I use Next.js or Remix for my project?"

**Google:** SEO-optimized articles with affiliate links. Took 20 minutes to get balanced view.

**Perplexity:** Summarized key differences, use cases for each, recent community sentiment. 3 minutes.

**Winner:** Perplexity. No agenda, just information.

### Current Events Context

**Search:** "What's happening with AI regulation in Europe?"

**Google:** News articles, each covering one angle.

**Perplexity:** Synthesized current state, recent developments, what's coming. Complete picture.

**Winner:** Perplexity. Actual context, not just headlines.

## Where Google Still Wins

### Quick Facts

**Search:** "Weather in Chicago"

**Perplexity:** Works but slower.

**Google:** Instant widget. Faster.

**Winner:** Google. Perplexity is overkill for simple lookups.

### Local Searches

**Search:** "Coffee shops near me"

**Perplexity:** Gives general recommendations. Doesn't know my location well.

**Google:** Maps integration. Reviews. Hours. Directions.

**Winner:** Google. Not even close.

### Shopping

**Search:** "Best running shoes under $150"

**Perplexity:** Good recommendations.

**Google:** Price comparisons, shopping results, current deals.

**Winner:** Google for buying, Perplexity for researching.

### Navigation Searches

**Search:** "Gmail login"

**Perplexity:** Tells me about Gmail. I just wanted the link.

**Google:** Direct link at top.

**Winner:** Google. AI search over-thinks simple navigation.

### Image Search

**Search:** "Modern minimalist logo examples"

**Perplexity:** Limited image results.

**Google:** Endless visual inspiration.

**Winner:** Google. Visual search isn't Perplexity's strength.

## The Surprising Insights

### I Use Search Differently Now

With Perplexity, I ask questions I never would have Googled.

**Old way:** "python async tutorial" → read 3 articles

**New way:** "Explain Python async/await like I know JavaScript promises but not Python" → get exactly what I need

AI search handles nuanced questions. Google needs you to know the right keywords.

### Source Quality Matters

Perplexity cites sources. I actually check them now.

This made me realize how rarely I verified Google results. I'd trust the first decent-looking link.

With citations visible, I naturally became a better researcher.

### Follow-Up Questions Change Everything

Perplexity remembers context.

**Initial:** "What's the best database for my project?"

**Follow-up:** "What about if I need real-time sync?"

**Another:** "How does the pricing compare?"

Try that with Google. You're starting fresh each search.

## Cost Analysis

**Perplexity Free:**
- Limited Pro searches/day
- Basic model
- Good enough for most uses

**Perplexity Pro ($20/month):**
- Unlimited Pro searches
- GPT-4, Claude access
- File uploads
- API access

**Google:**
- Free
- Your data is the product

**Is Pro worth it?**

If you research for work daily, yes. The time savings justify $20/month easily.

For casual use, free tier is sufficient.

## My New Search Strategy

After the month, I settled into a pattern:

**Use Perplexity for:**
- Research questions
- Learning new topics
- Comparisons and decisions
- Complex "explain this" queries
- Current events with context

**Use Google for:**
- Quick facts
- Local searches
- Shopping
- Navigation (going to specific sites)
- Image search

**Ratio after one month:** 60% Perplexity, 40% Google

## The Honest Verdict

### Perplexity is better for:

Knowledge work. If your job involves research, learning, and synthesizing information, Perplexity saves real time.

### Google is better for:

Action searches. Buying things, going places, quick lookups.

### The Future

AI search won't replace Google. But it's already replaced Google for certain searches.

The smart approach: use both. Know which tool fits which search.

## Should You Switch?

### Try Perplexity if:

- You do research-heavy work
- You're tired of SEO-optimized content farms
- You want answers, not links
- You ask complex questions

### Stick with Google if:

- Most searches are quick lookups
- You need local/shopping results
- You prefer choosing your own sources
- Free is non-negotiable

### My recommendation:

Add Perplexity to your toolkit. Don't delete Google. Use each for what it does best.

The best search engine is the one that answers your question fastest. Sometimes that's AI, sometimes that's Google.
