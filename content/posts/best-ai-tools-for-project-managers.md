## Project Management Platforms

### Notion AI

The flexible workspace:

- AI-generated project docs
- Database-driven tracking
- Wiki with AI search
- Template generation

Notion's AI writes project briefs, summarizes docs, and answers questions about your workspace.

**Cost:** $10/mo add-on to any Notion plan

### Linear

For engineering teams:

- Clean, fast interface
- AI-generated issue descriptions
- Auto-triage
- Cycles and roadmaps

PMs who work with engineers love Linear. It's opinionated in good ways.

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/mo Standard

### Monday.com

Visual project tracking:

- AI formula generation
- Workflow automation
- Custom dashboards
- Client-facing views

Good for visual thinkers and cross-functional teams.

**Cost:** $12/seat/month

### Asana

Enterprise-ready:

- AI task creation
- Smart status updates
- Goals and portfolios
- Workload management

The AI features help draft task descriptions and summarize project status.

**Cost:** Free basic, $11/seat/mo Premium

### ClickUp

Everything in one place:

- Docs, tasks, and goals
- AI writing assistant
- Time tracking
- Whiteboards

ClickUp tries to do everything. If you want one tool, this is comprehensive.

**Cost:** Free tier, $10/mo Unlimited

## Communication & Updates

### ChatGPT for PMs

Draft communications faster:

**Status update:**
> "Write a weekly status update for stakeholders. Project: website redesign. Progress: design approved, development 60% complete. Risks: API integration delayed 1 week. Next steps: user testing begins Monday."

**Meeting agenda:**
> "Create an agenda for a 30-minute sprint retrospective. Include time allocations."

**Stakeholder email:**
> "Draft an email explaining a 2-week delay to the executive sponsor. Tone: professional, transparent, solution-focused."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Plus

### Loom

Async video updates:

- Record quick updates
- Share without meetings
- Track who watched
- Comment threads

Replace status meetings with 3-minute videos.

**Cost:** Free tier, $15/mo Business

### Range

Team check-ins:

- Async standups
- Mood tracking
- Goal alignment
- Slack integration

Know how your team is doing without daily meetings.

**Cost:** $8/user/month

## Analysis & Planning

### Claude for PM Work

Deep analysis partner:

**Risk assessment:**
> "Review this project plan and identify the top 5 risks. For each, suggest a mitigation strategy and early warning indicator."

**Resource planning:**
> "Based on this scope document, estimate the team composition and timeline needed. Assume we have 2 senior developers and 1 junior developer available."

**Retrospective synthesis:**
> "Here are notes from our last 4 sprint retros. Identify patterns and suggest 3 systemic improvements."

Claude handles complex analysis better than ChatGPT.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo Pro

## What AI Can't Do

- Build relationships with stakeholders
- Navigate organizational politics
- Make judgment calls on trade-offs
- Motivate a struggling team member
- Know when to escalate vs. handle yourself

AI handles the administrative overhead. You handle the human stuff — which is actually the job.
