## Grant Writing

### Claude for Grants

Grant applications require nuance:

**Needs statements:**
> "Help me write a needs statement for a food bank serving 500 families monthly. Include local statistics, quotes from clients (I'll add real ones), and systemic context."

**Program descriptions:**
> "Write a program description for our after-school tutoring program. Include: activities, staffing model, outcomes we measure, and how we'll evaluate success."

**Budget narratives:**
> "Write a budget narrative explaining why we need $15,000 for staff training. Connect it to our program outcomes."

Claude handles the lengthy, detailed writing grants require.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Instrumentl

Grant research:

- Database of grant opportunities
- Deadline tracking
- Funder insights
- Application tracking

Find grants you actually qualify for.

**Cost:** $179/mo (steep, but valuable for grant-heavy orgs)

## Donor Communication

### ChatGPT for Outreach

Draft donor communications:

**Thank you letters:**
> "Write a thank you letter to a first-time donor of $100 to our animal shelter. Make it warm and specific about impact. Mention they'll receive quarterly updates."

**Appeal letters:**
> "Write year-end appeal for a literacy nonprofit. Focus on urgency without guilt. Include a specific story (I'll add details) and clear call to action."

**Major donor stewardship:**
> "Draft an update email for a major donor who gave $5,000 last year. Include: how their gift was used, impact achieved, and invitation to visit our site."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Mailchimp

Email marketing with nonprofit discount:

- 15% discount for registered nonprofits
- Automation sequences
- Donor segmentation
- Good free tier

Segment donors by giving level and history.

**Cost:** Free tier generous, discounted paid

## Nonprofit-Specific Tips

### Getting Discounts

Many AI tools offer nonprofit discounts:
- **Canva:** Free Pro for nonprofits
- **Mailchimp:** 15% off
- **Microsoft 365:** Donated or discounted
- **Google Workspace:** Free for nonprofits
- **Slack:** 85% off for nonprofits

Always ask. Many companies have programs they don't advertise.

### ChatGPT/Claude Ethics

Be transparent about AI use:
- Review all AI output
- Add authentic human touches
- Don't fabricate stories or quotes
- Funders increasingly ask about AI use

### Data Privacy

- Donor data is sensitive
- Check AI tool privacy policies
- Don't put donor PII into general AI tools
- Use nonprofit-specific platforms for donor info

## The Real Impact

AI won't replace the human connection that makes nonprofits work.

It will:
- Save staff time on routine writing
- Improve marketing quality with limited budgets
- Help small teams compete with larger organizations
- Free up energy for mission-critical work

Every hour saved on a newsletter is an hour available for the people you serve.
