# Notion AI: Complete Guide for Beginners

Notion added AI. If you use Notion, this matters.

Here's everything you need to know.

## What is Notion AI?

Notion AI is an AI writing assistant built directly into Notion. No switching apps. No copy-pasting. AI help right where you work.

**Key features:**
- Write and edit text
- Summarize documents
- Extract action items
- Translate content
- Brainstorm ideas
- Answer questions about your notes

## How to Access It

### Enable Notion AI

1. Open any Notion page
2. Press `Space` on an empty line, or
3. Highlight text and click "Ask AI"
4. Start using AI features

### Pricing

- **Free trial:** Limited queries to test
- **Add-on:** $10/month per member (on top of Notion subscription)

Worth it if you use Notion daily. Skip if you're a light user.

## Best Use Cases

### 1. First Drafts

Tell Notion AI what you need:
- "Write a project brief for..."
- "Draft an email about..."
- "Create an outline for..."

It generates a starting point. You edit from there.

### 2. Summarizing

Have a long document? Highlight it and ask for a summary. Great for:
- Meeting notes
- Research documents
- Long articles you've saved

### 3. Action Items

Highlight meeting notes. Ask: "Extract action items."

Notion AI pulls out tasks and to-dos. Works surprisingly well.

### 4. Improving Writing

Select text and choose:
- Make shorter
- Make longer
- Change tone
- Fix grammar
- Simplify language

### 5. Translation

Need content in another language? Highlight and translate. Supports major languages.

### 6. Brainstorming

"Give me 10 ideas for..."
"What are the pros and cons of..."
"How might I approach..."

Good for getting unstuck.

## What It Does Well

- **Context awareness:** Knows what's on your page
- **Integration:** No app switching
- **Speed:** Quick responses
- **Convenience:** Always available where you work

## What It Doesn't Do Well

- **Long-form writing:** Better for sections than full documents
- **Complex analysis:** Keep expectations realistic
- **Current information:** No internet access
- **Creative writing:** Functional, not artistic

## Notion AI vs. ChatGPT

| Feature | Notion AI | ChatGPT |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Integration | Built into Notion | Separate app |
| Context | Knows your pages | Manual context |
| Price | $10/month add-on | Free / $20 Plus |
| Capability | Writing-focused | General purpose |
| Best for | Notion users | Everyone |

**Verdict:** If you live in Notion, get Notion AI. If you don't, ChatGPT is more versatile.

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific

Bad: "Write something about marketing"
Good: "Write 3 tweet ideas promoting our new product launch to small business owners"

### 2. Give Context

Reference other content on your page. Notion AI can read it.

### 3. Iterate

First output not right? Ask for adjustments. "Make it more casual" or "Focus more on benefits."

### 4. Use Templates

Create pages with AI prompt templates for repeated tasks.

## Practical Workflows

### Meeting Notes Workflow

1. Take rough notes during meeting
2. After: "Clean up these notes"
3. Then: "Extract action items"
4. Finally: "Summarize key decisions"

### Content Creation Workflow

1. "Create an outline for [topic]"
2. Review and adjust outline
3. "Write section 1 based on this outline"
4. Edit and refine
5. Repeat for each section

### Email Workflow

1. "Draft a professional email about [situation]"
2. Edit for your voice
3. "Make it shorter" if needed
4. Copy to email client

## Should You Get It?

**Yes if:**
- You use Notion daily
- You write a lot in Notion
- $10/month fits your budget
- You want AI without leaving your workspace

**No if:**
- You rarely use Notion
- You already have ChatGPT Plus
- You need advanced AI capabilities

## Getting Started

1. Open Notion
2. Create a new page
3. Press Space on empty line
4. Try: "Write 5 ideas for improving my morning routine"
5. See what you think

Give it a real task from your actual work. That's the best test.
