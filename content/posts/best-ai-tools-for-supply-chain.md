## Analysis and Problem-Solving

### ChatGPT for Daily Operations

Quick analysis and communication:

**Vendor communication:**
> "Draft an email to a supplier about a late shipment. We need expedited delivery. Be firm but maintain the relationship."

**Root cause analysis:**
> "We had a stockout on [product]. Here's what we know: [data]. Help me structure a root cause analysis."

**Presentation prep:**
> "I'm presenting supply chain KPIs to leadership. Help me structure the narrative: [paste data]. Focus on improvements and remaining challenges."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Complex Analysis

Multi-variable problems:

> "Analyze this inventory data: [paste]. We're seeing high carrying costs but also stockouts. What's the optimal inventory policy? Consider seasonal demand."

> "Compare these three logistics providers: [details]. Create a decision matrix including cost, reliability, coverage, and flexibility."

Claude handles longer, more detailed analysis.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## The Enterprise Reality

Most supply chain AI lives in enterprise systems:

- **Blue Yonder** for demand planning
- **Kinaxis** for supply planning
- **Manhattan** for WMS
- **SAP/Oracle** integrated AI

ChatGPT/Claude are supplementary tools for analysis and communication, not replacements for these systems.
