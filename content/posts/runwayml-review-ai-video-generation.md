# Runway ML Review: Is AI Video Generation Actually Usable Yet?

AI image generation changed everything. AI video is supposed to be next.

I spent a month with Runway ML, one of the leading AI video tools, trying to use it for actual video projects. Here's the honest review.

## What Runway Actually Offers

Runway is less "AI video creator" and more "AI video toolkit." It has:

**Generation features:**
- Gen-2: Text/image to video
- Frame interpolation
- Video extension

**Editing features:**
- Background removal (Green Screen)
- Inpainting (remove objects)
- Super Slow Motion
- Style transfer

**Audio features:**
- Text to speech
- Audio cleanup

The toolkit approach is smart. Some features are very useful; others are tech demos.

## The Features Worth Using

### Green Screen (Background Removal)

This is genuinely good. Upload video with a subject, Runway removes the background.

**Quality:** 8/10 - Handles most footage well. Struggles with fine hair and complex edges, like all tools.

**Speed:** Fast enough for practical use

**Value:** Previously required expensive software or rotoscoping. Now one click.

**Verdict:** Worth the subscription just for this if you do video editing.

### Inpainting (Object Removal)

Remove unwanted objects from video frames. Draw a mask, AI fills it in.

**Quality:** 7/10 - Good for simple removals. Complex scenes or moving objects get messy.

**Best for:** Removing logos, signs, small distracting elements

**Verdict:** Useful but not magic. Simple removals work; complex ones need traditional methods.

### Super Slow Motion

Turn regular footage into slow motion without the artifacts.

**Quality:** 7/10 - Impressive for basic footage. Fast motion or complex scenes create weird artifacts.

**Best for:** Simple slow motion needs without proper equipment

**Verdict:** Nice to have. Not a replacement for actual high-frame-rate footage.

## The Features That Aren't Ready

### Gen-2 (Text/Image to Video)

This is what everyone wants: type description, get video.

**Reality check:** The output is 4-16 second clips that look... dreamy? Surreal? Definitely not realistic.

**What it's good for:**
- Abstract backgrounds
- Artistic/experimental content
- Music video aesthetics
- Social media visual effects

**What it's NOT good for:**
- Realistic footage
- Product videos
- Any business content requiring normal-looking video
- Anything requiring specific actions or timing

**My test:** "A person walking through a modern office, talking on phone, looking professional."

**Result:** Something that vaguely resembled the description but looked AI-generated. Movements were uncanny. Not usable for any professional purpose.

**Verdict:** Impressive tech demo. Not production-ready for realistic content.

### Video Extension

Extend video clips beyond their original length. AI generates new frames.

**Quality:** 5/10 - Sometimes works, often produces weird results. Movements become unnatural.

**Verdict:** Unreliable. Maybe useful for specific artistic effects.

## The Pricing Reality

**Free:** 125 credits to start (not enough to really evaluate)

**Standard:** $12/month - 625 credits
**Pro:** $28/month - 2250 credits
**Unlimited:** $76/month - Unlimited generation

**Credit math:** One Gen-2 generation (5 seconds) costs about 50 credits. So Standard gets you ~12 generations per month.

That's not much if you're experimenting.

**My recommendation:** If you just need Green Screen and basic editing features, Standard is fine. If you want to seriously use Gen-2, you need Pro or higher.

## Who Should Use Runway

### Yes, Use Runway If:

- You edit video and need background removal
- You do music videos or abstract visual content
- You want to experiment with AI video aesthetics
- You need specific AI editing tools (inpainting, etc.)

### Not Worth It If:

- You want AI to create videos for you from scratch
- You need realistic footage
- You're making standard business/marketing videos
- You're expecting results like AI image generation (not there yet)

## The Comparison to AI Images

AI image generation (Midjourney, DALL-E) can create professional-quality images that pass as photographs.

AI video generation in 2026 cannot create professional-quality video that passes as real footage.

The gap is significant. Don't expect to type a prompt and get usable video.

## What I Actually Use It For

After a month, here's my Runway workflow:

**Green Screen:** Regularly. Saves hours on background removal.

**Inpainting:** Occasionally. When it works, it's great.

**Gen-2:** Rarely. Only for abstract backgrounds or experimental content.

The editing tools justify the subscription. The generation features are bonuses for specific creative needs.

## The Future vs. Present

Is AI video generation the future? Almost certainly.

Is it ready now? For editing tools, yes. For generation, not for mainstream use.

In 2-3 years, this review will probably be obsolete. Gen-3 or Gen-4 might be the breakthrough.

For now, Runway is:
- Great AI video editing toolkit
- Interesting experimental generation tool
- Not yet a video creation solution

## Bottom Line

**Rating: 7/10** - Excellent for AI-powered editing features, especially background removal. Generation features are impressive tech but not yet practical for most real work.

**Verdict:** Worth it for video editors who want AI tools. Not worth it if you expect AI to create videos for you.

**Who should subscribe:** Video editors who do background removal regularly. Experimental content creators. People who want to play with cutting-edge AI.

**Who should wait:** Anyone expecting ChatGPT-for-video. We're not there yet.
