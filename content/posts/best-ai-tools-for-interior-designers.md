## Visualization

### Midjourney

Concept visualization:

- Show clients ideas before sourcing
- Explore style directions
- Quick mood visualization
- Present multiple concepts

"Imagine your living room could look like this."

**Example prompts:**
> "Modern Scandinavian living room with warm wood tones, minimalist furniture, large windows, soft neutral palette, interior photography"

> "Art deco inspired dining room, emerald green velvet chairs, brass accents, geometric patterns, luxury interior"

**Cost:** $10-30/mo

### REimagine Home

AI room transformation:

- Upload existing room photo
- Select new style
- Instant visualization
- Multiple design options

Show clients possibilities without sourcing.

**Cost:** Free tier / paid options

### Foyr Neo

Professional 3D rendering:

- Floor plan to 3D
- Walkthrough videos
- Furniture placement
- Client presentation

More professional output than AI generators.

**Cost:** $49/mo

## Client Communication

### ChatGPT for Design Proposals

Professional communication:

**Design concept descriptions:**
> "Write a design concept description for a transitional family room that blends traditional comfort with modern simplicity. Include color palette, textures, and key furniture pieces."

**Client emails:**
> "Draft an email presenting three design direction options to a client. They mentioned loving both contemporary and traditional styles."

**Scope and pricing:**
> "Help me write a scope of services document for a full-home interior design project including design development, sourcing, and project management."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## AI Limitations for Designers

### What AI Does Well
- Quick concept visualization
- Style exploration
- Communication drafting
- Mood board creation
- Basic floor planning

### What AI Can't Replace
- Understanding client lifestyle
- Spatial problem-solving
- Material expertise
- Vendor relationships
- Project management
- Installation oversight

AI generates images. You create livable spaces.

---

## The Visualization Question

Some clients now expect AI visualizations. Consider:

**Benefits:**
- Faster concept approval
- Multiple direction exploration
- Cost-effective presentations
- Client excitement

**Risks:**
- Unrealistic expectations
- "Why can't it look exactly like that?"
- Skipping proper planning
- Over-reliance on AI aesthetics

Use AI visualization as a starting point, not a promise.
