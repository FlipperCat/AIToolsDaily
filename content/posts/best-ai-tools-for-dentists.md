## Patient Communication

### ChatGPT for Patient Messages

Draft clear, reassuring communication:

**Treatment explanations:**
> "Explain why a patient needs a crown in simple terms. They're nervous about dental work. Be reassuring but honest."

**Post-procedure instructions:**
> "Write aftercare instructions for a patient who just had a tooth extraction. Include what to do, what to avoid, and warning signs."

**Insurance explanations:**
> "A patient doesn't understand why their insurance only covers 50% of their crown. Help me explain dental insurance limitations simply."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Weave

All-in-one communication platform:

- Two-way texting
- Automated reminders
- Phone system with caller ID
- Review requests
- Payment collection

Know who's calling before you answer.

**Cost:** Starting ~$400/mo

### RevenueWell

Patient engagement:

- Automated recall
- Treatment follow-up
- Review generation
- Patient education

Keep chairs filled.

**Cost:** Custom pricing

## Dental-Specific Tips

### Reducing No-Shows

Automated reminders:
- 1 week before: Confirmation request
- 2 days before: Reminder
- Day of: Final reminder

AI-powered systems learn optimal timing.

### Patient Education

Use ChatGPT to create:
- Simple procedure explanations
- Aftercare instructions
- FAQ documents
- Treatment option comparisons

Better informed patients = better compliance.

### Review Management

When patients leave reviews:
> "Draft a response to a positive review thanking them for their kind words about [specific thing they mentioned]."

> "A patient complained about wait times. Draft a response acknowledging their feedback and explaining our scheduling improvements."
