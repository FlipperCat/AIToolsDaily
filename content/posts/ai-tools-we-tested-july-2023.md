# 5 AI Tools We Tested This Month (July 2023)

AI tools are launching faster than ever. Here's what caught our attention this month.

## 1. Claude by Anthropic

**What it is:** A ChatGPT competitor focused on safety and helpfulness.

**Our take:** Claude impressed us with longer context windows and thoughtful responses. It handles nuanced questions well and admits when it's uncertain. For certain tasks, it outperforms ChatGPT.

**Verdict:** Worth trying, especially for complex writing tasks.

## 2. Runway Gen-2

**What it is:** Text-to-video generation.

**Our take:** The future is here, sort of. Gen-2 creates short video clips from text prompts. Quality varies wildly, but when it works, it's magical. Best for creative exploration, not production work yet.

**Verdict:** Exciting technology, but not ready for professional use.

## 3. Gamma

**What it is:** AI-powered presentation maker.

**Our take:** Type your content, get a polished presentation. Gamma genuinely saves hours on deck creation. The designs are modern, and the AI suggestions are surprisingly good.

**Verdict:** A real time-saver for frequent presenters.

## 4. Descript

**What it is:** Audio and video editing with AI transcription.

**Our take:** Edit audio by editing text. Remove filler words automatically. This tool changed how we produce podcasts and videos. The learning curve is minimal for basic use.

**Verdict:** Highly recommended for content creators.

## 5. Notion AI

**What it is:** AI features built into Notion.

**Our take:** Having AI directly in your notes is convenient. Summarize meeting notes, brainstorm ideas, improve writing - all without leaving Notion. Not revolutionary, but genuinely useful.

**Verdict:** Worth the $10/month if you already use Notion.

## The Trend

AI is moving from standalone tools to embedded features. The best tools this month aren't just doing AI - they're integrating AI into workflows we already use.
