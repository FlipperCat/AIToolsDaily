# GPT-4 Turbo: What's New and Is It Worth It?

OpenAI quietly rolled out GPT-4 Turbo. After a month of testing, here's what matters.

## The Big Changes

### 1. Fresher Knowledge
Knowledge cutoff moved from September 2021 to April 2024. Finally, you can ask about recent events without the model pretending it's still 2021.

### 2. Longer Context
From 8K to 128K tokens. That's roughly 100,000 words. Upload an entire book and ask questions about it.

### 3. Better Vision
Image understanding is now built into GPT-4 Turbo. Describe a photo, analyze a chart, or understand a screenshot.

### 4. Faster Responses
Noticeably snappier. Complex requests that took 30 seconds now complete in 15.

### 5. Lower Prices (API)
For developers: 3x cheaper than GPT-4. This matters for apps and businesses.

## Real-World Testing

We put GPT-4 Turbo through our standard tests:

**Writing quality:** Same excellence as GPT-4. No degradation noticed.

**Code generation:** Feels slightly better. More context means better understanding of large codebases.

**Reasoning:** Complex chains of logic still work well. Maybe marginally better.

**Speed:** Definitely faster. This makes a real difference in daily use.

## The Catch

With 128K context, it's tempting to dump everything in. But:
- More context = slower responses (even with Turbo)
- The model can still miss details buried in long text
- Quality prompts matter more than quantity

Use the context window wisely, not wastefully.

## Compared to Claude

| Feature | GPT-4 Turbo | Claude 2 |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Context window | 128K | 100K |
| Knowledge cutoff | April 2024 | Early 2023 |
| Speed | Fast | Fast |
| Writing style | Comprehensive | Nuanced |
| Coding | Excellent | Very good |
| Price (API) | $0.01/1K tokens | $0.008/1K tokens |

Both are excellent. GPT-4 Turbo has fresher knowledge. Claude has a different style some prefer.

## Should You Upgrade?

**If you have ChatGPT Plus:** You already have it. Enjoy.

**If you're on free ChatGPT:** The gap between GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 Turbo is substantial. Worth $20/month if you use AI seriously.

**If you're a developer:** The price drop makes GPT-4-level intelligence more affordable for apps.

## The Bottom Line

GPT-4 Turbo is GPT-4 made better, faster, and cheaper. Not revolutionary, but a meaningful upgrade.

The AI assistant wars continue. We all benefit.
