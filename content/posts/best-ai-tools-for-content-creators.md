## Ideation and Scripts

### ChatGPT for Content Ideas

Generate endless ideas:

**Video topics:**
> "I run a cooking channel for busy professionals. Give me 20 video ideas for 'meals under 15 minutes' that haven't been overdone on YouTube."

**Script outlines:**
> "Create a script outline for a 10-minute video about [topic]. Include a hook, main points, and a call to action. My style is casual and slightly sarcastic."

**Titles and hooks:**
> "Give me 10 different hooks for a video about [topic]. Mix curiosity gaps, controversy, and direct benefit approaches."

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

### Claude for Long-Form

When scripts need depth:

- Full video scripts with timestamps
- Multi-part series planning
- Research-heavy content
- Nuanced topics

Claude handles longer, more complex writing better.

**Cost:** Free / $20/mo

## Video Editing

### Descript

Edit video by editing text:

- Transcription-based editing
- Remove filler words automatically
- Overdub for fixing mistakes
- Screen recording included

Revolutionary for talking-head content.

**Cost:** Free tier / $12/mo Creator

### CapCut

Quick, trendy edits:

- Auto-captions
- Trending effects
- Mobile-first editing
- TikTok integration

The go-to for short-form content.

**Cost:** Free (with watermark options)

### Opus Clip

Turn long videos into shorts:

- AI finds best moments
- Auto-generates clips
- Adds captions
- Virality score

One long video becomes 10 shorts.

**Cost:** Free tier / $19/mo

## Graphics and Thumbnails

### Canva

Everything visual:

- Thumbnail templates
- Social media graphics
- Video intros
- Brand kit management
- AI-powered design suggestions

The Swiss Army knife for creators.

**Cost:** Free / $13/mo Pro

### Photoshop (Generative Fill)

Advanced thumbnail editing:

- Extend backgrounds
- Remove/add elements
- Professional quality

For when Canva isn't enough.

**Cost:** $23/mo (Adobe subscription)

## Recording

### Riverside

Studio-quality recording:

- Local recording (high quality)
- Separate audio/video tracks
- AI transcription
- Remote guest recording

Better than Zoom for content.

**Cost:** Free tier / $15/mo

### Descript (Screen Recording)

Screen content:

- Screen + camera recording
- Automatic transcription
- Direct editing
- Easy export

All-in-one for tutorial content.

## YouTube Optimization

### VidIQ

YouTube growth tools:

- Keyword research
- Title/tag optimization
- Competitor analysis
- Trend alerts
- AI title suggestions

Understand what works on YouTube.

**Cost:** Free tier / $10/mo Boost

### TubeBuddy

Alternative to VidIQ:

- Similar features
- A/B testing for thumbnails
- Bulk processing tools
- Comment management

Both are solid choices.

**Cost:** Free tier / $9/mo

## Repurposing

### Pictory

Turn content into videos:

- Blog posts to videos
- Script to video
- Auto-summarize long content
- Stock footage included

Great for repurposing written content.

**Cost:** Starting $19/mo

### Repurpose.io

Multi-platform distribution:

- Auto-post to all platforms
- Format conversion
- Scheduling
- Workflow automation

Create once, publish everywhere.

**Cost:** Starting $25/mo

## The Content Creator's Reality

AI won't make you successful. Good content will.

But AI can help you:
- Create more consistently
- Repurpose efficiently
- Optimize for discovery
- Reduce editing time

The creators winning aren't the ones with the fanciest tools. They're the ones who show up consistently with content people want.

AI just makes showing up easier.
