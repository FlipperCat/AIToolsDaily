Money-Saving Tips

When to Go Annual

  • Used monthly for 2+ months
  • Core to your workflow
  • Discount is 20%+ (usually 30-50%)

Stay Monthly If

  • Still testing the tool
  • AI tools evolving fast
  • Your needs might change

Watch Out For

  • "Lifetime deals" - often scams
  • Auto-renewal at full price
  • Feature limits on free tiers
