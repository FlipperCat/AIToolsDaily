## 3. Maker Story

### The Story

**Why I Built This:**

Last year, I spent weeks researching AI tools for my workflow. Every "best AI tools" article I found was:
- Obviously written by AI with no real testing
- Just a list of affiliate links with generic descriptions
- Outdated within months of publishing

I'd try a tool based on a recommendation, realize it didn't fit my needs, and start the research process again. Waste of time.

**The Solution:**

I started documenting my own testing. Which tools actually worked? Which were overhyped? What were the real pros and cons?

That documentation became AI Tools Daily.

**What Makes Us Different:**

1. **Honest testing** - I actually use these tools before writing about them
2. **Regular updates** - AI moves fast; content stays current
3. **Comparison focus** - Help people choose between options
4. **Free tiers highlighted** - Not everyone has $50/month for AI tools
5. **No manipulation** - Clear, honest recommendations

**The Goal:**

Save people the hours I wasted researching AI tools. Help them find the right tool for their specific needs in minutes, not days.

287 articles later, that's what we've built.
